## Group Categories

### Civic and State Institutions
Groups that openly govern, protect, educate, administer, or sustain the sanctuary.

### Religious and Scholarly Orders
Groups centered on sacred practice, preservation of knowledge, ritual, education, or moral authority.

### Trade and Economic Bodies
Groups that move goods, manage logistics, and connect the Hollow Crown to internal and external supply structures.

### Covert and Restricted Networks
Groups whose existence, methods, or full purpose are not public knowledge.

### Criminal, Dissident, and Adversarial Formations

## Civic and State Institutions

### The Draconic Council

The supreme governing authority of Drakenvale.

**Type:** Governing triumvirate  
**Leadership:** Eryndor the Radiant, Zarkeros the Inferno, Varethyn of the Amethyst Gaze
**Status:** Canonical, active, public

The Council is the final political authority of the sanctuary. It governs by consensus, with unanimous approval required for major decisions of enduring legal, military, theological, or strategic significance. It interprets and enforces the Codex of Remembrance, controls disclosure of the Hollow Crown’s existence, and oversees the balance of power among the realm’s major institutions.

The Council is not a broad senate or noble assembly. It is a concentrated draconic authority whose stability depends on the continuing willingness of three very different ancient dragons to rule together.

---

### Drakari Stewards

The civic-administrative class most closely associated with day-to-day governance below the Council level.

**Type:** Civic service body / administrative class  
**Leadership:** Distributed under Council authority  
**Status:** Canonical, active, public

Drakari Stewards manage records, petitions, civic process, local enforcement support, legal administration, logistics coordination, and a large share of public-facing governance. They are not the only officials in the realm, but they are its most visible non-draconic administrative spine.

They hold high standing in Drakenvale’s social order and are often the practical interface between the population and the higher institutions of the sanctuary.

---

### The Dragon Guard

Drakenvale’s primary external defense force.

**Type:** Military  
**Leadership:** Zarkeros the Inferno
**Status:** Canonical, active, public

The Dragon Guard is responsible for external defense, border security, aerial response, major threat mobilization, and military deterrence. Its composition includes drakari warriors, kobold engineers, mortal mages, dragons of varying alignments, mounted and aerial assets, and reserve support forces.

The Guard’s authority is strongest at the edges of the Hollow Crown, along dangerous corridors, and in periods of overt crisis. In ceremonial life, it also serves as a visible symbol of discipline and strength.

---

### The Wardens

Drakenvale’s internal security, sacred-site, and anomaly-response force.

**Type:** Internal security / sacred protection force  
**Leadership:** Eryndor the Radiant in peacetime; temporarily militarized under Zarkeros the Inferno in crisis
**Status:** Canonical, active, public

The Wardens protect the Platinum Heart, the Amethyst Vault, the Heartstone chamber, and the sealed Temple of Mordrax. They investigate magical anomalies, internal disturbances, corruption threats, cult activity, and breaches involving sacred or strategically sensitive sites.

They are distinct from the Dragon Guard, though their jurisdictions overlap during emergencies. This dual structure is one of the sanctuary’s persistent institutional tensions.

---

### Silver Wing Envoys

The formal diplomatic body of Drakenvale.

**Type:** Diplomatic corps  
**Leadership:** Council-aligned, administered below Council level  
**Status:** Canonical, active, public

The Silver Wing Envoys manage carefully controlled external relations, diplomatic contact, alliance vetting, and secrecy-conscious negotiation. They are the public face of Drakenvale’s outward contact when such contact is permitted at all.

They do not operate with open transparency. Their work is constrained by the sanctuary’s secrecy, by the need to control information leakage, and by the fact that every diplomatic action risks exposing something that cannot safely be known too widely.

---

### Circle of Artisans

The broad civic-cultural body responsible for infrastructure craft, commissioned work, and artistic maintenance.

**Type:** Civic craft and culture body  
**Leadership:** Distributed, council-recognized  
**Status:** Canonical, active, public

The Circle of Artisans supports Drakenvale’s built environment, ceremonial work, public design, repair culture, and commissioned artistic output. It overlaps with guild labor, but carries a broader civic identity tied to the stewardship of the sanctuary’s beauty, function, and symbolic coherence.

---

### Sapphire Choir

The organized keeper of oral tradition, performance, literary memory, and ceremonial cultural expression.

**Type:** Cultural institution  
**Leadership:** Civil-cultural leadership, council-recognized  
**Status:** Canonical, active, public

The Sapphire Choir maintains oral history, formal performance, ritual music, literary tradition, and festival expression. It is not politically neutral in the abstract — culture is power in Drakenvale — but it is not a covert or adversarial body. It helps shape how the sanctuary remembers itself.

## Religious and Scholarly Orders

### Platinum Acolytes

The spiritual backbone of Drakenvale’s public sacred life.

**Type:** Religious order  
**Leadership:** Broadly aligned with Eryndor the Radiant
**Status:** Canonical, active, public

The Platinum Acolytes maintain sacred rites, public observances, atonement practice, moral instruction, and the routine sacred life of the sanctuary. They tend the Platinum Heart, oversee much of the sanctuary’s visible Solveris-aligned religious life, and help preserve the moral authority of the Codex of Remembrance.

Their influence is wide, even where it is not coercive. In practice, they are one of the strongest cultural forces in the realm.

---

### Hall of Scales

The principal broad institution of higher learning in Drakenvale.

**Type:** Academy / university  
**Leadership:** Ardrynn the Measured, ancient dragon scholar, council-recognized
**Status:** Canonical, active, public

The Hall of Scales provides broad higher education across scholarship, civic learning, martial theory, magical foundations, trade, history, and formal intellectual development. It is the main university-level institution of the sanctuary.

It is not the same as the Arcane Conservatory. The Hall of Scales is broad, foundational, and civic-facing; the Conservatory is elite, specialized, and reserved for more advanced arcane refinement.

---

### Arcane Conservatory

The elite center of advanced magical study and dangerous arcane refinement.

**Type:** Advanced magical institution  
**Leadership:** Varethyn of the Amethyst Gaze
**Status:** Canonical, active, public

The Arcane Conservatory is where exceptional magical talent is cultivated beyond the level of general education. It is concerned with refined arcane mastery, difficult theory, dangerous magical practice, elite instruction, and the preservation and controlled handling of powerful knowledge.

Its role is not mass education. It is the sanctuary’s advanced magical threshold.

---

### The Vigil

The concealed surviving remnant of the Platinum Accord, publicly known only as the monastic order of Platinum Oath.

**Type:** Restricted monastic watch order / Accord remnant  
**Leadership:** Serevane
**Status:** Canonical, active, partially concealed

Publicly, the Vigil appears as the disciplined monastic order of Platinum Oath Monastery, associated with Rift vigilance, hard devotional practice, and dangerous perimeter work. Internally, it preserves the living remnant of the Platinum Accord.

Its duties include monitoring Rift instability, maintaining continuity with the Platinum Warden’s legacy, safeguarding restricted knowledge, and preparing selected candidates for extreme-edge operations. Its true identity is not common public knowledge.

## Trade and Economic Bodies

### Silver Scale Trading Company

Drakenvale’s primary high-level trade interface and the major mover of bulk goods, import-export flow, and external commercial reach.

**Type:** Trade body / logistics power  
**Leadership:** Multi-layered, with deep Council ties  
**Status:** Canonical, active, semi-public

The Silver Scale Trading Company handles a large share of Drakenvale’s major trade movement, especially in bulk supply, external commercial channels, long-route logistics, and controlled exchange beyond the Hollow Crown. It is not the same thing as ordinary local merchant life. It moves goods between systems, regions, and markets at scale.

The SSTC is not itself an intelligence service, but its routes, warehouses, contracts, and external presence make it one of the most important channels through which covert observation and information movement can occur.

Operationally, SSTC logistics often intersect with sanctuary intelligence. The **Amethyst Veil** leverages SSTC visibility and route reach for pattern awareness, while **Sapphire Sentinels** field actions may use SSTC movement lanes as cover, surveillance surface, or containment corridors when public deployment would be counterproductive.

Within Drakenvale, ordinary buying and selling still occurs through local merchants, workshops, markets, and regional settlements. The SSTC operates above that layer, controlling the larger arteries of movement and value.

It is both economically useful and politically sensitive because trade is one of the main ways secrecy, leverage, and outside influence can enter the sanctuary.

---

### Infernal Forge Guild

The principal crafted-power and strategic manufacturing body of the realm.

**Type:** Craft guild / industrial body  
**Leadership:** Operates under the strategic influence of Zarkeros the Inferno
**Status:** Canonical, active, public

The Infernal Forge Guild produces weapons, armor, tools, constructs, enchanted implements, and a range of specialized crafted goods necessary for both daily life and defense. It is one of the sanctuary’s most important production bodies and a major consumer of ore, heat, magical materials, and skilled labor.

Its work ties the Volcanic Highlands directly to the practical strength of the rest of the realm.

## Covert and Restricted Networks

### The Amethyst Veil

Varethyn’s covert intelligence architecture.

**Type:** Covert intelligence network  
**Leadership:** Varethyn of the Amethyst Gaze
**Status:** Canonical, active, secret

The Amethyst Veil is the hidden intelligence network through which Varethyn gathers information, shapes perception, tracks threat patterns, and maintains awareness beyond the visible reach of ordinary governance. It is not a public institution.

It uses multiple channels of reach, including arcane observation, discreet intermediaries, and carefully leveraged access through broader systems such as long-distance trade and external contact. The Silver Scale Trading Company is one of the most important practical channels through which this reach can extend, even though the Company itself is not simply reducible to the Veil.

It operates through layered methods rather than a single obvious structure, including arcane observation, informant networks, controlled watchers, and trade-linked channels. Much of its effectiveness comes from the fact that most people do not know where it begins or ends.

---

### Sapphire Sentinels

A covert threat-monitoring and containment body associated with sanctuary secrecy and severe internal risks.

**Type:** Covert field network / restricted security body  
**Leadership:** Not publicly transparent; tied to Varethyn’s intelligence reach  
**Status:** Canonical, active, semi-concealed

The Sapphire Sentinels monitor threats to the sanctuary that cannot be handled through ordinary public means: dark-quadrant movement, cult activity, information leakage, severe external threats, and certain forms of covert instability. They are one of the main field-facing expressions of the sanctuary’s hidden intelligence posture.

The cleanest way to understand them is as an operational arm within the broader shadow architecture that includes the Amethyst Veil.

In practical terms: **Amethyst Veil = strategic intelligence architecture**, **Sapphire Sentinels = field-facing covert response**, and **SSTC = major movement substrate sometimes leveraged by both**.

## Criminal, Dissident, and Adversarial Formations

### Mordrax Cults

The most enduring internal-adversarial religious threat in Drakenvale.

**Type:** Cult formations / covert ideological threat  
**Leadership:** Fragmented, cell-based, unstable  
**Status:** Canonical, active, suppressed

Mordrax-aligned cult activity persists in hidden form despite the sanctuary’s law, vigilance, and spiritual structure. These formations are not legitimate religious expression of any draconic lineage; they are adversarial and destabilizing movements tied to secrecy breach, corruption, ambition, and the danger of the Temple below the Shadowed Hollows.

They are most visible at the margins, especially where fear, resentment, resentment of Solveris-aligned centrality, or dark-quadrant proximity weaken ordinary social order.

---

### Greymantle’s Underworld Environment

A tolerated edge-zone of harder actors, illicit exchange, and noncompliant power.

**Type:** Criminal / outlaw environment rather than a single unified organization  
**Leadership:** Fragmented; shaped by local force, reputation, and hidden influence  
**Status:** Canonical, active, tolerated at the edge

Greymantle is not a single gang, syndicate, or cartel. It is an edge-settlement environment where smugglers, deserters, exiles, cult-adjacent actors, morally gray traders, and those avoiding full civic oversight can coexist under harsher local rules than the rest of the sanctuary would normally tolerate.

Elsewhere in Drakenvale, overt criminal structures are usually driven underground or crushed quickly. At the margins, especially near the Hollows and Rift corridors, survival of such formations becomes more possible.

---

### Dissident Hardliners and Sanctuary Malcontents

Informal dissident tendencies rather than a single named bloc.

**Type:** Political / ideological dissident tendency  
**Leadership:** Diffuse  
**Status:** Canonical, latent, variable

Not all threats to Drakenvale come from explicit cults or criminals. Some arise from those who believe the sanctuary has drifted too far from its founding compromise, too far into Solveris-aligned moral dominance, too far into softness, or too far into secrecy and control. These pressures do not always become organized movements, but they are part of the realm’s political ecology.

This category includes lingering Discordant War grievance, anti-outsider paranoia, anti-Council resentment, and other ideological tensions that may become more coherent under stress.

## Sovereign-Ally Reference Entries

The following groups are not Drakenvale institutions. They are sovereign-equal Feywood orders documented here as reference entries so realm-side queries can find them. Authoritative content for each lives in the Feywood files.

### Heartwarden

The elite sacred-guardian order of the Heartwood and the Silent Grove.

**Type:** Elven sacred-protection martial order (Feywood-internal)
**Status:** Reference pointer; canon authority lies with Feywood files

The Heartwarden order is sworn directly to the Heartwood and is responsible for guarding the Heartwood’s inner sanctums, the Silent Grove, and the ceremonies bound to those sites. Heartwardens are not a household order of any specific house; they are an order of the Feywood, taking their oaths at the Heartwood itself. Membership cuts across House lines and includes elves from any noble or common origin who have proven the discipline and devotion required. **Lethariel Heartwood** holds Heartwarden Captaincy in emeritus standing (see `npcs.md`); the order’s most consequential historical action in Drakenvale’s record was Lethariel’s leadership of the Feywood contingent during the Discordant War, fulfilling the Oath of the Fallen’s mutual-aid clause.

### Greenshield

The Feywood’s primary defensive military order.

**Type:** Elven defensive military order (Feywood-internal)
**Status:** Reference pointer; canon authority lies with Feywood files

The Greenshield is the Feywood’s main practical defense — the order responsible for settlement defense, daily security, patrols throughout the Feywood interior, and the elven side of the realm-to-realm border. Most Feywood elves serving in arms serve in the Greenshield. They are the most likely Feywood institution Drakenvale residents will encounter at the Feywood Border or at Vaelmere during ordinary diplomatic and trade contact. They are not part of Drakenvale’s defense structure; coordination between Greenshield and the Dragon Guard or Wardens is conducted at the Council level under the Oath of the Fallen.

### Ranger

A specialized branch of the Greenshield for outer-range fieldwork.

**Type:** Elven scout and outer-range order, organizationally part of the Greenshield (Feywood-internal)
**Status:** Reference pointer; canon authority lies with Feywood files

Rangers are Greenshield personnel selected and trained for fieldwork at the Feywood’s outer ranges and along the realm-to-realm threshold — long-range patrol, scouting, threshold-watch, escort of sanctioned visitors through the Feywood’s liminal geometry, and contact with realm-side forces. They are not a separate order from the Greenshield; they are the Greenshield’s specialist arm for work that cannot be conducted from settled patrol. Realm visitors traveling through the Feywood with elven escort are most often guided by Rangers.

For full doctrine, structure, and present-day operations of all three orders, see the Feywood `groups` files.

## Group Summary

Drakenvale is held together not by one institution, but by an interlocking structure of councils, orders, guilds, schools, diplomats, covert watchers, edge-groups, and dangerous remnants. Some preserve the sanctuary. Some exploit it. Some do both at once depending on perspective. Together, they form the organized power landscape of the Hollow Crown.
