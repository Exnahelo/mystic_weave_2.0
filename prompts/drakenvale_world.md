# Drakenvale

> A hidden sanctuary governed by the Ptarian Code — justice, balance, and wisdom — where metallic, chromatic, and gem dragons coexist alongside dragonborn, kobolds, and mortals.

## Overview

Drakenvale is a sanctuary carved into a secluded mountain valley, its precise location known only to trusted allies. The valley's weather mirrors the emotional state of its dragons — calm mists in times of peace, powerful storms in times of conflict. The land is rich in platinum, gemstones, and magical flora, all saturated with draconic energy.

The governing philosophy is the **Ptarian Code**: justice tempered by mercy, power tempered by restraint, harmony above individual ambition.

## Governance

### The Draconic Council

Three ancient dragons rule by consensus. Major decisions require unanimous approval.

| Dragon | Type | Alignment | Domain |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[Eryndor the Radiant]] | Gold Dragon | Lawful Good | Justice, diplomacy, moral guidance |
| [[Zarkeros the Inferno]] | Red Dragon | Lawful Evil | Strength, defense, resource management |
| [[Varethyn of the Amethyst Gaze]] | Amethyst Dragon | Neutral | Wisdom, arcane knowledge, balance |

When the Council cannot reach consensus, disputes are resolved by invoking the **Radiant Crystal** — a divine artifact housed at the center of the Draconic Hall, forged from the combined magic of the founding dragons. It provides impartial guidance through Bahamut's influence and also serves as a general instrument of guidance for unresolved community disputes.

### The Ptarian Code

- **Justice and Mercy** — fairness, proportionality, opportunity for redemption
- **Balance and Unity** — harmony among all alignments
- **Responsibility and Restraint** — power wielded for the greater good
- **Sanctity of Sacred Sites** — the Platinum Heart, Amethyst Vault, and Temple to Tiamat are inviolable
- **Secrecy of the Sanctuary** — location disclosure requires unanimous Council approval

Legal violations are addressed through reparations, exile, or atonement rituals. Execution exists as a penalty but requires unanimous Council approval and is reserved for the gravest offenses (e.g., attempting to unseal the Temple to Tiamat).

The Code is a living document. Amendments are proposed and debated at the **Draconic Conclave**.

### Advisory Roles

- **Dragonborn Stewards** — mediators and enforcers of Council rulings; can hold law enforcement roles as Acolytes of Justice
- **Mortal Representatives** — advise the Council but hold no vote; may petition for leniency in cases beyond their understanding
- **Kobolds** — essential contributors to craftsmanship, maintenance, and ward upkeep; protected class with access to welfare and education programs

### The Draconic Conclave

A gathering of all resident dragons, convened approximately every century or when significant challenges demand it. Its purpose is to debate amendments to the Ptarian Code and address major issues facing the sanctuary. The timing of the next Conclave is not fixed — it is called by Council decree and can serve as a major story anchor.

## History

### The Founding

After centuries of destructive dragon wars — culminating in the annihilation of a mortal civilization caught in the crossfire — a coalition led by an ancient gold dragon called the **Platinum Warden** (a devotee of Bahamut) convened in a neutral valley. They forged the **Ptarian Pact**, establishing the sanctuary and its governing code.

### Construction

Dragons, dragonborn followers, kobold laborers, and mortal artisans carved the **Stronghold of Drakenvale** from platinum-veined stone. Ancient glyphs and collective dragon magic wove protective wards into the valley itself.

### The Age of Harmony

Drakenvale thrived as a center of learning and craft. The Draconic Conclave was established. The **Radiant Crystal** was forged from the combined magic of the founding dragons and installed in the Draconic Hall. The **First Trial of Wings** resolved a territorial dispute without bloodshed, establishing a tradition.

### The Discordant War

A rogue faction of chromatic dragons attempted to unseal the **Temple to Tiamat**. The conflict scarred the valley, creating the **Rift of Discord** — a chasm of unstable magical energy. The Platinum Warden sacrificed himself to reseal the Temple. The Code was amended to prohibit actions destabilizing alignment harmony, and redemption rituals were introduced.

**Conflict resolution** occurs through mediation in the Amethyst Vault, the **Trial of Wings**, or structured community justice in the Draconic Hall. Severe cases may invoke Zone of Truth or divination magic.

### The Trial of Wings

A non-lethal, ritualized duel overseen by the Council, used to settle disputes of honor or grievance. Rarely invoked but highly respected. Originated from the first territorial dispute post-founding. Open to dragons and dragonborn; mortals and kobolds may request it but typically use mediation instead.

## Military and Security

### The Dragon Guard

Led by **Zarkeros the Inferno**. Drakenvale's primary external defense force.

- **Composition** — dragonborn warriors, kobold engineers, mortal mages, dragons of various alignments
- **Aerial Division** — dragons, wyvern riders, enchanted constructs; reconnaissance and aerial combat
- **Reserve Forces** — volunteers from mortal and kobold populations; logistical and combat support in crises
- **Responsibilities** — border patrol, repelling external threats, enforcing the Code during escalated internal disputes, ceremonial roles at festivals

### The Wardens

Led by **Eryndor the Radiant** in their standard capacity. Temporarily fall under **Zarkeros** when militarized.

- **Composition** — dragonborn paladins and clerics, kobold ward specialists, mortal investigators and mages, construct guardians
- **Responsibilities** — guarding the Platinum Heart, Amethyst Vault, and sealed Temple to Tiamat; investigating magical anomalies and internal disturbances; neutralizing internal threats; precision military operations when mobilized
- **Note** — the Wardens are distinct from the Dragon Guard. They are Drakenvale's internal security and sacred site force; the Dragon Guard is its external military.

### Intelligence

**Varethyn of the Amethyst Gaze** operates the **Amethyst Veil** — a covert intelligence network running through the Silver Scale Trading Company's trade routes. Not publicly known.

## Economy

- **Internal** — barter in enchanted artifacts, knowledge, and services; no currency
- **External** — limited trade with trusted allies; rare materials only; requires Council vetting
- **Key exports** — enchanted goods, magical expertise, rare crafted artifacts
- **Key imports** — rare materials unavailable in the valley (mithral, adamantine, etc.)
- **External trade partner** — [[Silver Scale Trading Company]] (independent guild with deep Council ties; all three dragons hold advisory or operational roles within it)

## Religion

| Deity | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Bahamut | Primary — justice, mercy, law; inspires the Code; the Platinum Heart is his sanctum |
| Tiamat | Cautionary figure; her sealed Temple is a warning against unchecked ambition |

Spiritual life is maintained by the **Platinum Acolytes**, a religious order led by Eryndor dedicated to Bahamut's ideals. Seasonal ceremonies, atonement rituals, and the Trial of Wings reinforce the sanctuary's spiritual identity. Other peaceful faiths aligned with the Code are permitted.

## GM Notes

- The valley's location is a secret. Players learn it only through earned trust, a specific discovery action, or meeting the strict Council disclosure criteria (strategic alliance, humanitarian need, or diplomatic necessity — all requiring unanimous approval).
- The Temple to Tiamat is a standing tension hook. It can be partially unsealed as a major story event. The Wardens monitor it constantly.
- The Rift of Discord is a physical location with unstable magic; usable as a hazard, dungeon site, or plot anchor.
- The Radiant Crystal is in the Draconic Hall. Players who witness a Council session may see it invoked. It cannot be removed without severe consequences.
- The Draconic Conclave is a malleable story anchor — its timing is unset. A Conclave approaching, underway, or just concluded can drive major political storylines.
- Mortal and kobold characters have petition rights but no vote. A player character of either type navigating Council politics must work through dragonborn stewards or earn direct dragon favor.
- The Wardens and Dragon Guard have overlapping jurisdiction during crises — a source of internal tension between Eryndor and Zarkeros.
- Varethyn's Amethyst Veil is unknown to most residents. Discovery of it creates leverage — and danger.
