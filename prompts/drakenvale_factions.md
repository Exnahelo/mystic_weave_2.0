# Drakenvale — Factions & Governance

Version 1.0 — April 2026
Status: Canonical. This is the authoritative reference for all organizations, NPCs, and governance structures.

---

## The Draconic Council

Three ancient dragons govern Drakenvale by consensus. All major decisions require unanimous approval. When consensus fails, they invoke the **Radiant Crystal** — a divine artifact in the Draconic Hall forged from the combined magic of the founding dragons. It provides impartial guidance through Bahamut's influence. It cannot be removed from the Hall without severe consequences.

| Dragon | Type | Alignment | Domain |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Eryndor the Radiant | Gold Dragon | Lawful Good | Justice, diplomacy, moral guidance; leads the Wardens and Platinum Acolytes |
| Zarkeros the Inferno | Red Dragon | Lawful Evil | Strength, defense, resource management; leads the Dragon Guard and Infernal Forge Guild |
| Varethyn of the Amethyst Gaze | Amethyst Dragon | Neutral | Wisdom, arcane knowledge, balance; oversees the Amethyst Vault; runs the Amethyst Veil covertly |

**Council operations:**

- Regular decisions: unanimous vote required
- Radiant Crystal: invoked when consensus fails; provides guidance, does not issue commands
- Execution: valid penalty, requires unanimous approval; reserved for offenses of the gravest order (unsealing the Temple to Tiamat, equivalent)
- Exile: serious violations; enforced by magical ward
- No independent judiciary — an acknowledged structural gap

**The Draconic Conclave:**
All resident dragons. Convened every century or by Council decree. Debates amendments to the Ptarian Code. Timing is unset — a major story anchor. A Conclave approaching, underway, or recently concluded can drive major political storylines.

## The Ptarian Code

The governing philosophy and living legal document of Drakenvale.

**Core Principles:**

- Justice and Mercy — fairness, proportionality, opportunity for redemption
- Balance and Unity — harmony among all alignments
- Responsibility and Restraint — power wielded for the greater good
- Sanctity of Sacred Sites — Platinum Heart, Amethyst Vault, Temple to Tiamat are inviolable
- Secrecy of the Sanctuary — location disclosure requires unanimous Council approval; criteria: strategic alliance, humanitarian need, or diplomatic necessity

**Legal proceedings:** Held in the Draconic Hall. Evidence, argument, divine insight. Severe cases invoke Zone of Truth or divination magic.

**Punishments:** Restorative service (minor) → exile (serious) → binding oaths (severe) → execution (gravest, unanimous Council only).

**Amendments:** Proposed and debated at the Draconic Conclave only.

## Military & Security

### The Dragon Guard

**Led by:** Zarkeros the Inferno
**Role:** External defense. Primary military force.
**Composition:** Dragonborn warriors, kobold engineers, mortal mages, dragons of various alignments.

Divisions:

- *Aerial Division* — dragons, wyvern riders, enchanted constructs; reconnaissance and aerial combat
- *Reserve Forces* — mortal and kobold volunteers; logistics and combat support in crises

Responsibilities: Border patrol, repelling external threats, enforcing the Code during escalated internal disputes, ceremonial roles at festivals.

### The Wardens

**Led by:** Eryndor the Radiant (peacetime); temporarily Zarkeros when militarized
**Role:** Internal security and sacred site protection.
**Composition:** Dragonborn paladins and clerics, kobold ward specialists, mortal investigators and mages, construct guardians.

Responsibilities: Guarding the Platinum Heart, Amethyst Vault, and sealed Temple to Tiamat; investigating magical anomalies and internal disturbances; precision military operations when mobilized.

**Tension:** The Wardens and Dragon Guard have overlapping jurisdiction during crises — a persistent source of friction between Eryndor and Zarkeros. When Wardens are militarized, sacred site coverage becomes a vulnerability.

### The Sapphire Sentinels

**Reports to:** Eryndor (justice/spiritual violations); Zarkeros (militarized/high-threat operations)
**Role:** Internal intelligence and covert threat neutralization.
**Composition:** Dragonborn paladins and clerics, mortal investigators and mages, kobold specialists.

Operations: Investigate corruption, sabotage, alignment tensions, forbidden magic. Rotate patrols in the Shadowed Hollows with strict two-hour maximum in-zone windows. Rules of engagement: observe, document, withdraw. Do not engage shadow mastiff packs unless directly attacked. Track: wraith population changes, flora harvesting, ritual markings, any chromatic dragon presence (immediate escalation trigger).

## Intelligence

### The Amethyst Veil

**Operated by:** Varethyn of the Amethyst Gaze — covertly
**Mechanism:** Runs through the Silver Scale Trading Company's trade routes
**Status:** Unknown to most residents of Drakenvale
**Discovery consequence:** Creates leverage against Varethyn — and significant danger for whoever holds it

## Civil Organizations

### The Platinum Acolytes

**Led by:** Eryndor the Radiant
**Role:** Internal religious order. Spiritual guidance, sacred site maintenance, seasonal ceremonies, atonement rituals, community healing after crises.
**Faith:** Bahamut.

### The Infernal Forge Guild

**Location:** Volcanic Highlands (powered by volcanic heat and dragon fire)
**Role:** Crafts enchanted weapons, armor, tools, artifacts, and constructs. Sustains daily life and defense. Overseen by Zarkeros.

### The Arcane Conservatory

**Role:** Preserves and advances magical knowledge and education. Houses the Lore Vault.
**Access:** Criteria not formally defined — open design question for content authoring.

### The Silver Wing Envoys

**Role:** External relations and trade. Manages interactions with trusted allies while maintaining Drakenvale's secrecy. Operates from neutral meeting locations — never Drakenvale itself.

### The Silver Scale Trading Company

**Role:** Primary external trade partner. Deep Council ties — all three dragons hold advisory or operational roles within it. Varethyn's Amethyst Veil intelligence network runs through its trade routes.

## Advisory & Representative Roles

- **Dragonborn Stewards** — mediators and enforcers of Council rulings; can hold Acolyte of Justice law enforcement roles
- **Mortal Representatives** — advise the Council; no vote; may petition for leniency
- **Kobolds** — protected class; access to welfare and education; essential to craftsmanship, maintenance, ward upkeep

## The Platinum Accord (Historical / Possibly Active)

A secretive order founded by the Platinum Warden. Overt purpose: peacekeeping and mediation. Covert purpose: guarding the Temple to Tiamat, the Rift of Discord, and the most dangerous draconic artifacts.

**Historical structure:**

- *Platinum Warden* — founder; ancient gold dragon; devoted to Bahamut
- *The Radiant Triad* — after the Warden's sacrifice, Eryndor, Zarkeros, and Varethyn assumed leadership
- *The Accorded*: Platinum Shields (dragonborn warriors), Amethyst Oracles (mages/seers), Warden Scouts (mortal and kobold covert agents)

**The Oath of Scales:** Sacred initiation performed before the Platinum Flame. Bonds the member's soul to Bahamut's ideals. Grants divine blessings; binds to strict adherence to the Ptarian Code.

**Current status:** Officially faded. Some believe it still operates in shadow, members chosen by Bahamut directly, identities hidden even from the Council. Prophecy: the Platinum Warden's spirit will rise again in Drakenvale's greatest hour of need.

## Key NPCs

### Eryndor the Radiant

Gold dragon. Lawful Good. Head of diplomacy, judiciary, and moral guidance. Leads the Wardens and Platinum Acolytes. Embodies Bahamut's ideals. In tension with Zarkeros over overlapping jurisdictions during crises.

### Zarkeros the Inferno

Red dragon. Lawful Evil. Oversees defense, military strategy, and resource management. Leads the Dragon Guard and Infernal Forge Guild. Practical, decisive. In tension with Eryndor. His domain is the Volcanic Highlands.

### Varethyn of the Amethyst Gaze

Amethyst dragon. Neutral. Mediator, guardian of arcane knowledge, protector of balance. Oversees the Amethyst Vault. Covertly runs the Amethyst Veil intelligence network through the Silver Scale Trading Company. This is not publicly known — discovering it is a major plot event.

## Crisis Protocols

**Level 1 — Minor:** Handled by Wardens and Reserve Forces.
**Level 2 — Moderate:** Coordination across multiple divisions and guilds.
**Level 3 — Severe:** Full mobilization under direct Draconic Council oversight.

**Command hubs:** Primary — Draconic Hall. Secondary — temporary hubs near affected areas led by Warden and Dragon Guard captains.

**Communication:**

- Primary: draconic telepathy (dragons, instant); enchanted scrolls (documentation and directives)
- Redundant: magical messengers/constructs; signal beacons (enchanted crystals with crisis-level signals); kobold courier division

**After-action:** All organizations debrief to Draconic Council. Infernal Forge and Circle of Artisans restore supplies and repair damage. Platinum Acolytes conduct community healing rituals.

## Economy

**Internal:** Barter in enchanted artifacts, knowledge, and services. Knowledge Tomes certified by the Amethyst Vault serve as intellectual currency. Traditional platinum, gold, silver, copper coins for daily transactions. Platinum coins engraved with draconic symbols for high-value trades.

**External:** Limited trade with trusted allies via Silver Wing Envoys. Rare materials only. Requires Council vetting. All agreements include secrecy clauses.

**Key exports:** Enchanted goods, magical expertise, rare crafted artifacts.
**Key imports:** Mithral, adamantine, materials unavailable in the valley.

**Sustainability:** Mining, crafting, and agriculture conducted under Ptarian Code principles. Environmental preservation is mandatory.
