## Alpine Peaks

**Description:** Towering snow-capped mountains encircling Drakenvale. Natural barrier. Hardy flora and fauna adapted to cold, high-altitude conditions. Glacial crystals infused with ice magic form natural wards against fire-based threats.

**Features:** Jagged cliffs and icy slopes; glacial streams feeding the valley; perches and caves for large dragons.

**Flora (summary):** Frost-tolerant ironwood trees, alpine moss, crystalline flowers.

**Fauna (summary):** Sabertooth tigers, winter wolves, giant eagles, ice drakes, frost wyverns.

**Monsters:** Frost Linnorm, Ice Drake (Chaotic Evil; freezing touch), Whitespawn Hordling, Whitespawn Iceskidder, Silver Dragons, White Dragons, Mountain Landwyrm (Lawful Evil; up to 80 ft. long; sleeps most of the time; waking causes earth tremors), Tundra Landwyrm (Neutral; cold plains; lies under snow in semi-hibernation; drinks blood of warm-blooded creatures), Whitespawn Berserker.

**Amphibious:** Ice Mephit (Amphibious Form), Frost Frog, Glacier Crab.

**Aquatic:** Ice Minnow, Quipper, Water Weird (Larval Form).

**Arboreal:** Treant Sapling, Twig Blight (Climbing Variant).

**Semi-Arboreal:** Sabretooth Tiger.

**Semi-Subterranean:** Ice Mephit Minions.

**Subterranean:** Ice Rats, Crag Cats, Giant Ice Beetle, Frost Myconid.

**Cryophilic:** Phase Spiderlings (Frost Variant), Ice Wasp, Ice Scorpions, Frost Serpent.

**Alpine Generalists:** Frost Jackal, Frost Bear, Elk of the Frost Lord.

## Feywood Glade

**Description:** Liminal space partially in this plane, partially in the Feywild. Fixed external location within the Temperate Forest; variable internal spatial logic. Entry and exit points do not correspond — you may enter at one point and emerge anywhere in Drakenvale. Time inside is roughly equivalent to outside for short visits; diverges on extended stays.

**Features:** Shifting internal paths; moonlit bark glow on all trees; fey circles (glowing toadstool rings); the Heart Tree at the deepest navigable point.

**The Heart Tree:** Ancient tree of unidentifiable species. The land's consciousness made physical. Not a deity, not a character — a presence. Communicates through impression, emotional state, image rather than language. The GPT describes it; does not run it.

**The Logic:** No rules — a nature. Consequences flow from understanding or misunderstanding the land. Respect it as living. Know what you're touching. Promises made here carry fey weight. Domination attempts fail — the land is older than any of them.

**Flora:** Fey Orchids (charm/compulsion/counter-enchantment component; acknowledge before taking), Illusion Blossoms (hallucinations on skin contact; illusion crafting), Moonlit Bark Trees (tended by dryads; bark has warding properties; harvest with acknowledgment), Glowing Toadstools in fey circles (stepping in without awareness risks brief Feywild displacement), mixed edible/toxic/hallucinogenic flora with no visual distinction.

**Fauna:** Pixies, dryads, sprites, magical butterflies. Capricious, not hostile. Interested in players as phenomena. Elves live at the glade's edges as informal intermediaries.

**Magical Properties:** Fey Orchid extract for charm/compulsion/counter-enchantment; illusion and enchantment spell crafting amplified by Feywild bleed-through; fey circles for planar attunement work; Heart Tree proximity for land-state divination.

**Drakenvale Relationship:** Known, culturally present, rarely entered deeply. Elves at edges serve as interface. Dragonborn youth enter as informal rite of passage. Small group of regular practitioners (mostly elves) harvest for Arcane Conservatory. Council formally neutral — sovereign fey territory, no authority asserted.

**Magical Influence:** Ideal for illusion and enchantment crafting; fey orchid extract for charm and compulsion magic; time distortion as narrative tool.

## Shadowed Hollows

**Description:** Surface expression of the underground Temple to Tiamat. Not a natural biome — a symptom of centuries of necrotic seepage upward through the rock. Occupies the valley's darkest quadrant alongside the Rift of Discord (separate origin, shared neighborhood). No clean boundary — bleeds gradually into surrounding forest. Ungoverned in practice; monitored but not held.

**Features:** Half-radius magical illumination; shadows move independently of light; paths shift between visits; metallic taste in air; muffled sound; dead black ground at points directly above the Temple's thinnest rock cover.

**The Corruption:** Passive necrotic seepage, not directed by Tiamat (sealed). Hourly DC 13 Wisdom save for neutral/good-aligned creatures: failure advances Unease → Dread → Erosion. Long rest outside clears one level. Trained Wardens/Sentinels have advantage. Tiamat's followers and chromatic-aligned creatures experience the opposite — resonance, empowerment, magical responsiveness. This asymmetry makes the space unclearable by any aligned force.

**Flora:** Shadow Ferns (light-absorbing; concealment component; navigational disorientation), Nightshade Blossoms (paralytic pollen; poison crafting; Sentinel-tracked for cult harvest activity), Deathroot Vine (necrotic toxin; 24-hour healing suppression; cult ritual use; proximity awareness), Black Moss (absorbs magical energy; sleep in dense areas = -1 spell slot; ritual silence component).

**Fauna:** Shadow Mastiffs (enlarged, Temple-altered; leave Tiamat's followers alone — passive cult guardians), Giant Spiders (magically enhanced paralytic venom), Necrotic Wraiths (bound echoes of Hollows dead; hostile to positive energy; Tiamat's followers know how to avoid triggering them), Corruption-Drawn Visitors (rogue chromatics, corrupted dragonborn, Tiamat-attuned creatures using Hollows as waypoint/meeting/power site).

**Cult Presence:** Primary gathering location for Tiamat's followers in Drakenvale. No fixed installation — traces only (ritual markings, Deathroot arrangements, ceremony ground). Sentinels track frequency and scale of activity as Temple seal intelligence.

**Warden/Sentinel Operations:** Rotating patrols, two-hour maximum in-zone. Perimeter-focused. Observe, document, withdraw. Watch for: wraith population increases, flora harvesting, new ritual markings, any chromatic dragon presence.

**Magical Properties:** Necrotic crafting components; Nightshade paralysis; Deathroot healing suppression poison; Black Moss silence component; healing magic suppressed (half value) throughout.

## Rift of Discord

**Description:** A chasm of floating rock shards and glowing rifts in the air created during the Discordant War when the Platinum Warden sacrificed himself. No sustained life. Completely unpredictable magical weather. The Rift pulses with energy that shifts color and intensity.

**Features:** Floating rock shards at various heights; rifts flickering between colors (each corresponding to different magical effects); wild magic zones, dead magic zones, and spatial distortions.

**Flora:** None — magic is too chaotic for sustained life.

**Fauna:** Chaos elementals, shadow drakes, wandering undead drawn by residual Discordant War necrotic energy.

**Magical Influence:** Chaotic energy for powerful unstable enchantments — high reward, high risk; rift shards carry residual chaos magic for wild magic and chaos-themed crafting.

*Note: No extended species list authored for this biome — description level only.*
