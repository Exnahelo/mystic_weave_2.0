# Eryndor's Lair

The primary residence of Eryndor the Radiant, carved into the mountain's interior near Lastmark's approach. Unlike the dark hoard-caves of lesser dragons, the Lair is bright, open, and deliberately beautiful — enchanted skylights bring real sunlight to the inner chambers, polished marble and veined gold-quartz line the floors and walls, and the whole space carries the character of a sacred Bahamutic shrine integrated with the working rooms of a practicing Council diplomat. Eryndor lives, meditates, and conducts sensitive external business here. Access is controlled; arrival is typically by sanctioned convoy from Lastmark, and foreign diplomats approved for his audience are brought in under the realm's disorienting-wagon protocol so their route cannot be traced back after the visit.

## Scene Texture

The Lair is carved into a substantial mountain face not far from Lastmark's approach road, accessible by a controlled route that departs the main fort traffic at a screened junction. The entrance is a tall arched opening cut from the living stone, sized for Eryndor's passage but not ostentatious — the opening reads as a natural cleft that has been refined rather than a monument carved to impress. From outside, the Lair is easy to miss if one does not know it is there. This is deliberate.

Past the outer arch, the Lair opens into its **Reception Hall** — a vaulted chamber of polished stone floored in pale marble, its ceiling pierced by four large enchanted skylights that channel daylight from the mountain's outer face through carefully angled crystal lensing. Even on overcast days, the Reception Hall is bright. On clear days it is luminous. The skylights are engineering work of the first order, a collaboration between the Arcane Conservatory's ward-wrights and the Infernal Forge's enchantment-craft specialists; they do not admit rain, wind, or cold, but they admit light in a way that feels unmistakably like real sunlight.

The Hall's walls are inlaid with veined gold-quartz running in organic patterns, catching the skylight and reflecting it softly outward. A raised dais at the Hall's far end accommodates Eryndor when he receives visitors in this space. The dais is mortal-scale at its outer edge — visitors address him from below the raised stone — while the inner portion is sized for a gold ancient dragon to settle in repose.

Beyond the Reception Hall, the Lair branches into its functional chambers:

### The Shrine

Deeper into the mountain, the **Shrine of Bahamut** occupies a circular domed chamber approached by a long corridor whose walls bear relief carvings of the Ptarian Pact's founding scenes. The Shrine itself holds a central altar of pale marble, an eternal flame (smaller than the Platinum Heart's, but tended by the same order and drawn from the same sacred source), and the realm's finest private collection of Bahamutic liturgical and meditative artifacts. Eryndor meditates here daily. The Shrine is private to Eryndor and his senior religious staff; visitors are not ordinarily admitted, though an invited diplomatic guest on a matter of unusual sacred weight may be brought here for a formal oath.

### The Audience Chambers

A series of chambers — three formally titled, several smaller antechambers — accommodate different tiers of Eryndor's diplomatic work:

- **The High Audience** — largest, most formal, for full diplomatic receptions with ranking external emissaries. Used sparingly; most external contact is handled at lower formality.
- **The Working Chamber** — Eryndor's ordinary working space for day-to-day diplomatic correspondence, Silver Wing coordination, and the continuous administrative flow of his Council portfolio. He is most often encountered here.
- **The Council Annex** — a small private chamber where Eryndor meets with Silver Wing Envoy leadership, senior Ward-Keepers, and the rare visiting figure from outside the realm who carries unusual standing. Discussions here are held in confidence.
- **Antechambers** — smaller spaces for waiting, private preparatory work, or conversations that do not rise to a formal audience.

### The Private Quarters

Eryndor's personal residence is at the Lair's deepest and highest interior, accessible through a private corridor from the Shrine. His sleeping space, his personal library, his working study on matters not requiring formal engagement — all here. The Private Quarters are absolutely not open to visitors. They exist for narrative completeness and for the occasional storyline in which a character has genuine cause to reach him there (unlikely in ordinary play).

### The Steward's Wing

A practical working complex closer to the entrance houses the **Lair Steward's** staff — scribes, attendants, the couriers who handle Silver Wing dispatch, the small detachment of Dragon Guard assigned to the Lair's security, and the kitchens and service spaces supporting the entire facility. The Lair is a working residence; its back-of-house is substantial.

## The Disorienting-Wagon Protocol

The realm occasionally needs to receive foreign diplomats whose cause is legitimate but whose full knowledge of the realm's interior cannot be permitted. The **disorienting-wagon protocol** handles this.

An approved foreign diplomat arriving at Lastmark under this protocol is escorted into an enclosed wagon — ordinary-looking from outside, specially prepared within. The wagon's interior is comfortable but windowless; subtle wardwork in the walls provides gentle disorientation that makes tracking direction, elapsed time, or surface terrain impossible from within. The wagon departs Lastmark, travels what appears to the passenger to be a winding and variable route, and arrives at the Lair's outer approach. The passenger disembarks with no reliable sense of how far they have traveled or in what direction.

The return trip uses the same protocol in reverse. The diplomat leaves the Lair, rides back through the same disorienting interior, and is decanted at Lastmark with no retained geographic knowledge of the Lair's location.

This protocol is used for genuine diplomatic necessity. It is not a trick played on casual visitors. Its use is formally agreed to by the visiting diplomat as a condition of audience, and they are aware they are being disoriented; they consent to it because the audience is worth it. No deception is involved, merely the realm's structural insistence that foreign visitors not map its interior.

## Strategic Function

Eryndor's decision to reside near Lastmark rather than deeper in the realm is deliberate and long-standing. The placement serves multiple purposes:

- **Diplomatic acceleration.** Foreign messages, Silver Wing dispatch coordination, and external diplomatic work can reach Eryndor directly at Lastmark-adjacent speed rather than traversing the full realm interior. Urgent matters are handled in hours rather than days.
- **Border defense.** A Council dragon residing at the realm's primary entry point means the realm has immediate high-tier response capacity if Lastmark is ever threatened. Eryndor has never been required to intervene in Lastmark's defense, but his presence alone is a meaningful deterrent that the realm's external adversaries are aware of.
- **Audience convenience.** Foreign dignitaries, sanctioned messengers, and external contacts who require Eryndor's audience can be received without requiring full interior transit. The disorienting-wagon protocol allows audience without compromising realm secrecy.
- **Silver Wing hub.** The Silver Wing Envoys dispatch from and return to the Lair for external missions under Eryndor's portfolio. This makes the Lair the operational hub for much of the realm's external diplomatic activity.

The arrangement trades some distance from Council deliberations (the Draconic Hall at the Stronghold) for considerable operational advantage at the border. Eryndor manages the tradeoff by flying the distance as needed — the journey from the Lair to the Stronghold is routine for him, and he is a regular presence at full Council sessions despite residing here.

## Eryndor the Radiant

Eryndor is the Lair's defining inhabitant. His character is established in canon: justice, moral guidance, diplomacy, Bahamut-aligned devotion. Within his own space, the qualities that define his Council role are visible in concentrated form. He is formal without being cold. He is patient with petitioners whose matters deserve patience and direct with those whose matters are simple. He takes his devotional practice seriously and does not perform it for external observers.

Audience with Eryndor at the Lair is substantial. Unlike a brief Council address or a formal ceremonial reception, a Lair audience is typically an extended engagement — the petitioner or diplomat is received in one of the audience chambers, the matter is thoroughly examined, and Eryndor's response is considered rather than quick. He values the time spent; a Lair audience is the realm's most focused engagement with this Council dragon.

Characters encountering Eryndor here should understand: he is interested in them, he will listen carefully, and he will respond on the merits. He does not suffer dishonesty well. He is not unreachable, but reaching him is earned through proper channels and genuine purpose.

## The Lair Steward

The **Lair Steward** manages the day-to-day operations of the Lair: staff, scheduling, Silver Wing coordination, security liaison, the practical administration of a working draconic residence. Tier-3 generative role. Typically a senior dragonborn or long-tenured mortal with decades of service to Eryndor's household and deep knowledge of the realm's diplomatic operations.

The Steward is the practical interface for most matters that do not require Eryndor's personal attention. A character arriving at the Lair for routine business — a Silver Wing dispatch, a message delivery, an administrative matter involving Eryndor's portfolio — will typically meet with the Steward rather than Eryndor himself. Access to Eryndor for direct audience is granted through the Steward's scheduling.

## Access

Lair access is strictly controlled. The standard path:

1. **Authorization at Lastmark.** A traveler requiring Lair access presents credentials at Lastmark's customs or command levels. Routine authorizations (known Silver Wing operatives, identified foreign diplomats on approved agendas, recognized realm officials) are processed quickly. Unfamiliar requests are routed up for confirmation.
2. **Convoy or disorienting-wagon transit.** Authorized residents of the realm travel to the Lair under light convoy escort — a minor Dragon Guard detachment that accompanies the traveler along the screened route. Foreign diplomats travel under the disorienting-wagon protocol.
3. **Reception at the Lair.** Arrival is received by the Steward's staff. The visitor is escorted to the appropriate chamber for the nature of their business.

Unauthorized approach does not reach the Lair. The route from Lastmark's screened junction is patrolled, the Lair's entrance is watched, and the Dragon Guard detachment stationed here is not passive. A traveler who somehow evaded Lastmark's controls and attempted to find the Lair independently would be intercepted before arrival or, in rare cases of actual competence evading the patrols, confronted at the entrance by the detachment with firm but non-lethal response. Genuine hostile intent would invoke Eryndor himself, and that encounter would be brief.
