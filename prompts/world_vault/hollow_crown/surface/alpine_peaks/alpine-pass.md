# Alpine Pass

A high mountain pass cutting through the outer ring of peaks that encircle Drakenvale. The air is thin and bitterly cold. Glacial crystals embedded in the cliff faces glint with pale blue light — ice magic infused into the rock itself, forming passive wards against fire-based intrusion. The path is narrow and exposed, with sheer drops on both sides. Ice drakes and frost wyverns patrol the skies overhead, territorial creatures that tolerate no trespass lightly.
