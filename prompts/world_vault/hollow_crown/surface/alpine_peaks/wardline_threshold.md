# Wardline Threshold

The air changes here. A faint shimmer hangs across the path like heat haze over cold stone — the outer boundary of Drakenvale's ancient wards. The barrier is invisible to the untrained eye but unmistakable to anyone with magical sensitivity. It deters intruders through a deepening sense of wrongness, vertigo, and disorientation that worsens with every step forward. Passing through requires either draconic guidance, knowledge of the ward-paths, or sheer force of will. Carved wardstones mark the boundary at intervals, half-buried in moss and scree, their runes still faintly luminous after centuries.
