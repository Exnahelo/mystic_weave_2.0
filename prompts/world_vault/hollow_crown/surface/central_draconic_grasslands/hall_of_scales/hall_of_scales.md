# Hall of Scales

The realm's broad university and its surrounding college town, set in the open Draconic Grasslands between Drakenvale City and Ashfield. The Hall itself is an academic complex of tiered lecture halls, drilling grounds, workshop annexes, and archives, built for general higher education across scholarship, civic learning, martial training, guild foundations, and the first tiers of magical study. The town that has grown around it exists to support the student population — inns, taverns, bookbinders, supply shops, and the ordinary civic trades of a place shaped by an academic calendar. Ardrynn the Measured, dragon scholar, oversees the institution and makes her residence here.

## Scene Texture

The Hall sits on an open rise in the central Draconic Grasslands, within a day's walk of both Drakenvale City to the southwest and Ashfield to the southeast. The approach from either direction follows the broad stone-and-packed-earth civic roads that cross the grasslands, passing fields and scattered herder camps before the Hall's outer buildings come into view.

The academic complex is not a single structure. It is a scatter of buildings arranged loosely around a central quadrangle — the Hall proper at the quad's north side, a formal columned structure of pale grassland stone with layered roofs in the realm's draconic architectural idiom. The flanking buildings handle specialized functions: the Martial Yards to the east, where Dragon Guard instructors and Warden-aligned trainers drill combat candidates; the Workshop Wings to the west, where guild-track students apprentice with visiting craftsmen; the Archive Block to the south, a squat reinforced structure holding the Hall's teaching libraries and student records; and the Tower of Foundational Arts to the northeast, where first-tier magical instruction is conducted under heavy containment wards appropriate for beginners' mistakes.

Ardrynn herself is housed in the Hall proper. A private wing extends off the building's rear, sized to accommodate a dragon and constructed to admit her through a ground-level arch rather than forcing a landing on the Hall's roof. She can be seen walking the outer grounds at most hours — slow, deliberate, occasionally stopping to speak with a passing student or to look at something in the sky only she has noticed.

Outward from the academic complex, the town spreads across the open grassland in a loose grid. Closest to the Hall are the student inns and boarding houses, clustered thickly along the three main entry roads. Beyond them, the commercial district — bookbinders, stationers, ink and parchment merchants, food stalls, clothiers, and a surprising number of taverns for a town this size. Further out, the residential quarter and the handful of permanent faculty residences. The town's population swells during the academic terms and empties noticeably during the long breaks, when most students return home to settlements across the realm.

The mood is young. The streets carry the particular texture of a place shaped by people in their late teens and early twenties — argumentative, experimental, sometimes ridiculous, frequently hungover. The Dragon Guard maintains a small presence to keep order, but most of the town's discipline comes from Ardrynn's quiet expectation that students represent the Hall well, and the fact that anyone who embarrasses the Hall answers to her.

## Functions

- **General higher education.** The Hall trains the realm's broad professional and civic class. Courses of study cover scholarship, history, linguistics, law, civic administration, guild trades, martial foundations, Warden pre-training, and the first two tiers of magical theory and practice. Students typically arrive at sixteen to eighteen and remain for three to six years depending on track.
- **Pre-institutional training.** Candidates destined for the Dragon Guard, Wardens, Platinum Acolytes, Silver Wing Envoys, or Arcane Conservatory often complete preparatory study here before advancing to their specialized institutions. The Hall is the realm's common intellectual foundation.
- **Civic scholarship.** Faculty produce the ordinary scholarly work that keeps the realm's institutions literate — annotated editions of the Codex of Remembrance, trade manuals, historical monographs, civic law reviews. The Archive Block holds both teaching copies and working scholarly materials.
- **Commissioned research.** The Hall handles commissioned work that does not rise to the Arcane Conservatory's specialized domain: historical investigations for the Administrative Quarter, genealogical work for families, trade and logistical analysis for the Silver Scale Trading Company, treatises on practical matters for the Circle of Artisans.
- **Cultural anchor.** Alumni of the Hall fill the realm's middle and senior professional ranks. Its graduates staff the Administrative Quarter, the lower tiers of the Dragon Guard and Wardens, the guild administrations, and the civic institutions of every settlement. The Hall's reach is cultural, not just academic.

## Ardrynn the Measured

Ardrynn is the Hall's overseer and its most visible figure. She is not its day-to-day administrator — that role falls to the Rector — but she sets its direction, arbitrates its deepest disputes, and guarantees its institutional continuity. She has held the position for centuries. Her bearing is calm in a way that is sometimes mistaken for slowness. It is not slowness. It is the deliberateness of a mind that weighs everything before speaking.

Students occasionally approach her with questions. She answers thoughtfully, at whatever length the question deserves, and does not patronize. Faculty address her by name. The Rector consults her on weighty matters but handles routine administration without bothering her. She accepts this arrangement.

Ardrynn holds the distinction of being one of the few non-Council dragons with a standing right of address before the Draconic Council on matters of educational policy. She exercises this right rarely. When she does, the Council listens.

## The Rector

The Rector of the Hall of Scales is the operational head of the institution — handling faculty, curriculum, admissions, student discipline, and the interface with visiting instructors and commissioned work. The role is Tier-3 generative. The current Rector may be instantiated during play as needed. Typically the Rector is a long-tenured drakari or mortal scholar whom Ardrynn trusts to run the institution's daily business without requiring her attention.

## Student Life

The student body is species-mixed and age-cohorted. First-year students are easily identified by their uncertainty and their new academic robes. Upper-year students move with the assurance of people who have figured out where the best cheap food is. The social pattern follows academic tracks loosely — scholars cluster with scholars, martial candidates with martial candidates — but the town's central taverns mix everyone indiscriminately.

Students traveling to Ashfield for weekend escape is a well-established pattern. Ashfield is close enough to walk, has cheaper beer than the Hall's own taverns, and is just far enough from faculty eyes to feel like freedom. Drakenvale City is the term-break destination — longer trip, bigger options, significantly more trouble available. The Hall's faculty pretend not to notice the migration patterns, which is itself a tradition.

A handful of students each year transfer upward to the Arcane Conservatory, to the Warden candidacy track at Platinum Oath Monastery, to Dragon Guard command training, or to formal Acolyte initiation. Most graduate into civilian professional life and scatter across the realm. The alumni network is informal but real; a Hall graduate in any settlement can usually find another Hall graduate within an afternoon's inquiry.

## Access

The Hall and its town are fully open to travelers. The civic roads from Drakenvale City and Ashfield converge here, and the Draconic Grasslands Edge corridor runs through the outer fields. Visitors are welcome; scholars passing through for research, family visiting students, caravans resupplying between longer routes. The academic buildings themselves are open during teaching hours and locked overnight. Ardrynn's wing is private but not guarded — respect for the institution is considered sufficient security.

Students arriving for enrollment are processed through the Rector's offices in the Archive Block. Admission is by examination, recommendation, or regional sponsorship; the Hall does not turn away qualified applicants but does turn away the unqualified without apology.

## Authoring Notes

The Hall of Scales is explicitly a SEPARATE SETTLEMENT from Drakenvale City and the Stronghold. It is its own navigable node with its own civic character. Ardrynn is ANCHORED HERE — not in Drakenvale City, not in the Stronghold. A character seeking Ardrynn goes to the Hall.

The Hall's relationship to the Arcane Conservatory is explicitly upward-transition. The Hall handles foundational magical education (first two tiers); the Conservatory takes over at advanced levels. A student demonstrating exceptional arcane talent at the Hall may be evaluated for Conservatory transfer; acceptance is rare.

The Rector is a Tier-3 generative role.

The connection to `drakenvale-city` is two-way (students commute, faculty visit). The connection to `ashfield` is two-way (weekend student migration). The connection to `draconic-grasslands-edge` establishes the Hall as linked to the broader grasslands corridor system.

Student culture and the Ashfield escape pattern are established here as canon. Future Ashfield authoring should acknowledge the student-weekend phenomenon.
