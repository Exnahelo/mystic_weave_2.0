# The Outer Bounds

Where Thornveil thins. Newer residents begin here. Trade leaves and returns here. The boundary between the city and the Glade around it is gradient, not line; the Outer Bounds is that gradient.

## Scene Texture

The district feels less like a city district than any of Thornveil's inner zones. Canopy is managed but less dense; dwellings are set further apart; the ambient field is moderated by the mix of civic and wild. A walker moving out through the Outer Bounds experiences the city gradually releasing them into the Glade's larger forest — the change in light, the quieting of civic sound, the sense of space opening.

Paths here are better developed than in the Outer Wild but less continuous than in the inner districts. The trade-staging area has its own working infrastructure; the residential clusters have their own walks; the district's outer edge trails dissolve into Glade wild paths that lead toward Vaelmere or outward.

The district receives more realm visitors than any other part of Thornveil. Not many — Thornveil is not an open city — but more than the inner districts. This shapes the Outer Bounds' register: slightly more accommodating, slightly more ready for outsiders, while still firmly Thornveil.

## Trade Staging

The Outer Bounds' trade-staging area is the city's interface with Vaelmere's flow. Goods move through it in both directions:

**Outbound.** Craft goods from the Crafthall, refined preparations from the Alchemy workshops, honey and wax from household keepers, forest wines and Goldcap cider from district producers — all aggregated at the staging area for transport to Vaelmere. The staging area includes covered storage, inventory-working spaces, and the working infrastructure for elven trader caravans.

**Inbound.** Realm goods arriving from Vaelmere are received here, inventoried, and distributed onward to Thornveil's markets, the Crafthall, specific noble family receiving halls, and the Council's administrative infrastructure. Metal tools, specialty craft materials, realm books and scrolls for those who commissioned them, occasional specific goods that particular Thornveil institutions have ordered.

The Trade Envoy Outer Bounds Liaison is a Council-appointed senior role, coordinating directly with Vaelmere's Trade Master. The Liaison's offices are in the staging area.

## Integration Zone

The Outer Bounds holds a disproportionate share of Thornveil's newer residents:

**Integrated non-elves.** Humans, rare dwarves, occasional dragonborn, others of long-term residence in Thornveil most commonly begin and often remain in the Outer Bounds. Integration here is less socially observed than in the inner districts — the Hearth's tight cluster life or the Groves' house-inflected register can be difficult to enter; the Outer Bounds is more open.

**Returning elves.** Elves returning from extended absences — realm-side work, outer-Glade postings, long study elsewhere, diplomatic service — often settle in the Outer Bounds first as they re-establish Thornveil residency. Some stay; some move inward over years.

**Younger elves establishing lives.** Elves in the equivalent of young adulthood sometimes establish their first independent residences in the Outer Bounds, working in the Crafthall or Common Hearth and moving inward as their own work and relationships require.

The district's residential pattern reflects all this. Clusters exist but are looser and less continuous than in the Hearth. Single-household dwellings are more common. Specific households maintain their own character — some quite elven-traditional, some visibly shaped by non-elven residents' contributions to the shared life.

## Visitors

Realm visitors on sanctioned business who are not proceeding to deeper Thornveil access typically spend their visits here. Trade representatives conducting extended business with the Trade Envoy corps, scholars with specific Outer-Bounds-level access, diplomatic staff for matters that do not require Council-level contact — all stay within the Outer Bounds during their Thornveil visits.

Visitors proceeding to deeper access pass through the Outer Bounds on their way and are collected by elven hosts here. The Outer Bounds is often the visitor's first in-city Thornveil experience and shapes their sense of the rest of the city accordingly.

## Connection to Vaelmere

The path between Thornveil's Outer Bounds and Vaelmere is Thornveil's most-traveled outbound route. The journey is done by elven trader caravans, by diplomatic envoys moving between the city and the trade waypoint, by elves with Vaelmere family or working relationships, and occasionally by realm visitors moving between the two under elven escort. The route is well-established and reasonably safe under Standing Green; under Turning or Stillfall the Glade's general responsiveness adjusts, and caravans move with more care.

## Authoring Notes

The Outer Bounds directly connects Thornveil to Vaelmere. GPT scenes involving travel between the two cities pass through this district.

The district's register is slightly more accommodating to realm visitors than inner Thornveil's. This is canonical. GPT should reflect the slight warming of social atmosphere without sacrificing the underlying Thornveil character.

Non-elven residents in Thornveil most often live here. Scenes involving non-elf Thornveil residents should generally place them in the Outer Bounds unless a specific deeper-district residency has been established.

The Trade Envoy Outer Bounds Liaison is a canonical senior role, distinct from the Crafthall Liaison and the Vaelmere Trade Master. Specific Liaison NPCs can be invented at scene time.

Threat level 1 reflects the slightly higher edge-condition risk — the district borders the Glade's wilder areas, trade traffic introduces more variables, and the realm-facing exposure is real. Still safe by any ordinary measure.
