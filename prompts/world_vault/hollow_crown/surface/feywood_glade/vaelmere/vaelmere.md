# Vaelmere

The Feywood Glade's outer settlement and trade waypoint, positioned in the Glade's realm-facing interior where the sanctioned path from the Feywood Glade Border leads inward before continuing onward to Thornveil. Vaelmere is where external commerce becomes elven commerce — realm traders bringing goods through the Border meet Vaelmere's trade houses; elven goods bound for the Border are staged here before their outward journey. The settlement is also a real elven community in its own right, home to roughly six hundred residents who combine the trader, craftfolk, and civic roles a commercial waypoint requires with the continuous elven life that characterizes all Feywood settlements. Vaelmere exists to protect Thornveil from the friction of daily commercial contact with the realm while honoring the mercantile alliance that has sustained the Feywood-realm relationship for a thousand years.

## Scene Texture

A traveler approaching Vaelmere from the Feywood Glade Border traverses several hours of Glade interior before reaching the settlement. The path is guided for sanctioned visitors and known to returning elven traders; it passes through ordinary Glade wilderness that gradually shows signs of regular use — subtle wear on the trail, occasional waymarkers, the softened character that regular elven presence imparts to terrain over generations.

Vaelmere emerges from the forest with more visible structure than Thornveil does. The settlement's outermost edges are clearer-bounded, marked by a gentle transition from wild Glade terrain to cultivated margins. Gardens appear, grown in loose organic patterns that follow the land's natural contours. Paths become clearer. Buildings become visible — first scattered at the settlement's outer reaches, then increasingly dense as the traveler approaches the central commons.

The architecture shares Thornveil's organic-elven idiom but is markedly more modest. Vaelmere's buildings are grown-with and crafted-with the forest, incorporating living trees and using elven methods, but the buildings are smaller on average, less ambitious in their integration, and more practical in their function. Where Thornveil's older structures represent centuries of accumulated craft in single dwellings, Vaelmere's buildings reflect a more working character — structures that serve their purpose well without demanding to be architectural statements.

The **Central Commons** of Vaelmere is a broad clearing roughly fifty paces across, with a grown wooden pavilion at its center that shelters the settlement's primary gathering space. Unlike Thornveil's Council-linked central areas, Vaelmere's Commons is explicitly a civic and commercial space — trade announcements, community meetings, and seasonal observances all happen here. The pavilion itself is a distinctive structure, woven from living and crafted wood over generations, with sides open in fair weather and closeable in storms.

Surrounding the Commons are the settlement's most important buildings:

- **The Trade Hall.** Vaelmere's primary mercantile institution, where elven and realm trade goods are received, inventoried, and prepared for onward movement. The Trade Master's offices are here, along with the storage spaces for goods in transit. Realm visitors on trade business interact primarily with this facility.
- **The Warden's Hall.** The office and residence of the Warden of Vaelmere, the settlement's civic authority. Smaller than the equivalent civic spaces in Thornveil but functionally complete for Vaelmere's scale.
- **The Guest House.** Accommodations for realm visitors who are sanctioned for Vaelmere access but not proceeding onward to Thornveil. Simple but comfortable lodging; most visits last from several days to weeks.
- **The Commons Hearth.** A shared kitchen and dining space where the settlement's community takes many of its meals. Not mandatory — residents eat at home as well — but a significant social anchor where daily life coalesces.

Beyond the Commons, Vaelmere's **residential districts** spread outward through the surrounding forest. Clusters of dwellings in the elven pattern, with individual families and extended family groups occupying specific grown-spaces maintained across generations. The settlement has been here for centuries; families have deep local roots; the character is stable and communal.

Workshops and craft sites are scattered through the residential areas rather than concentrated in a single crafthall. Vaelmere's craft economy is smaller and more diverse than Thornveil's — individual craftfolk working on goods for local use and for trade rather than the concentrated specialist production of the capital's Crafthall. Weavers, small-scale metalworkers, woodworkers, herbalists, and others work from home-workshops integrated with their dwellings.

Small **observance sites** dot the settlement — not major sacred spaces like the Heartwood or the Silent Grove, but small shrines and meditation spots tied to specific families or neighborhood traditions. Each is individual in character; collectively they reflect Vaelmere's living religious practice in the elven mode.

The **greenshield guardhouse** sits at the settlement's outer edge, on the path that continues inward to Thornveil. This is where Greenshield members holding outer-rotation duty operate from — not a fortress in any realm sense, but a practical working facility where the rotating guard presence is coordinated. The Greenshield Outer-Rotation Captain (Tier-3 generative role) commands this post.

The **ambient sound** of Vaelmere is livelier than Thornveil's but quieter than any realm city of comparable size. Trade activity produces periodic bursts of noise — cart wheels, loading sounds, conversation among arriving or departing traders — but the overall tempo is elven-quiet. Craftfolk at work produce the soft sounds of their specific crafts. The Commons holds conversation throughout the day. Seasonal birds and small wildlife add their continuous background layer.

The **light** matches the Feywood Glade's general character — dappled canopy filtering, distinctive quality compared to realm forests, the specific Feywood magical ambient register. Vaelmere's canopy is thinner than Thornveil's — this is a newer settlement in elven terms, built in terrain where the canopy has not yet grown as dense — so more direct light reaches the ground areas. Many residents appreciate this; the settlement's character includes more sun exposure than the deeper Glade interior, making it feel slightly more connected to the realm's ordinary world.

## Functions

### Mercantile Waypoint

Vaelmere's primary institutional function is mercantile coordination. The realm-Feywood exchange windows conducted at the Border are operationally supported from Vaelmere:

- **Outbound side.** Elven goods bound for realm trade are aggregated at Vaelmere from across the Glade — craftwork from Thornveil's Crafthall, herbal preparations and specimens from outer Glade sites, specific magical materials, and the various rare goods that the Feywood exports. Vaelmere's Trade Hall inventories and prepares these goods for the journey to the Border.
- **Inbound side.** Realm goods arriving through exchange windows are received at Vaelmere and processed. From here, goods are distributed onward — to Thornveil's markets for the realm-imports the capital requires, to specific noble families or institutions with standing orders, and to the Glade's broader elven community through Vaelmere's own markets.
- **Trade staffing.** The Trade Master and their staff coordinate the full operational flow. Trade Envoys who present at Border exchanges are drawn from Vaelmere's experienced trade community — these are the elves with the most sustained exposure to realm commercial practice, selected and trained for the work over careers that span centuries.

### Diplomatic Buffer

Vaelmere serves as the primary site for realm-Feywood diplomatic contact that does not require Council-level engagement. When realm officials visit the Feywood on matters below the Council threshold, they typically:

- Cross the Border with sanctioned approval
- Travel to Vaelmere
- Conduct their business with the Warden of Vaelmere, the Trade Master, or specific other Vaelmere figures as appropriate
- Depart from Vaelmere back to the Border

This structure protects Thornveil from the friction of routine diplomatic traffic while ensuring realm concerns receive appropriate engagement. Council-level matters — substantive diplomatic questions, major treaty revisions, formal ceremonial engagement — escalate to Thornveil with appropriate protocol. Ordinary matters resolve at Vaelmere.

Realm visitors who are approved for Vaelmere access but not for Thornveil access stay at the Guest House and conduct their business within the settlement. This arrangement is not viewed negatively by either side; it is simply the functional calibration of the realm-Feywood relationship.

### Elven Community

Beyond its institutional functions, Vaelmere is a real elven community where approximately six hundred elves live permanent lives. The community includes:

- **Established families.** Multi-generational families whose deep local roots span the settlement's history. Some families have held trade specializations for centuries; others are craftfolk, druids, or in other civilian roles.
- **Civic and trade leadership.** The Warden's circle, the Trade Master and experienced traders, and the senior figures in specific community functions.
- **Craftfolk.** Weavers, metalworkers, woodworkers, herbalists, and others producing both for local use and for the realm trade flow.
- **Greenshield members.** A rotating population of Greenshield members holding outer-rotation duty. Tours typically last years; some members choose to resettle in Vaelmere permanently after their rotation ends.
- **Non-elf integrated residents.** A small proportion of Vaelmere's population (perhaps 5-10% — notably higher than Thornveil's rate) consists of non-elves who have earned integration over years. Vaelmere's realm-facing function attracts somewhat more non-elf residents than the capital does; some are individuals who came as realm traders and eventually stayed, some are family members of integrated Thornveil residents who chose Vaelmere's somewhat more accessible life, and some have other specific histories.

The community's social fabric is warm and interconnected. Residents know one another across generations; the Commons Hearth hosts continuous low-intensity social life; observance practices integrate the community in the specifically elven way.

### Glade Edge Awareness

Vaelmere's residents serve as the Feywood's informal sensor for realm-side pressure. The Greenshield Outer-Rotation Captain coordinates monitoring of Border activity — not through formal patrol but through routine information gathering from traders returning from exchanges, visitors arriving with news, and the natural attentiveness of elves living near the Glade's edge.

Unusual realm-side activity — shifts in trade patterns, diplomatic developments of note, rare realm-side events with potential implications — flows from Vaelmere inward to Thornveil through established reporting channels. The Council receives this information through Vaelmere's Warden as part of the settlement's standing coordination with the capital.

## Civic Character

The **Warden of Vaelmere** is the settlement's senior civic authority. Tier-3 generative role. The Warden handles:

- Local dispute resolution
- Coordination with Thornveil's Council on matters affecting Vaelmere
- Oversight of the Trade Hall's operations
- Host-diplomat role for realm visitors conducting business at Vaelmere
- General civic administration

The Warden is accountable to the Council of Elders but operates with significant autonomy on routine matters. Council direction on major policy questions is observed; day-to-day judgment remains with the Warden. Wardens of Vaelmere typically serve for long tenures — decades to centuries — and become deeply identified with the settlement over their service.

The **Trade Master** handles the settlement's commercial operations. Tier-3 generative role. The Trade Master coordinates the operational flow of goods through the Trade Hall, manages the Vaelmere-based Trade Envoy corps, maintains the institutional relationships with realm-side SSTC counterparts, and ensures quality standards on both inbound and outbound exchange. The Trade Master works closely with the Warden but holds distinct operational authority.

The **Greenshield Outer-Rotation Captain** commands the outer-rotation duty at Vaelmere. Tier-3 generative role. This is a rotating position — senior Greenshield officers take outer-rotation commands for fixed tours before returning to Thornveil headquarters or to other rotations. The position is not hierarchical over Vaelmere's civilian administration; the Captain coordinates with the Warden on security-adjacent matters but does not direct civilian affairs.

## Character

Vaelmere has a distinctive atmospheric character that differentiates it from both Thornveil and the Silent Grove while remaining clearly part of the Feywood Glade. The settlement is **working**, in ways that neither the capital (civic/ancient/sacred-core) nor the Grove (focused/disciplined/ceremonial) quite are. Vaelmere's work is practical, commercial, outward-facing — the elves here are engaged with realm affairs in ways that the deeper Glade is not, and this engagement shapes the community's character.

The Vaelmere community is also somewhat more accustomed to external presence than Thornveil or any deeper Glade location. Residents see realm faces regularly — traders arriving for business, diplomats on specific matters, the occasional scholar or other approved visitor. This familiarity has produced a community that is comfortable with external interaction without losing its elven character. Vaelmere residents are still elves of the Feywood; they simply include realm contact in their ordinary working life in ways that deeper-Glade elves do not.

Humor in Vaelmere is somewhat more accessible than in Thornveil. The layered, slow-developing elven humor is still present, but Vaelmere residents have learned to include realm visitors in simpler observational humor that realm minds can register. A realm trader who becomes a regular at Vaelmere's Commons Hearth develops, over years, genuine social relationships with Vaelmere residents — something that happens far more rarely in Thornveil.

The respect principle — the Glade's continuous ecological-ethical framework — operates at Vaelmere as it does throughout the Feywood. Visitors behaving appropriately are welcomed warmly. Visitors behaving disrespectfully find the settlement's warmth cooling and, eventually, the Glade's geometry making their departure more straightforward than their continued presence. But Vaelmere's calibration toward external contact means that the threshold for disrespect is slightly more forgiving than at Thornveil — the settlement has learned patience for realm visitors who haven't yet fully learned the Feywood's way.

## Access

Vaelmere is accessible from the Feywood Glade Border by the sanctioned path, which a guide or Hall of Guides member handles for realm visitors without personal Glade experience. Travel time is several hours. Most realm visits to the Feywood end at Vaelmere; continuation to Thornveil requires additional approvals and arrangements.

**Typical sanctioned visitors:**
- Realm traders on mercantile business (the largest visitor category)
- Realm diplomats on matters below Council threshold
- Scholars with Feywood Council approval for research at Vaelmere-level access
- Approved guests of specific Vaelmere residents or institutions

**Visit duration:** Varies by purpose. Trade visits often several days to weeks. Diplomatic visits typically brief but sometimes extended. Scholar visits can run weeks or months depending on the work.

**Lodging:** At the Guest House for most visitors. Resident-hosted stays for guests of specific Vaelmere families or institutions.

**Continuation to Thornveil:** Requires separate approval from the Thornveil Council. Visitors approved for Thornveil access proceed with elven escort from Vaelmere; the journey takes additional hours. Most Vaelmere visitors do not continue onward.

**Dragon Guard presence:** None. Vaelmere is a sovereign elven settlement; the realm's military does not operate within it.
