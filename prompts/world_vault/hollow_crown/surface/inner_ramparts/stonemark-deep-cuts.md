# Stonemark Deep Cuts

The deeper quarry excavations below Stonemark's active operations — where the stone cuts become tunnels and the tunnels occasionally break into crystal vein territory. The Deep Cuts are not officially mapped beyond the last marked extraction point. Deephollow claims jurisdiction over anything crystal-bearing; Stonemark's Mining Authority claims everything within the Alpine Peaks foothills. The Council has ruled twice and satisfied nobody. Workers who push too deep sometimes do not come back, which is attributed to unstable rock rather than examined carefully.
