# Platinum Oath Approach

The single controlled corridor leading to the Platinum Oath Monastery. The route is narrow, exposed, and intentionally easy to defend, with layered sightlines and prepared fallback positions. Monastery-aligned traffic moves under explicit Vigil control and is treated as protected movement; attacks on this corridor are met with disproportionate response shaped by a prior elite Warden retaliation that established lasting deterrence. Greymantle passage is tolerated as an agreement of convenience, not trust.
