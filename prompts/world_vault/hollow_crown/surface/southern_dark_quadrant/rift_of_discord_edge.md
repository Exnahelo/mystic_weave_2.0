# Rift of Discord Edge

The perimeter of the Rift of Discord — the unstable magical scar left by the Discordant War. The edge is identifiable by floating rock shards, visible magical distortion in the air, and an ambient wrongness that most sentient creatures register before they can articulate it. The Rift itself begins where the rock stops being predictable. There is no settlement here, no trail, and no reason to be here without a specific purpose. The Wardens mark the perimeter with warning posts that are periodically destroyed by the Rift's instability and periodically replaced.
