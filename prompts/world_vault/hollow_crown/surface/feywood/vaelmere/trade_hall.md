# The Trade Hall

Where the Feywood meets the realm in the commercial register. Ancient, continuous, operationally precise.

## Scene Texture

The Trade Hall is the largest single structure in Vaelmere, built in the elven idiom to realm-utility scale — spanning multiple generations of growth, with working halls for inventory, storage chambers for goods in transit, receiving courts for exchange windows, and the Trade Master's offices set above the working floors. The structure integrates with several large old-growth trees; the floors are worked wood over packed earth; natural light floods the main floor through canopy-vents during the day, with bioluminescent Hearthlight supplementing in evenings.

The Hall's working register is purposeful and professional without being rushed. Trade goods move through in a steady rhythm — caravans arriving from Thornveil's Outer Bounds in the outbound direction, Border exchanges returning in the inbound direction, internal Glade goods flowing through at the scale appropriate to their production. Crates, sacks, barrels, woven baskets, bundled textiles, corked flasks of forest wine, sealed jars of preserved Heartmorel — the goods pass through, inventoried, checked, prepared for the next stage.

Sound in the Hall is the sound of competent commerce. Workers checking inventory against manifests, the controlled movement of goods, occasional conversation between elven staff and realm traders who are known regulars. The register is notably more "working" than Thornveil's ceremonial quiet; Vaelmere's signature atmosphere is most pronounced here.

## The Trade Master's Office

The Trade Master — Vaelmere's senior commercial figure — holds offices on the Hall's upper level. This is where the operational coordination happens: scheduling for upcoming Border exchange windows, quality-standard decisions, Trade Envoy corps management, coordination with Thornveil's Outer Bounds Liaison and Crafthall Liaison, institutional relationships with realm-side SSTC counterparts and specific realm trade houses.

The office is not grand. Elven civic architecture generally isn't; the Trade Master works from a space sized for their work rather than their status. Records — ledgers, correspondence, trade-route documentation, and the written archive that the Glade's mercantile relationship generates despite its oral primacy elsewhere — occupy significant space.

Meetings with visiting realm trade partners happen here. These are substantive working sessions; the relationships between the Trade Master and senior realm counterparts are often decades deep.

## The Trade Envoy Corps

Vaelmere-based Trade Envoys — the elves who present at Border exchange windows — are staffed and trained from the Trade Hall. This is not a military organization; it is a specialist professional corps with distinct selection, training, and career path. Envoys typically come up through Vaelmere's trade community, with the sustained exposure to realm commercial practice that the work requires. Careers span centuries; senior Envoys often have realm-counterpart relationships that trace back through multiple generations of their realm partners.

Training includes realm-language fluency (Common across multiple registers, specific trade jargon, diplomatic vocabulary), realm-side commercial practice (weights, measures, Drake-currency, negotiation conventions), Glade production knowledge (the origin and character of every good the Feywood exports), and the relational skill set the work requires — patience, discretion, professional warmth, and the specific elven humor that translates across the Border without giving offense.

## Inbound Flow

Realm goods arriving at Vaelmere from the Border are received, inspected, inventoried, and prepared for onward movement. The inbound flow handles:

- Metal tools and worked metal goods (the Feywood's largest import category).
- Specialty craft materials the Glade cannot produce internally.
- Realm books and scrolls commissioned by specific Glade institutions.
- Foodstuffs and consumables that specific Glade clients have ordered.
- Standing-order goods for specific noble houses, Council institutions, and the Silent Grove.

Goods are tagged, documented, and forwarded — most to Thornveil's Outer Bounds, some to specific recipients in the Glade, a small amount retained for Vaelmere's own use.

## Outbound Flow

Glade goods arriving at the Trade Hall from Thornveil (and from other Glade producers directly) are aggregated, standardized for export, and prepared for Border exchange windows. The outbound flow handles the full Feywood export set:

- Refined herbal preparations (Silverleaf, Dawnmint, Redveil Shelf).
- Preserved culinary fungi (Heartmorel in particular).
- Resinheart Pine refined resins, sealants, incense.
- Thornfiber basketry and fine weaving.
- Forest wines, Goldcap cider, Goldcap vinegar, honey mead.
- Select Honeybound honey and wax products.
- Winterglass Lichen dyes and ritual-marking pigments.
- Elven-worked goods from the Crafthall's metal, wood, and leather lineages (finished goods only; no raw timber ever).
- Very rare Longbough artifacts when available.

Oathbloom-based preparations are never exported. Heartwood-derived material of any kind is never exported. Living Witheroak wood is never exported. These exclusions are enforced rigorously at the Trade Hall.

## Visitors

Realm traders on sustained Feywood business spend much of their Vaelmere time in and around the Trade Hall. Longer-term relationships are supported by the Hall's working infrastructure — meeting rooms, documentation access, scheduling coordination, referral to specific Glade producers when needed.

Realm scholars of elven commerce occasionally have access to the Hall's records with Trade Master approval. These relationships span careers.

Casual visitors do not enter the Hall. It is a working institution, not a public space.

## Authoring Notes

The Trade Hall is the operational heart of the Feywood-realm trade relationship. GPT scenes involving cross-border commerce should place them here (or at the Border's exchange windows, which remain their own scene context).

The Trade Master is canonical Tier-3 generative. Specific Trade Master NPCs can be invented per scene; their character register is canonically professional, experienced, diplomatically fluent, and operationally precise.

Trade Envoys are a canonical professional corps, not a diplomatic or military one. They occupy their own distinct institutional register. GPT should not conflate Envoys with diplomats or with Greenshield Outer-Rotation staff.

Export and import categories are canonically fixed. Export exclusions (Oathbloom, Heartwood material, Witheroak) are hard rules. GPT narration involving Feywood trade should respect these without exception.

Threat level 1. The Hall is a working institution in a generally safe settlement; routine commercial risk exists (disputes, quality concerns, theft as a rare event), but no physical danger to visitors under ordinary conditions.
