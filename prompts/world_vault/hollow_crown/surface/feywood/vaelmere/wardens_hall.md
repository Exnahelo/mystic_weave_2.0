# The Warden's Hall

Vaelmere's civic seat. Operational, welcoming, restrained.

## Scene Texture

The Hall is mid-scale in Vaelmere's terms — larger than a typical family dwelling, smaller than the Trade Hall, sized for the combined functions of office, formal reception, and Warden residence. The structure is elven-architectural throughout, with an old-growth tree serving as the building's primary structural anchor and additional grown-and-woven extensions handling specific functional spaces. A small garden courtyard at the rear provides the Warden's private domestic space; formal receiving courts at the front handle visitor business.

The register is civic but warm. Vaelmere's general accommodation of realm visitors is carried through here; the Hall does not feel inaccessible or fortified. Visitors can arrive on reasonable business and be received; the Warden's working office is open to Vaelmere residents with civic questions on a pattern of casual availability that realm civic institutions rarely match.

Sound is moderate. Routine Vaelmere business produces a steady low hum; formal diplomatic meetings generate intermittent higher traffic; between these, the Hall is quiet enough to think in.

## The Warden's Office

The Warden's working office is the Hall's functional center. Records, correspondence, and the administrative documentation that Vaelmere's civic life generates occupy significant space — Vaelmere runs on more written record than Thornveil's oral primacy suggests, a reflection of its realm-facing register. Maps of the Feywood, the Border approaches, and the realm-side western forest hang where the Warden works with them.

The Warden meets with Vaelmere residents on civic matters through the office. Typical business:

- Coordination with the Trade Master on matters affecting Vaelmere-wide operations.
- Coordination with the Greenshield Outer-Rotation Captain on security-adjacent civic matters.
- Mediation of disputes that exceed cluster-level resolution.
- Oversight of integration cases (new residents, long-term guests transitioning to resident standing).
- Administrative decisions affecting Vaelmere's civic infrastructure.
- Specific coordination with Thornveil's Council on matters requiring their attention.

## Formal Reception

The Hall's formal reception spaces handle visiting dignitaries — realm envoys conducting Vaelmere-level business, Council envoys from Thornveil on specific matters, occasional visits from other Feywood institutions with Vaelmere-scale needs. The Warden holds court (in the specifically elven sense, not a realm courtroom sense) for matters requiring ceremonial register; this is not frequent but it is not rare.

Realm-side diplomatic protocol routes through the Warden for anything that does not require Council-level attention. This is substantial — the day-to-day diplomatic work of the Feywood-realm relationship largely resolves at Warden scale rather than Council scale.

The Warden of Vaelmere is canonically a senior civic figure — typically with decades of Vaelmere experience and sustained realm-contact fluency. The current Warden (unnamed canonically) holds the role as a matter of ongoing stable tenure rather than political rotation.

## Warden's Residence

The rear of the Hall houses the Warden's own living quarters. These are modest by realm noble standards and private by default; they are the Warden's home rather than a public space. The Warden's family, if any, lives here. Routine Vaelmere social invitations involving the Warden typically happen at other settings (the Commons Hearth, specific noble estates) rather than at the Hall itself.

## Visitors

Realm visitors arriving at Vaelmere with diplomatic business are typically received here. Those proceeding to Thornveil pass through a formal introduction at the Warden's Hall; those whose business is Vaelmere-level conduct it entirely here and at the Trade Hall.

Vaelmere residents attend the Hall for civic business on a pattern that is neither rushed nor scheduled — the Warden's availability is generally sufficient for the settlement's size, and queues of waiting residents are unusual rather than routine.

Casual visitors to the Hall are not common. The register is civic, not social.

## Authoring Notes

The Warden of Vaelmere is canonical Tier-3 generative (vaelmere.md). Specific Warden NPCs can be invented per scene within the canonical character register: senior civic figure, diplomatically fluent, operationally competent, socially warm within professional limits.

The Warden's civic authority is distinct from the Trade Master's operational authority and from the Greenshield Captain's security authority. The three roles coordinate but do not overlap hierarchically. GPT scenes should preserve this separation.

The Hall is a working civic building, not a palace or fortified seat. GPT narration should resist realm civic-monumentalism tropes.

Realm diplomatic business routes through the Warden by default. Only business that explicitly requires Council attention ascends to Thornveil. Realm visitors who expect to deal with "the elven leadership" at the Council level by default are navigating the relationship incorrectly; Vaelmere-level engagement is the default.

Threat level 1. Routine civic work carries routine civic risk; no physical danger to visitors under ordinary conditions.
