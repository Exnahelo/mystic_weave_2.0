# The Greenshields

The Feywood's primary defensive military order, and the order most Feywood elves know personally. Where the Heartwardens guard the sacred center, the Greenshields hold the practical perimeter — the settlements, the patrol-paths, the border, the daily security work that keeps Feywood life livable. The order is large by elven standards (though small by realm reckoning), distributed across multiple posts, and structured to sustain continuous defensive presence indefinitely. Greenshield service is the most common path for Feywood elves who choose to serve in arms; most elves of the Feywood have at least one family member who has served, currently serves, or will eventually serve in the order.

## Doctrine and Purpose

The Greenshields exist to defend the Feywood — the land, the people, the settlements, and the relationships that constitute the Feywood as it is. The framing is deliberately practical and strictly defensive. The order does not project elven power, does not deter realm-side forces, does not maintain a martial culture for its own sake, and does not seek combat. When combat comes, the order meets it competently and decisively; when it does not, the order does its work in the absence of combat without seeking to manufacture it.

The doctrine breaks into four pillars:

**Settlement defense.** Continuous protection of Thornveil, Vaelmere, the smaller Feywood settlements, and the outlying noble estates. This is most of what the order does on most days — presence, patrol, response, the practical maintenance of safety in places where elves live their lives.

**Border watch.** Protection of the elven side of the realm-to-realm border, in coordination with the Feywood's liminal geometry and the Heartwood's broader defensive working. The Border is not a wall the order mans; it is a threshold the order attends. Border-facing Greenshields read what crosses, who crosses, what does not cross that should have, and what crosses that should not have.

**Interior patrol.** Movement through the Feywood interior on regular patrol patterns, maintaining presence on the paths between settlements, attending to ecological signals the druids identify, keeping the order familiar with the land and the land familiar with the order.

**Crisis deployment.** When defense at the settlements or the border is insufficient — when the threat has come deeper than perimeter work can hold, or when external events require the Feywood to deploy beyond its own borders under the Oath of the Fallen — the Greenshields are the bulk of the deployable force. Crisis deployments are rare; the Discordant War is the canonical instance.

The order's stance toward realm-side forces is strictly cooperative and strictly bounded. The Greenshields do not coordinate operationally with the Dragon Guard, the Wardens, or any other realm body in routine work. Coordination occurs at Council-to-Council level under the Oath of the Fallen. The two realms defend separately and aid each other in true crisis, exactly as the Oath specifies.

## Structure

### The Commander

A single Commander leads the order, based at the Thornveil headquarters. Selected by the Council of Elders from among the order's most senior career officers, typically with input from the Heartwarden Captain and the senior Captains of the order itself. The Commander holds overall authority for doctrine, deployment, posting decisions, training oversight, and coordination with the Council, the Heartwarden Captain, the Ranger branch leadership, and (through Council channels) realm-side military authorities. Commanders serve until they choose to step down, until the Council requests their replacement, or until they accept transition to veteran's standing. Tenures of one to three centuries are typical.

House Thornmere is overrepresented in the Commandership through the same mechanism documented in `noble_families.md` — generational defensive-vinework tradition, Thornveil founding-mythic association, and family-culture transmission of the relevant martial-civic knowledge. Common but not exclusive; the role is not house-bound, and any sworn Greenshield is eligible.

### Captains

Senior officers commanding specific posts, units, or branches:

- **Senior Captain of Thornveil** — Thornveil settlement defense, headquarters operations, central training cadre.
- **Outer-Rotation Captain** — Vaelmere outer-rotation post, Border-facing operations conducted from Vaelmere, realm-facing diplomatic-security interface.
- **Captain of the Border Garrison** — continuous border-watch presence at the realm-to-realm threshold itself, distinct from Vaelmere-based outer-rotation.
- **Patrol Captain** — rotating interior patrol units between the named posts.
- **Captain of the Ranger Branch** — the order's specialist outer-range and threshold-watch arm; see `rangers.md`.
- **Captain of Training** — Thornveil-based initial training program for new members and continuing-skill maintenance.

Captains report to the Commander and exercise full operational authority within their posts. Captain tenures are typically half a century to a century and a half before rotation to other posts or to senior advisory roles.

### Senior Sergeants and Lieutenants

The order's working middle layer. Senior Sergeants are experienced veterans who lead patrol units, command shifts at the various posts, and carry the practical knowledge that turns Captain-level direction into actual work. Lieutenants are commissioned officers below Captain rank, typically commanding sub-units or specific functional sections within a larger post. The order draws no sharp line between the two in cultural standing; both hold real authority and both are understood as the layer where the order's effectiveness actually lives.

### Sworn Greenshields

The body of the order. Approximately five hundred at any given time, distributed across the various posts and units. Sworn Greenshields hold equal standing among themselves; differentiation is by post, specialty, experience, and the particular work the individual is suited to.

### Recruits

Approximately fifty to seventy recruits in initial training at Thornveil at any given time. Recruits are not yet sworn; their five years of training precede the oath. Either party may end the path before the oath.

### The Ranger Branch

A specialist branch of the order, organizationally distinct but operationally part of the Greenshields. Rangers handle outer-range fieldwork, threshold-watch duties, escort of sanctioned visitors through the Feywood's liminal geometry, and contact with realm-side forces in operational rather than diplomatic capacity. The branch maintains its own selection, training cadre, and unit structure under the Captain of the Ranger Branch. Rangers wear the Greenshield identity but operate under specialized doctrine; details in `rangers.md`. Ranger numbers are included in the order's overall ~600 active count.

## Service Terms

The Greenshield oath is taken at Thornveil headquarters at the close of initial training. The candidate stands before the Commander or a designated Senior Captain, speaks the oath in Elven, receives their first formal assignment, and takes their place in the order. The witnesses are typically the candidate's training cadre, family members invited as guests, and the Captain of Training.

The oath commits the candidate to **fifty years of initial service**. After fifty years, service continues at-pleasure with no formal renewal cycle. A Greenshield who wishes to continue simply continues; a Greenshield who wishes to step down submits notice through their chain of command and transitions out over a period appropriate to their post's continuity needs. Most career members serve a century to several centuries; some serve longer. The order's oldest currently-active Sergeants and Lieutenants have served seven hundred years or more.

Veteran's standing is a recognized status among Greenshields who have completed at least their initial fifty years. Veterans who have transitioned out of active service retain a formal connection to the order, may be called for consultation, retain access to community functions, and in extreme crisis may be recalled. The order does not impose a cap on service length.

For non-elven members — uncommon but not unprecedented — the fifty-year initial commitment is a meaningful fraction of most non-elven lifespans, and the order recognizes this in post and assignment decisions. Non-elven members are placed where their service will be effective within their lifespan and where eventual transition out will not destabilize a critical post.

## Training

### Initial Training

Five years at Thornveil headquarters with rotations through Vaelmere and the Border garrison. Structured but not regimented in the realm military sense; the order's culture preserves elven thoughtfulness even within the practical work of preparing soldiers.

- **Year one** — foundations: physical conditioning, basic combat, weapons handling, doctrine, geography and ecology of the Feywood, command structure, practical literacy. Recruits do not stand operational post.
- **Year two** — competence: deeper combat training, specialty introductions, first practical assignments under direct senior supervision, junior support at Thornveil settlement defense.
- **Year three** — application: operational training in real conditions, including extended Vaelmere rotations, border-garrison rotations, and patrol unit rotations. Operational under senior command, contributing to the order's work though not yet at full sworn capacity.
- **Year four** — selection: specialist tracks divide. Most recruits continue toward general sworn standing; those showing aptitude for Ranger work begin the branch's selection process; those showing other specialist aptitude begin those tracks. Year four is also when recruits who will not complete training typically separate.
- **Year five** — integration: final operational year before swearing, with assignments that closely approximate the recruit's intended post-swearing role. Year-five recruits are functionally sworn-equivalent in capability.

### Continuing Service

Greenshields maintain their skills through continuous practical service rather than periodic refresher training. Specific advanced training programs exist for specialist roles (Ranger-specific instruction, command-track preparation, training-cadre development) and are accessed as the member's career path requires. Veterans rotating between posts often spend a transition period at Thornveil's training facilities, refreshing skills relevant to the new post.

### Specialist Selection

Beyond Ranger selection (handled in `rangers.md`), the order maintains tracks for: border-garrison specialization, Vaelmere outer-rotation, patrol leadership, training cadre, and command track. Selection occurs through demonstrated aptitude during initial training (for tracks branching from year four) or through career progression for tracks that develop in mid-service.

## Posts and Stations

### Thornveil Headquarters and Settlement Defense

The order's command center and largest single post. Approximately one hundred fifty Greenshields are stationed at Thornveil at any given time, distributed across headquarters command and administrative cadre, settlement defense rotations, training cadre, and central reserve.

Thornveil settlement defense work is mostly low-tempo. Thornveil is a sovereign elven capital with no resident realm-military presence, no walls, and no gates; the city's defense is conducted through visibility, presence, and the Feywood's broader liminal protection. Greenshields on settlement-defense duty walk the city's paths in modest patrol patterns, attend scheduled posts at the Council chambers and the Heartwood Approach district's perimeter, and respond to incidents as they arise. Stationed Greenshields typically rotate through different roles on multi-year cycles to maintain breadth of experience.

### Vaelmere Outer-Rotation

The order's realm-facing operational center. Approximately one hundred Greenshields are stationed at Vaelmere, commanded by the Outer-Rotation Captain. The Vaelmere posting includes settlement defense for Vaelmere itself (a smaller commitment than Thornveil's), the realm-facing diplomatic-security work that supports trade and visitor flow, operational coordination with the Border garrison, and the specialist work of integrating the order's practice with Vaelmere's mixed-sovereign character.

Outer-rotation tours typically last several years to a decade. Greenshields who develop a particular fit for Vaelmere's work sometimes serve multiple consecutive tours or transition to permanent Vaelmere residency after their career; some become long-term outer-rotation specialists. The Vaelmere community recognizes its Greenshield contingent as part of the settlement's social fabric, not just as institutional presence.

### Border Garrison

Continuous presence at the realm-to-realm threshold itself. Approximately fifty Greenshields are assigned to the Border garrison at any given time, commanded by the Captain of the Border Garrison and operating in coordination with the Outer-Rotation Captain at Vaelmere.

Border-garrison work is specialized and demanding. The threshold is not a fixed line in the realm sense; it is a liminal geometry the Heartwood maintains and the order attends from the elven side. Border Greenshields develop the specific reading-skills the threshold work requires: who is approaching, with what intent, with what permission, with what awareness. They coordinate with the Hall of Guides for sanctioned visitor escort, with Vaelmere for trade-related crossings, and with the order's broader chain on anything that exceeds routine.

Border garrison rotations are typically shorter than other postings — two to four years — because the work's specific intensity rewards fresh attention and risks staling with extended exposure.

### Feywood Interior Patrols

Continuous patrol presence on the paths between settlements and through the Feywood interior. Approximately one hundred Greenshields serve in patrol units at any given time, organized into rotating patrol companies under the Patrol Captain. Companies typically operate in groups of four to eight, conducting circuits that range from days to weeks.

Patrol work is the order's most distinctively elven posting. Patrol Greenshields develop deep familiarity with the Feywood's geography and ecology, attend to the druidic signals the order is trained to read, and serve as the order's primary informational sensor for what is happening across the broader Feywood. They are also the order's most visible-yet-most-distributed presence — most Feywood residents who do not live in Thornveil or Vaelmere encounter the Greenshields primarily through patrol passage.

Patrol rotations are typically multi-year — five to fifteen years. Patrol Greenshields who develop deep regional specialization (Outer Wild patrol track, Heartwater Basin patrol track, Reading Glade approach track) become particularly valuable and often serve extended careers in patrol work.

### The Ranger Branch

See `rangers.md`. Ranger numbers are part of the order's overall ~600 active count.

### Smaller Settlement Presence

The Feywood maintains presence at several smaller settlements beyond Thornveil and Vaelmere. The order's posture at these locations is light — typically a small detachment of three to six Greenshields, often integrated with the local community in a way that blurs the line between order presence and resident neighbor. The Patrol Captain or the Senior Captain of Thornveil typically holds nominal command depending on the location's geography and operational context.

### Reserve and Training Depth

Beyond the actively-stationed Greenshields, the order maintains structural depth: recruits in initial training (~50–70), specialist trainees in advanced programs, and the reserve of veterans who have transitioned out but remain on call for crisis deployment. In a Discordant War-scale event, the order can mobilize from approximately 600 to perhaps 800 within days through veteran recall, and to a higher number through extended mobilization.

## Equipment

More variable than the Heartwardens', reflecting the greater diversity of the order's posts and roles.

The standard primary weapon is the bow. Most Greenshields carry a Longbough or comparable family bow in active service; the order does not standardize bow type, but training and skill maintenance are conducted in a unified bow-doctrine that produces consistent capability across the order regardless of individual weapon variation. Specialist roles may carry alternate primary weapons — Border garrison Greenshields often carry shorter bows suited to forest engagement at threshold range; some patrol specialists prefer longer-range bows for the open patrol routes.

Close-combat secondary weapons are individually chosen. Swords are common; spears and staves are present in specific roles; some patrol specialists carry shorter weapons better suited to forest movement.

**Shields are the order's distinctive equipment** and the source of the name. The order maintains a tradition of small-to-medium round shields used for settlement-defense work, ceremonial standing post, and certain formation-defense applications. Not every Greenshield carries a shield in every role — patrol work, Ranger work, and most border-garrison work do without — but the shield is closely associated with the order in elven cultural recognition. A Greenshield with shield in hand at a settlement-defense post is the canonical visual image the broader Feywood holds of the order.

Armor varies by role. Settlement-defense Greenshields wear medium-weight elven armor — layered cloth-and-leather with hard pieces at the shoulders and chest. Patrol Greenshields wear lighter kit suited to multi-day forest movement. Border-garrison Greenshields wear specialized threshold-appropriate armor that accommodates the specific defensive working they support. Ranger gear is detailed in `rangers.md`.

The order maintains some institutional regalia for ceremonial occasions — formal cloaks in the Greenshield's traditional muted-green coloration, ceremonial circlets for senior officers at major Council convocations, and similar items used only in specific contexts. The order does not wear distinctive uniform in daily work; Greenshields are recognized by context, weapon, shield (when present), and bearing.

## Eligibility and Recruitment

Open to elves of the Feywood who have reached at least young adulthood. Unlike the Heartwardens, the Greenshields routinely accept young-adult recruits — the order is the most common path of first service for elves coming of age, and the recruit cohort each year is typically dominated by young adults rather than older candidates.

The order looks for: physical capability appropriate to the work, mental composure under sustained pressure, willingness to follow the order's doctrine and chain of command, demonstrated relationship with the Feywood land sufficient to support the work, and *steadiness in the unremarkable* — the capacity to do useful work in conditions that will mostly never become exciting. Candidates who require constant excitement do not last in the order.

Recruitment is conducted through several channels: direct application at Thornveil headquarters, recommendation by serving Greenshields or their families, recruitment visits to the Feywood's smaller settlements during specific seasons, and the natural pipeline from coming-of-age communities where Greenshield service is a known and respected option. The order does not aggressively recruit; it accepts those who present themselves and meet the standards.

Non-elven eligibility is the same in principle as for the Heartwardens: open to any individual the order judges suitable. In practice, non-elven Greenshields are uncommon but not unprecedented. A small number of integrated Vaelmere residents have served across the centuries, primarily in Vaelmere outer-rotation roles where their existing community standing supports the work. Other non-elven candidates have served less commonly in patrol or Thornveil postings. The order's culture treats non-elven members as full Greenshields once sworn.

The order does not inherit posts in family lines. The fact that a candidate's parent or grandparent served does not affect the candidate's evaluation; the order's culture is explicitly anti-nepotistic on this point. Family service often passes through generations through the same mechanism that produces house-civic association more broadly — accumulated knowledge, ceremonial exposure from childhood, family-culture transmission — but the connection is cultural, not institutional.

## The Discordant War Deployment

The Greenshields contributed approximately one hundred eighty members to the Feywood's two-hundred-elf contingent during the Discordant War: ~150 general Greenshield personnel and ~30 Ranger-branch members operating in their respective specialties, alongside the ~20 Heartwardens whose deployment is detailed in `heartwardens.md`. The combined contingent under Lethariel Heartwood's command operated as one force in the field, with the Heartwarden contingent embedded in the larger Greenshield deployment but retaining separate identity and chain. Greenshield casualties during the deployment were approximately fifteen percent of the deployed force, and the order's recovery from those losses occupied its institutional attention for decades after the war's end.

The Discordant War deployment is canonically the only time the Greenshields have deployed under the Oath of the Fallen's mutual-aid clause. The order maintains the readiness for similar deployment as a continuous structural commitment.

## Cultural Character

Broadly accessible, broadly respected, broadly familiar. Most Feywood elves have at least one family member who has served, currently serves, or will eventually serve in the order. Greenshield service is woven into ordinary civic life in a way that the more set-apart Heartwarden order is not. A young elf considering coming-of-age options sees the Greenshields as a real and reasonable path; an elderly elf in a Thornveil neighborhood may have served fifty years in their middle centuries; a Vaelmere trader may be married to an active outer-rotation Greenshield. The order is *of* the community in a way that requires no special framing.

Internal culture is practical, plainspoken, and humorously self-aware. Settlement-defense rotations develop their own micro-cultures around specific posts. Patrol companies develop their own running jokes, traditions, and shared observational stories from years on the same routes. Border garrison Greenshields develop a particular stylistic dryness around the threshold work's strangeness. Vaelmere outer-rotation Greenshields develop a specific cosmopolitan ease that other postings sometimes find amusing.

Relationship with the Heartwardens is friendly but distinct — parallel and culturally separate. A career Greenshield is not a failed Heartwarden candidate; the orders are different vocations with different selection patterns and different cultural homes. Senior officers know one another personally and coordinate cleanly in the rare situations that require both, but the orders do not socially blend in their day-to-day life.

Relationship with the broader civilian Feywood is warm and functional. Greenshield presence at settlements is welcomed; Greenshield Sergeants and Lieutenants are often community figures known by name in the neighborhoods they serve.
