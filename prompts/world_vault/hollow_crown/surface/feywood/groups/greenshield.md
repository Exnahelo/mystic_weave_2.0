# The Greenshield

The Feywood's primary defensive military order, and the order most Feywood elves know personally. Where the Heartwarden guards the sacred center, the Greenshield holds the practical perimeter — the settlements, the patrol-paths, the border, the daily security work that keeps Feywood life livable. The order is large by elven standards (though small by realm reckoning), distributed across multiple posts, and structured to sustain continuous defensive presence indefinitely. Greenshield service is the most common path for Feywood elves who choose to serve in arms; the order is broadly accessible, broadly respected, and broadly woven into ordinary Feywood civic life. Most elves of the Feywood have at least one family member who has served, currently serves, or will eventually serve in the Greenshield. The order is not exotic. It is not sacred-set-apart. It is the Feywood's standing answer to the practical question of who keeps the land safe enough for everyone else's work to be possible.

## Doctrine and Purpose

The Greenshield exists to defend the Feywood. The framing is deliberately practical: not to project elven power, not to deter realm-side forces, not to maintain a martial culture for its own sake — to defend the land, the people, the settlements, and the relationships that constitute the Feywood as it is. This defensive framing is doctrinally central. The Greenshield does not campaign. It does not pursue threats beyond its operational range. It does not seek combat. When combat comes, the order meets it competently and decisively; when it does not, the order does its work in the absence of combat without seeking to manufacture it.

The order's doctrine breaks into four pillars:

**Settlement defense.** Continuous protection of Thornveil, Vaelmere, the smaller Feywood settlements, and the outlying noble estates. This is most of what the Greenshield does on most days — presence, patrol, response, the practical maintenance of safety in places where elves live their lives.

**Border watch.** Protection of the elven side of the realm-to-realm border, in coordination with the Feywood's liminal geometry and the Heartwood's broader defensive working. The Border is not a wall the Greenshield mans; it is a threshold the Greenshield attends. The order's border-facing presence reads what crosses, who crosses, what does not cross that should have, and what crosses that should not have. Most border traffic is sanctioned and routine; the work is in noticing what is not.

**Interior patrol.** Movement through the Feywood interior on regular patrol patterns, maintaining presence on the paths between settlements, attending to ecological signals the druids identify, keeping the order familiar with the land and the land familiar with the order. Patrol units rotate; the work is continuous; the Feywood interior is never unattended for long.

**Crisis deployment.** When defense at the settlements or the border is insufficient — when the threat has come deeper than perimeter work can hold, or when external events require the Feywood to deploy beyond its own borders under the Oath of the Fallen — the Greenshield is the bulk of the deployable force. Crisis deployments are rare; the Discordant War is the canonical instance. The order maintains the readiness for such deployment as a continuous structural commitment, distinct from the work it does most of the time.

The Greenshield's doctrinal stance toward realm-side forces is strictly cooperative and strictly bounded. The order does not coordinate operationally with the Dragon Guard, the Wardens, or any other realm body in routine work. Coordination occurs at the Council-to-Council level under the Oath of the Fallen and is escalated upward, not improvised in the field. The order does not maintain joint training, joint patrols, or joint structure with realm forces. The two realms defend separately and aid each other in true crisis, exactly as the Oath specifies.

## Structure

### The Commander

A single Commander leads the order. The Commander is based at the Thornveil headquarters and holds overall authority for Greenshield doctrine, deployment, posting decisions, training oversight, and coordination with the Council of Elders, the Heartwarden Captain, the Ranger branch leadership, and (through Council channels) realm-side military authorities.

The Commander is selected by the Council of Elders from among the order's most senior career officers, typically with input from the Heartwarden Captain and the senior Captains of the order itself. Commanders serve until they choose to step down, until the Council requests their replacement, or until they accept transition to veteran's standing. Tenures of one to three centuries are typical.

Members of House Thornmere have commonly held the Commandership across the order's history; the house's defensive-vinework tradition and Thornveil founding-mythic association produce a steady supply of senior officers from which the Council has frequently selected. The pattern is common but not exclusive — see `noble_families.md`. Any sworn Greenshield is eligible for the post; the current Commander's house identification can be instantiated when narrative requires.

### Captains

Senior officers commanding specific posts, units, or branches. The current Captain structure includes:

- **The Senior Captain of Thornveil** — commanding the Thornveil settlement defense, the headquarters operations, and the order's central training cadre.
- **The Outer-Rotation Captain** — commanding the Vaelmere outer-rotation post, the border-facing operations conducted from Vaelmere, and the realm-facing diplomatic-security interface.
- **The Captain of the Border Garrison** — commanding the continuous border-watch presence at the realm-to-realm threshold itself, distinct from the Vaelmere-based outer-rotation work.
- **The Patrol Captain** — commanding the rotating interior patrol units that maintain Feywood-internal presence between the named posts.
- **The Captain of the Ranger Branch** — commanding the order's specialist outer-range and threshold-watch arm; see the Ranger file for the branch's full structure.
- **The Captain of Training** — commanding the Thornveil-based initial training program for new members and the continuing-skill maintenance for the order at large.

Captains report to the Commander, coordinate with one another on cross-post matters, and exercise full operational authority within their posts. Captain tenures are typically half a century to a century and a half before rotation to other posts or to senior advisory roles.

### Senior Sergeants and Lieutenants

The order's working middle layer. Senior Sergeants are the experienced veterans who lead patrol units, command shifts at the various posts, and carry the practical knowledge that turns Captain-level direction into actual work on the ground. Lieutenants are commissioned officers below Captain rank, typically commanding sub-units or specific functional sections within a larger post.

The Greenshield draws no sharp line between Senior Sergeants and Lieutenants in cultural standing. Both are addressed with the same respect; both hold real authority in their domains; both are understood by the order as the layer where the order's effectiveness actually lives. Captains direct; Senior Sergeants and Lieutenants execute; the order works because the middle layer is competent.

### Sworn Greenshields

The body of the order. Approximately five hundred Greenshields hold sworn standing at any given time, distributed across the various posts and units. Sworn Greenshields hold equal standing among themselves; differentiation is by post, specialty, experience, and the particular work the individual is suited to. New entrants who have completed initial training join the sworn ranks at the equivalent of senior recruit; further advancement to Sergeant, Lieutenant, and Captain occurs through demonstrated capability and the natural progression of long service.

### Recruits

At any given time, the order has approximately fifty to seventy recruits in initial training at Thornveil. Recruits are not yet sworn; their five years of training precede the oath. Some will not complete training. Some will complete training and decline to swear. As with the Heartwarden, the recruit period is understood as the time in which both the candidate and the order test whether the relationship will hold; either party may end the path before the oath.

### The Ranger Branch

A specialist branch of the order, organizationally distinct but operationally part of the Greenshield. The Ranger branch handles outer-range fieldwork, threshold-watch duties, escort of sanctioned visitors through the Feywood's liminal geometry, and contact with realm-side forces in operational rather than diplomatic capacity. The branch maintains its own selection, training cadre, and unit structure under the Captain of the Ranger Branch. Rangers wear the Greenshield's identity but operate under their own specialized doctrine; details are in the Ranger file.

Ranger numbers are included in the order's overall ~600 active count.

## Service Terms

The Greenshield oath is taken at Thornveil headquarters at the close of initial training. The ceremony is more practical than the Heartwarden's — the candidate stands before the Commander or a designated Senior Captain, speaks the oath in Elven, receives their first formal assignment, and takes their place in the order. The witnesses are typically the candidate's training cadre, family members invited as guests, and the Captain of Training. The Council of Elders does not attend Greenshield oath-takings except when specific circumstances warrant.

The oath commits the candidate to **fifty years of initial service**. Fifty years is enough to develop genuine expertise, enough to serve in multiple posts and unit roles, and enough to carry the order's work into its actual depth — most Greenshields are still finding their full competence at year twenty and reach their senior-Sergeant standing somewhere between forty and sixty.

After fifty years, service continues at-pleasure. There is no formal renewal cycle. A Greenshield who wishes to continue simply continues; a Greenshield who wishes to step down submits notice through their chain of command and transitions out over a period appropriate to their post's continuity needs. Some leave at exactly the fifty-year mark, having earned their veteran's standing and chosen civilian life. Most serve longer. A century to several centuries of service is typical for career members. Some serve longer than that; the order's oldest currently-active Sergeants and Lieutenants have served seven hundred years or more.

Veteran's standing is a recognized status among Greenshields who have completed at least their initial fifty years. Veterans who have transitioned out of active service retain a formal connection to the order — they may be called upon for consultation, they retain access to Greenshield community functions, and in extreme crisis they may be recalled to service. The order's culture treats veterans as continuing members of a broader community even when they are not actively serving.

The order does not impose a cap on service length. A Greenshield who chooses to serve indefinitely may do so as long as their physical and mental capacity supports the work, with periodic informal review by the Commander and the relevant Captain to ensure the assignment remains a fit.

For non-elven members — uncommon but not unprecedented, see Eligibility — the fifty-year initial commitment is a meaningful fraction of most non-elven lifespans, and the order recognizes this in its post and assignment decisions. Non-elven members are not assigned to roles requiring continuous decades of static post-holding; they are placed where their service will be effective within their lifespan and where their eventual transition out will not destabilize a critical post.

## Training

### Initial Training

Five years at Thornveil headquarters with rotations through Vaelmere and the Border garrison. The training program is structured but not regimented in the realm military sense; the Greenshield's culture preserves elven thoughtfulness even within the practical work of preparing soldiers.

Year one is foundations: physical conditioning, basic combat training, weapons handling, the order's core doctrine, the geography and ecology of the Feywood, the order's command structure, the practical literacy a serving Greenshield requires. Recruits in year one do not stand any operational post; they train, they observe, they begin to integrate into the order's culture.

Year two is competence: deeper combat training, specialty introductions, the first practical assignments under direct senior supervision, beginning rotations through Thornveil's settlement defense work as junior support. Recruits in year two are visible at posts and may be encountered by visitors but are always under direct senior oversight.

Year three is application: operational training in real conditions, including extended Vaelmere rotations to expose recruits to the realm-facing work, border-garrison rotations to expose them to threshold conditions, and patrol unit rotations to develop the interior fieldwork skills. Recruits in year three are operational under senior command and contribute to the order's work, though not yet at full sworn-Greenshield capacity.

Year four is selection: specialist tracks divide here. Most recruits continue toward general sworn standing; those who show aptitude for Ranger work are identified and begin the Ranger branch's selection process; those who show aptitude for specific specialist roles (training-cadre development, command-track development, border-garrison specialization, etc.) begin those tracks. Year four is also the year in which recruits who will not complete training typically separate, having developed enough self-knowledge to make the call.

Year five is integration: the final operational year before swearing, with assignments that closely approximate the recruit's intended post-swearing role. Year-five recruits are functionally sworn-equivalent in capability and are treated by the order as nearly-full members; the swearing ceremony at year-five end formalizes what the recruit has already become.

### Continuing Service

Greenshields maintain their skills through continuous practical service. The order does not impose periodic refresher training in the manner of the Heartwarden's century-cycle renewal; the practical work of the order keeps the skills sharp. Specific advanced training programs exist for specialist roles — Ranger-specific instruction, command-track preparation, training-cadre development — and these are accessed as the member's career path requires.

Veterans rotating between posts often spend a transition period at Thornveil's training facilities, refreshing skills relevant to the new post and integrating with the new post's culture before reporting for assignment. This is not a formal training program; it is recognized practice that allows post transitions to occur smoothly.

### Specialist Selection

Beyond Ranger selection (handled in the Ranger file), the order maintains several specialist tracks:

- **Border-garrison specialization** — focused training for the threshold-watch work, including enhanced reading of liminal-geometry signals and specialized coordination with the Heartwood's defensive working.
- **Vaelmere outer-rotation** — focused training for the realm-facing diplomatic-security interface, including familiarity with realm trade culture, basic realm Common (most Vaelmere-based Greenshields develop this through service rather than formal training), and the specific protocols of mixed-sovereign operations.
- **Patrol leadership** — focused training for the interior-patrol Sergeant track, including the ecological reading and Feywood-geometry navigation skills that distinguish patrol work from settlement work.
- **Training cadre** — focused training for those who will themselves train new recruits, including pedagogical skills the order has developed across its history.
- **Command track** — extended preparation for officers being groomed for Lieutenant or Captain rank.

Selection into specialist tracks occurs through demonstrated aptitude during initial training (for tracks that branch from year four) or through career progression for tracks that develop in mid-service.

## Posts and Stations

### Thornveil Headquarters and Settlement Defense

The order's command center and largest single post. Approximately one hundred fifty Greenshields are stationed at Thornveil at any given time, distributed across the headquarters command and administrative cadre, the settlement defense rotations covering the city's various districts, the training cadre supporting recruits and continuing-skill instruction, and the central reserve held ready for crisis deployment.

The Thornveil settlement defense work is mostly low-tempo. Thornveil is a sovereign elven capital with no resident realm-military presence, no walls, and no gates; the city's defense is conducted through visibility, presence, and the Feywood's broader liminal protection. Greenshields on settlement-defense duty walk the city's paths in modest patrol patterns, attend to specific scheduled posts at the Council chambers and the Heartwood Approach district's perimeter, and respond to incidents as they arise.

Greenshields stationed at Thornveil typically rotate through different roles within the headquarters posting on multi-year cycles — settlement defense for some years, training cadre support for some years, administrative or command-staff work for some years — to maintain breadth of experience and prevent any one role from staling.

### Vaelmere Outer-Rotation

The order's realm-facing operational center. Approximately one hundred Greenshields are stationed at Vaelmere, commanded by the Outer-Rotation Captain. The Vaelmere posting is the order's most diverse, including settlement defense for Vaelmere itself (a smaller commitment than Thornveil's), the realm-facing diplomatic-security work that supports trade and visitor flow, the operational coordination with the Border garrison, and the specialist work of integrating the order's practice with Vaelmere's mixed-sovereign character.

Outer-rotation tours typically last several years to a decade. Greenshields who develop a particular fit for Vaelmere's work sometimes serve multiple consecutive tours or transition to permanent Vaelmere residency after their Greenshield career; some become long-term Outer-Rotation specialists who rotate to other posts only rarely. The Vaelmere community recognizes its Greenshield contingent as part of the settlement's social fabric, not just as institutional presence.

### Border Garrison

The continuous presence at the realm-to-realm threshold itself. Approximately fifty Greenshields are assigned to the Border garrison at any given time, commanded by the Captain of the Border Garrison and operating in coordination with the Outer-Rotation Captain at Vaelmere.

Border-garrison work is specialized and demanding. The threshold itself is not a fixed line in the realm sense; it is a liminal geometry that the Heartwood maintains and that the Greenshield attends from the elven side. Border Greenshields develop the specific reading-skills the threshold work requires: who is approaching, with what intent, with what permission, with what awareness. They coordinate with the Hall of Guides for sanctioned visitor escort, with Vaelmere for trade-related crossings, and with the order's broader chain on anything that exceeds routine.

Border garrison rotations are typically shorter than other postings — two to four years — because the work's specific intensity rewards fresh attention and risks staling with extended exposure.

### Feywood Interior Patrols

Continuous patrol presence on the paths between settlements and through the Feywood interior more broadly. Approximately one hundred Greenshields serve in patrol units at any given time, organized into rotating patrol companies under the Patrol Captain. Companies typically operate in groups of four to eight, conducting circuits that range from days to weeks depending on the route.

Patrol work is the order's most distinctively elven posting. Patrol Greenshields develop deep familiarity with the Feywood's geography and ecology, attend to the druidic signals the order is trained to read, and serve as the order's primary informational sensor for what is happening across the broader Feywood. They are also, in routine practice, the order's most visible-yet-most-distributed presence — most Feywood residents who do not live in Thornveil or Vaelmere encounter the Greenshield primarily through patrol passage.

Patrol rotations are typically multi-year postings, often five to fifteen years. Patrol Greenshields who develop deep regional specialization (the Outer Wild patrol track, the Heartwater Basin patrol track, the Reading Glade approach track, etc.) become particularly valuable to the order and often serve extended careers in patrol work.

### The Ranger Branch

See the Ranger file. Ranger numbers are part of the order's overall ~600 active count; Ranger doctrine, structure, training, and operations are detailed in the branch's own documentation.

### Smaller Settlement Presence

The Feywood maintains presence at several smaller settlements beyond Thornveil and Vaelmere. The Greenshield's posture at these locations is light — typically a small detachment of three to six Greenshields, often integrated with the local community in a way that blurs the line between order presence and resident neighbor. Specific assignments at these locations vary; the Patrol Captain or the Senior Captain of Thornveil typically holds nominal command depending on the location's geography and operational context.

### Reserve and Training Depth

Beyond the actively-stationed Greenshields, the order maintains structural depth: recruits in initial training (~50-70), specialist trainees in advanced programs at any given time, and the reserve of veterans who have transitioned out but remain on call for crisis deployment. The reserve component is not a separate force structure; it is a list maintained by the Commander's staff of veterans whose recall in crisis would be appropriate. In a Discordant War-scale event, the order can mobilize from approximately 600 to perhaps 800 within days through veteran recall, and to a higher number through extended mobilization if circumstances required.

## Equipment

The Greenshield's equipment is more variable than the Heartwarden's, reflecting the greater diversity of the order's posts and roles.

The standard primary weapon is the bow. Most Greenshields carry a Longbough or comparable family bow in active service; the order does not standardize bow type, but training and skill maintenance are conducted in a unified bow-doctrine that produces consistent capability across the order regardless of individual weapon variation. Specialist roles may carry alternate primary weapons — Border garrison Greenshields often carry shorter bows suited to forest engagement at threshold range; some patrol specialists prefer longer-range bows for the open patrol routes.

Close-combat secondary weapons are individually chosen, similarly to the Heartwarden. Swords are common; spears and staves are present in specific roles; some patrol specialists carry shorter weapons better suited to forest movement. The order's culture is to support individual weapon choice within the broader competence framework rather than to mandate uniformity.

**Shields are part of the Greenshield's distinctive equipment.** Specifically, the order maintains a tradition of small-to-medium round shields used for settlement-defense work, ceremonial standing post, and certain formation-defense applications. Not every Greenshield carries a shield in every role — patrol work, Ranger work, and most border-garrison work do without — but the shield is closely associated with the order in elven cultural recognition, and the order's name reflects this. A Greenshield with shield in hand at a settlement-defense post is the canonical visual image the broader Feywood holds of the order.

Armor varies by role. Settlement-defense Greenshields wear medium-weight elven armor — layered cloth-and-leather with hard pieces at the shoulders and chest, suitable for sustained standing post and formal-presence work. Patrol Greenshields wear lighter kit suited to multi-day forest movement. Border-garrison Greenshields wear specialized threshold-appropriate armor that accommodates the specific defensive working they support. Ranger gear is detailed in the Ranger file.

The order maintains some institutional regalia for ceremonial occasions — formal cloaks in the Greenshield's traditional muted-green coloration, ceremonial circlets for senior officers at major Council convocations, and similar items used only in specific contexts. The order does not wear distinctive uniform in daily work; Greenshields are recognized by context, weapon, shield (when present), and bearing.

## Eligibility and Recruitment

Eligibility is open to elves of the Feywood who have reached at least young adulthood. Unlike the Heartwarden, the Greenshield routinely accepts young-adult recruits — the order is the most common path of first service for elves coming of age, and the recruit cohort each year is typically dominated by young adults rather than older candidates.

The order looks for: physical capability appropriate to the work, mental composure under sustained pressure, willingness to follow the order's doctrine and chain of command, demonstrated relationship with the Feywood land sufficient to support the work, and the specific quality of *steadiness in the unremarkable* — the capacity to do useful work in conditions that will mostly never become exciting. Most Greenshield service is uneventful; recruits who require constant excitement do not last in the order.

Recruitment is conducted through several channels: direct application at Thornveil headquarters, recommendation by serving Greenshields or their families, recruitment visits to the Feywood's smaller settlements during specific seasons, and the natural pipeline from coming-of-age communities where Greenshield service is a known and respected option. The order does not aggressively recruit; it accepts those who present themselves and meet the standards.

Non-elven eligibility is the same in principle as the Heartwarden: open to any individual the order judges suitable, with the Heartwood-trust requirement applying through the same general framework. In practice, non-elven Greenshields are uncommon but not unprecedented. A small number of integrated Vaelmere residents have served across the centuries, primarily in Vaelmere outer-rotation roles where their existing community standing supports the work. Other non-elven candidates have served less commonly in patrol or Thornveil postings. The order's culture treats non-elven members as full Greenshields once sworn; the rarity of non-elven service is a function of the candidate pool, not of the order's reception.

The order does not inherit from previous Greenshield service in family lines. The fact that a candidate's parent or grandparent served does not affect the candidate's evaluation; the order's culture is explicitly anti-nepotistic on this point. In practice, family service does often pass through generations — the order is woven into Feywood civic life and many families have multiple members who have served. House Thornmere is the most prominent multi-generational presence (see `noble_families.md`). Other houses contribute their own steady streams. The pattern is sustained through cultural alignment and the order's accessibility rather than through any institutional preference.

## The Discordant War Deployment

The Greenshield contributed approximately one hundred eighty members to the Feywood's two-hundred-elf contingent during the Discordant War. The deployment included both general Greenshield personnel (~150) and Ranger-branch members (~30) operating in their respective specialties, alongside the ~20 Heartwardens whose deployment is detailed in the Heartwarden file.

The deployment composition included: settlement-defense veterans, patrol-unit Sergeants with crisis experience, Ranger specialists for threshold and outer-range work in unfamiliar territory, command staff for combined-force coordination, and supporting cadre. Approximately the same number of Greenshields were held in reserve at the home posts — Thornveil settlement defense at full strength, Vaelmere outer-rotation maintained at operational level, the border garrison reinforced rather than depleted, patrol rotations continued at reduced but sustained level. The deployment did not strip the Feywood's home defense; it represented approximately thirty percent of total active Greenshield strength, deployed for what was understood at the time as a defined-duration crisis.

The combined Feywood contingent under Lethariel's command operated as one force in the field, with the Heartwarden contingent embedded in the larger Greenshield deployment but retaining separate identity and chain. The deployment served alongside the Platinum Warden through the war's final days. Greenshield casualties during the deployment were significant in proportion — approximately fifteen percent of the deployed Greenshield force did not return — and the order's recovery from those losses occupied its institutional attention for decades after the war's end.

The Discordant War deployment is canonically the only time the Greenshield has deployed under the Oath of the Fallen's mutual-aid clause. The order maintains the readiness for similar deployment as a continuous structural commitment, but no equivalent crisis has occurred since.

## Cultural Character

The Greenshield's cultural character within the broader Feywood is one of broad accessibility, broad respect, and broad familiarity. Most Feywood elves have at least one family member who has served, currently serves, or will eventually serve in the order. Greenshield service is woven into ordinary civic life in a way that the more set-apart Heartwarden order is not. A young elf considering coming-of-age options sees the Greenshield as a real and reasonable path; an elderly elf sitting in a Thornveil neighborhood may have served fifty years in the Greenshield in their middle centuries; a Vaelmere trader may be married to an active outer-rotation Greenshield. The order is *of* the community in a way that requires no special framing.

The order's internal culture is practical, plainspoken, and humorously self-aware. Greenshields recognize that most of their work is uneventful and treat that fact with the elven equanimity that makes uneventful work bearable across centuries. Settlement-defense rotations develop their own micro-cultures around specific posts. Patrol companies develop their own running jokes, traditions, and shared observational stories from years on the same routes. Border garrison Greenshields develop a particular stylistic dryness around the threshold work's strangeness. Vaelmere outer-rotation Greenshields develop a specific cosmopolitan ease that other postings sometimes find amusing.

Relationship between the Greenshield and the Heartwarden is friendly but distinct. The two orders are parallel and culturally separate; Greenshields recognize the Heartwarden's specific work as set-apart and respect it appropriately, while not seeing themselves as a lesser version of the same thing. A career Greenshield is not a failed Heartwarden candidate; the orders are different vocations with different selection patterns and different cultural homes. The two orders' senior officers know one another personally and coordinate cleanly in the rare situations that require both, but the orders do not socially blend in their day-to-day life.

Relationship between the Greenshield and the broader civilian Feywood is warm and functional. Greenshield presence at settlements is welcomed; Greenshield Sergeants and Lieutenants are often community figures known by name in the neighborhoods they serve; Greenshield families are part of the civic fabric. The order does not hold itself apart from civilian life; it lives within it.

## Authoring Notes

The Greenshield is a Feywood-internal order, parallel to the Heartwarden and incorporating the Ranger as its specialist branch. The realm-side `prompts/groups.md` carries pointer entries for all three orders; this file is the canonical source for Greenshield doctrine, structure, and operations.

**Size is canonical at ~600 active members**, distributed roughly: 150 at Thornveil, 100 at Vaelmere, 50 at the Border garrison, 100 in interior patrol, ~150 in the Ranger branch (specifics in the Ranger file), with smaller settlement detachments and Thornveil training/admin cadre making up the balance. Plus 50-70 recruits in initial training. Plus the veteran reserve and emeritus-equivalent population. Total order presence including all categories: approximately 700-750 individuals at any time, with mobilization potential to ~800-900 in crisis.

**The 50-year initial commitment with at-pleasure continuation is canonical.** This is structurally less austere than the Heartwarden's century cycle, fitting the Greenshield's broader-accessibility character. Veterans transition to civilian life or veteran's retirement; some serve indefinitely.

**Five-year initial training at Thornveil with rotations through Vaelmere and Border posts is canonical.** Specialist selection (most importantly Ranger) happens at year four for those showing aptitude. The progressive structure (foundations, competence, application, selection, integration) is the canonical training arc.

**The order's posting structure is canonical**: Thornveil HQ + settlement defense, Vaelmere outer-rotation, Border garrison, Feywood interior patrols, smaller settlement detachments, Ranger branch. Specific named Captains for each post are Tier-3 generative roles.

**Shields are canonically associated with the Greenshield** and the order's name reflects this. Not every Greenshield carries a shield (patrol, Ranger, much border-garrison work does without), but the shield is the order's iconic image — a Greenshield with shield in hand at a settlement-defense post is the canonical mental picture the broader Feywood holds of the order.

**Bow remains the primary weapon** across the order, with role-specific variations. The order does not standardize weapon type beyond competence requirements.

**The Discordant War deployment is canonical**: ~180 Greenshield+Ranger members deployed alongside ~20 Heartwardens, ~30% of the Greenshield's then-active force, with home posts maintained at operational strength. Deployment casualties were ~15% of the deployed Greenshield force.

**Eligibility is open in principle to non-elven integrated residents**, more accessibly than the Heartwarden in practice. Non-elven Greenshields are uncommon but not unprecedented; small numbers of Vaelmere-integrated residents have served across the centuries. GPT scenes involving non-elven Greenshield characters are canonically possible.
