# Deephollow

An underground city in the Crystal Caverns beneath the central-west / south-central basin structure, with major access routes running upward toward the Stronghold and outward toward western and southern corridors. Deephollow is Drakenvale's primary crystal-export node - raw crystalline formations, refined psionic resonance crystals, and spell-amplification components used by both the Arcane Conservatory and the Infernal Forge Guild. The city glows from within: crystal veins in the cavern walls catch and amplify light, making darkness rare and shadows strange. The population is predominantly gnome and kobold, with significant dwarven engineering presence in deeper extraction zones. The SSTC maintains a substantial logistics operation here.
