# Crystal Caverns

A subterranean labyrinth beneath the valley floor, accessible through cave systems under the Stronghold, under the Temperate Forest, and at scattered surface points none of which are publicly known. Finding an entrance is a significant discovery. The caverns glow with embedded gemstones and magical minerals. Radiant crystals provide soft, shifting light. Luminescent pools dot the cavern floors. The walls amplify sound and magic in unexpected ways — spells behave differently here, their effects heightened for better and worse equally. Dreammoss grows in sheltered alcoves and induces visions in those who linger. This is Varethyn's domain. His Lair Sight trait operates through every gemstone here. Players who explore the caverns should assume they are being observed.
