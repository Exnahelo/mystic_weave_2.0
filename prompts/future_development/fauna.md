# Drakenvale Fauna (Future Development)

Biome-scoped fauna catalog. This document is in-progress worldbuilding,
not shipping data. It captures elevated regional fauna of Drakenvale
and will feed future bestiary/combat/encounter system work.

Not all entries will become companion-eligible. The companion catalog
at `data/beasts/creatures.json` ships a subset.
