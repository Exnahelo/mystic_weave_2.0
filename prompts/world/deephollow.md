description: An underground city built into the Crystal Caverns beneath Drakenvale's valley floor. Deephollow is the primary source of the valley's crystal exports — raw crystalline formations, refined psionic resonance crystals, and spell-amplification components that the Arcane Conservatory and Infernal Forge Guild both depend on. The city glows from within — crystal veins in the cavern walls catch and amplify any light source, making darkness rare and shadows strange. The population is predominantly gnome and kobold, with a significant dwarven engineering presence managing the deeper extraction operations. The psionic resonance of the caverns has a mild but measurable effect on long-term residents — most Deephollow natives are slightly more intuitive and slightly harder to read than surface dwellers. The SSTC has a substantial operation here managing crystal export logistics.
