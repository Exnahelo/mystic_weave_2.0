description: The final settled point before the Alpine Peaks proper. Lastmark is small, functional, and unromantic — a cluster of stone buildings built low against the wind, a stable, a supply depot, and an inn that stays warm through means that nobody asks about too carefully. Everyone who passes through Lastmark is either coming from somewhere important or going somewhere dangerous. The permanent population is small — guides, outfitters, a healer, and a handful of families who decided the edge of the world suited them. The Dragon Guard maintains a small rotating post here. It is the last place to resupply before the outer peaks and the first place travelers see when returning from them.
