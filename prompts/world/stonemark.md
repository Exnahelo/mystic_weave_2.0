description: A quarry settlement in the Alpine Peaks foothills supplying cut stone and raw mineral extraction to the Stronghold and surrounding construction projects. Stonemark is built from its own product — every structure is stone, low, and wind-resistant. The quarry operations go deep into the foothills, and the deeper cuts occasionally break into crystal vein territory that technically belongs to Deephollow's jurisdiction — a source of ongoing low-level dispute that the Council has never fully resolved. The population is predominantly dwarf and human, with the dwarven families holding most of the quarry master positions. Work here is physical, well-paid by valley standards, and carries the particular pride of people who build things that last.
