description: A farming settlement on the southern edge of the Draconic Grasslands where the soil is rich from centuries of draconic activity and the growing season is long. Ashfield produces grain, vegetables, and livestock at a scale that quietly underpins the Stronghold's food supply. The town is orderly, practical, and community-oriented in the way that agricultural settlements tend to be — everyone's survival depends on everyone else doing their part. The population is predominantly human and dragonborn, with strong ties to the Stronghold through the SSTC grain contracts that have been running for three generations. The Dragon Guard does training exercises in the surrounding fields periodically, which the farmers tolerate as long as the soldiers stay off the crops.
