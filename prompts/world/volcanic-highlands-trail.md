description: The primary overland route through the Volcanic Highlands connecting Zarkharath and Cinderpit to the broader valley road network. The trail follows the lower slopes where the heat is manageable and the basalt is stable enough to walk on. Sulfur vents are marked with cairns by Forge Guild workers. Travelers without highland experience are advised to hire a guide from Zarkharath or Cinderpit before attempting the upper sections.
