description: A dreamlike settlement at the lowest elevation point in Drakenvale, where fog collects against the southwestern crater wall and the boundary between the physical world and something else feels thinner than it should. Mirefall is built on elevated platforms and connected walkways above the wetland floor — ground-level construction is impractical and inadvisable. The population is mixed and unusual even by Drakenvale standards — diviners, herbalists, those who came seeking the Wetlands' restorative properties and never quite left, and a small but significant community of Mystic Wetlands scholars studying the valley's drainage and the magical properties of the water. Trade is in rare herbs, divination services, restorative compounds, and information. The SSTC maintains a small factor presence here for wetlands goods export.
