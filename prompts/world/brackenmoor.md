description: A rough edge settlement between the Temperate Forest and the Mystic Wetlands, built around hunting, trapping, and guide work. Brackenmoor is the kind of place where everyone has a skill the wilderness demands and nobody asks too carefully about anyone's past. Hunters here work the forest fringe and the wetland borders — the variety of terrain means the variety of quarry is significant, and Brackenmoor's trappers supply fur, leather, rare creature components, and wetlands specimens to markets in Dracélune, Mirefall, and the Arcane Conservatory. Guides out of Brackenmoor are considered among the most reliable in the valley for wetlands navigation. The settlement has no formal government — disputes are settled by whoever has the most social weight in the room at the time, which changes.
