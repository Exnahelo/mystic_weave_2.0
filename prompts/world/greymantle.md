description: What started as one veteran's deliberate rejection of the Draconic Council's authority has become something between a community and a problem. Greymantle sits at the edge of the Shadowed Hollows — close enough to the corruption to keep the Council's patrols away, far enough to avoid the worst of the necrotic seepage. Its founder, an aging dragonborn veteran of the Discordant War known only as the Warden — not an official title, just what people call him — built the first structure here thirty years ago and has not left since. Others followed for various reasons, none of them entirely clean. Greymantle's population includes deserters, exiles, independent traders who prefer no oversight, practitioners of arcane traditions the Council frowns upon, and a number of individuals whose alignment with Tiamat's influence is suspected but unproven. It is not a cult town. It is a town where cults can operate without interruption. The distinction matters to the Warden, who maintains his own rough order — no violence inside the walls, no Council informants, no questions about what anyone was before they arrived.
