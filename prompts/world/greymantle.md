description: What started as one veteran's deliberate rejection of the Draconic Council's authority has become something between a community and a problem. Greymantle is an edge-settlement between the southern dark quadrant and the outer basin routes — close enough to the Shadowed Hollows and Rift corridors to keep stricter civic order at distance, far enough to avoid the worst direct seepage. Its founder, an aging dragonborn veteran of the Discordant War known only as the Warden, built the first structure here thirty years ago and has not left since. Others followed for reasons rarely clean: deserters, exiles, independent traders, cult-adjacent actors, and people avoiding full civic oversight. It is not a cult town. It is a town where cults can hide among harder underworld traffic. The Warden enforces rough local order: no violence inside the walls, no open betrayal, and no easy questions about who anyone used to be.
