description: A chasm of unstable magical energy left by the Discordant War, when a rogue faction of chromatic dragons attempted to unseal the Temple to Tiamat. The Rift is not a dungeon — it is a wound. Chaotic energy crackles along its edges. Spells cast near the Rift behave unpredictably. The chasm is deep enough that the bottom is not visible. The proximity to the Shadowed Hollows means necrotic and chaotic energies interact and reinforce each other here. If the Temple's seal weakens, the Rift destabilizes first — it is an early warning system for those who know to watch it.
