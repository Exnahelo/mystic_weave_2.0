name: Scalemere

description: A river fishing town built where one of the Temperate Forest's major waterways widens before feeding into the Mystic Wetlands drainage system. Scalemere smells of fish, woodsmoke, and river mud and its residents would not have it any other way. The economy is fish — fresh, smoked, dried, and traded. Boat builders operate out of two yards on the eastern bank. The population is mixed but predominantly human and halfling, with a few elf families who have been here long enough to be considered locals. The SSTC has a small buying post here for preserved fish destined for the Stronghold and Zarkharath. Scalemere is safe, unremarkable, and quietly important — the valley would notice if it stopped producing.
