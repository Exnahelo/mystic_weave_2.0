type: city
description: The fey capital at the heart of the Feywood Glade — not a city in any sense the outside world would recognize. Thornveil has no walls, no straight streets, and no consistent geography between visits. It is a place that exists when it chooses to and presents what it wishes to those who enter. The ruling presence is not a monarch but a council of ancient dryads and fey elders who have occupied the Glade since before Drakenvale was settled. They are not hostile to dragonborn or mortals but they are not obligated to be helpful either. Trade here is entirely barter — coin means nothing. What the fey want varies and is rarely predictable. The elven communities at the Glade's edges serve as translators and intermediaries for those seeking to deal with Thornveil directly. Entering Thornveil without an elven guide or existing fey relationship is technically possible and inadvisable.
