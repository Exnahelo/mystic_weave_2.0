# Silver Scale Trading Company — Operations Network

Version 1.0 — April 2026  
Status: Supplemental operational reference (author/runtime aid), not a primary canon file.

---

## Operating Model

The SSTC runs a hub-and-spoke network with **Stronghold logistics arbitration** and specialized regional depots. Route reliability depends on season, threat posture, and Council restrictions, but these nodes are considered stable operating anchors.

Core institutional canon for SSTC, Amethyst Veil, and Sapphire Sentinels lives in `groups.md`.

---

## Core Connected Nodes

- **Stronghold of Drakenvale** — central contracts, manifests, dispute handling, caravan scheduling
- **Dracélune** — western trade interface, fey-adjacent exchange, courier redistribution
- **Zarkharath** — forge output export and heavy craft logistics
- **Deephollow** — crystal export and specialist arcane material handling
- **Lastmark** — alpine resupply chokepoint and guide contracting node
- **Ashfield Fields / Draconic Grasslands Edge** — observed southern overland transit into Stronghold-facing redistribution routes

---

## Resource Village Supply Chain (Primary)

The seven primary resource villages are active SSTC suppliers and should be treated as normal procurement points:

- **Ashfield** — grain/livestock bulk contracts
- **Silvercut** — silverwood timber and treated lumber
- **Dewhollow** — magical flora and prepared botanical components
- **Stonemark** — cut stone and quarry output
- **Cinderpit** — ore extraction feeding Zarkharath/Forge demand
- **Scalemere** — fish and river transport support
- **Brackenmoor** — hunting/trapping goods and wetland guide services

---

## Route Lanes (Narrative Defaults)

1. **Highland Industrial Lane**  
   `Cinderpit -> Zarkharath -> Stronghold`

2. **Cavern Materials Lane**  
   `Deephollow -> Stronghold -> Arcane/Forge demand sinks`

3. **Western Mixed-Goods Lane**  
   `Dracelune <-> Mirefall <-> Brackenmoor` (with links to core depot routing)

4. **Northern Stone/Alpine Lane**  
   `Stonemark <-> Lastmark <-> Stronghold`

5. **Forest-Wetland Provisioning Lane**  
   `Ashfield / Silvercut / Dewhollow / Scalemere -> Stronghold (and regional redistribution)`

6. **Southern Open-Corridor Lane**  
   `Ashfield Fields <-> Draconic Grasslands Edge <-> Stronghold`

## GM Runtime Notes

- In SSTC-connected settlements, baseline availability and pricing are generally more stable than isolated wilderness points.
- In dark-quadrant and restricted zones, treat SSTC movement as indirect, escorted, or deniable rather than routine.
- If a route is disrupted, reflect effects in shortages, delivery delays, and higher local price pressure before assuming total market failure.
