# Drakenvale — World Geography

Version 1.0 — April 2026
Status: Canonical. Revise if sessions contradict it.

---

## Formation

Drakenvale is a crater valley formed when a massive meteor destroyed a mountain of Everest-scale magnitude. The impact obliterated the peak and left a basin roughly 420km across at the interior floor. The surviving mountain mass forms the crater rim — the Alpine Peaks — irregular, jagged, and from the outside indistinguishable from the surrounding broken terrain. No visible pass, no river outlet, no signal that anything habitable exists inside.

Magical wards woven into the valley walls during construction reinforce this concealment. The valley is not invisible — it is simply unreasonable to approach without specific knowledge of the routes through the rock.

## Orientation

The valley organizes around two axes that are not aligned with each other.

**Theological axis (North–South):**
Platinum Heart sits at the northern end. Temple to Tiamat sits at the southern end, underground. These two divine sites are as far apart as the valley allows. The Stronghold anchors slightly north of center, closer to the Platinum Heart. The dark quadrant occupies the southern periphery.

**Geological axis (Northeast–Southwest):**
Volcanic Highlands occupy the northeastern quadrant against the crater wall near Zarkeros's lair. They descend through the valley toward the Mystic Wetlands at the southwest — the lowest drainage point against the opposite wall.

Because the axes do not align, no single quadrant concentrates all danger or all civilization. This distribution is why Drakenvale functions as a working society rather than a fortress under constant siege.

## Biome Positions

### Alpine Peaks — The Crater Rim (All Elevations, Outer Ring)

The crater walls encircle the entire valley. Not a destination — a boundary. Impassable without knowledge of specific routes. Glacial crystals embedded in the rock act as passive magical wards. Entry points are unmarked and known only to residents and trusted allies.

### Volcanic Highlands — Northeast

Inner slope of the crater wall, northeastern quadrant. Zarkeros's lair is here. Active volcanism, basalt formations, geothermal vents, magma rivers. The Infernal Forge Guild operates here. Transition to the Draconic Grasslands via rocky plains with heat-resistant grasses and ash ferns.

### Draconic Grasslands — Center

The open heart of the valley floor. The Stronghold of Drakenvale sits here at the original meteor impact site — the geographic and political center. Rolling meadows, dragon perch boulders, iridescent seasonal wildflowers. Dragons use this space for social gathering and ceremony including the Trial of Wings. Most exposed zone in the valley — nowhere to hide.

Borders: Volcanic Highlands (northeast), Sacred Pools / Platinum Heart (north), Temperate Forest (west), transitional terrain toward the dark quadrant (south).

### Sacred Pools / Platinum Heart — North-Central

Eryndor's domain. Sits between the Grasslands and the northern crater wall — as far from the Temple to Tiamat as the valley allows. Serene, protected, tranquility-radiating. Borders the Grasslands to the south and the Temperate Forest to the west via pristine connecting streams.

### Temperate Forest — West

The largest biome by surface area. Covers the western half of the valley floor from the Alpine Peaks foothills in the northwest down through center-west to the Mystic Wetlands transition in the southwest. Most non-dragon residents travel through or live near this biome. The Feywood Glade is a fixed pocket within it — physically located here but spatially sovereign.

Borders: Alpine Peaks foothills (north/northwest), Draconic Grasslands (east), Sacred Pools via streams (northeast), Mystic Wetlands via boggy transition (south).

### Feywood Glade — Within the Temperate Forest (Western Valley)

A fixed external location within the Temperate Forest. Its boundary is identifiable from outside. Its interior does not obey normal spatial logic — it exists simultaneously in this plane and the Feywild. Entry and exit points do not correspond. The Feywood can deposit a traveler anywhere within Drakenvale. It is constrained by the crater's magical field and cannot deposit anyone outside the valley walls. Elven communities live at the glade's edges as informal intermediaries.

### Mystic Wetlands — Southwest

The lowest elevation point in the valley. Water collects here against the southwestern crater wall. Magical drainage runs through underground channels beneath the Alpine Peaks rather than over them — this is why there is no visible river outlet from outside, and a primary reason the valley remains hidden. Dreamlike, fog-heavy, disorienting. The most liminal zone — closest to the outside world.

Borders: Temperate Forest (north, boggy transition), transitional terrain toward Shadowed Hollows (east), Alpine Peaks wall (south/west).

### Crystal Caverns — Underground, Beneath the Entire Valley

Subterranean network beneath the valley floor. Access points exist under the Stronghold, under the Temperate Forest, and at multiple other surface locations — none are publicly known. Discovering an access point is a significant event. Connected to Varethyn's lair region. Crystal resonance amplifies all magical effects — beneficial and harmful equally. Varethyn's Lair Sight trait operates through every gemstone here.

### Shadowed Hollows — South (Dark Quadrant)

Not a natural biome — a symptom. Centuries of necrotic seepage from the sealed Temple to Tiamat directly below have saturated the soil and warped the terrain. Surface zone in the southern valley, adjacent to the Mystic Wetlands on the west. No clean boundary — bleeds gradually into surrounding terrain as light dims and air thickens. Ungoverned in practice; monitored but not held.

The Sapphire Sentinels patrol the perimeter. Maximum in-zone time enforced at two hours for Wardens and Sentinels. Primary gathering zone for Tiamat's cult within Drakenvale.

### Rift of Discord — South (Dark Quadrant, Adjacent to Shadowed Hollows)

A chasm scar from the Discordant War. Not the meteor impact site. Not the Temple location. A wound where the worst fighting occurred during the war. Floating rock shards at various heights, rifts in the air that flicker between colors (each corresponding to a different damage type or magical effect), unpredictable magical weather, no sustained life. The Platinum Warden died here.

### Temple to Tiamat — Underground, Beneath the Shadowed Hollows

Sealed underground structure. The necrotic seepage creating the Shadowed Hollows originates here. Predates the Discordant War — it was sealed once before, and the rogue chromatic faction of the Discordant War attempted to unseal it. Heavily warded. Constant Warden and Sentinel vigilance. The state of the Hollows above is a direct read on the seal's integrity.

## Dracélune — Placement

Town on the edge of the Feywood, within the Temperate Forest biome, western valley. Position: center-west of the valley floor near the Feywood Glade boundary. Sits between the open Temperate Forest and the Feywood itself.

| Destination | Travel Time from Dracélune |

## GPT Notes

- The valley is not mapped for outsiders. Players may know biome names and general directions but not precise distances without local knowledge or a guide.
- The Feywood cannot be navigated by distance — only by relationship with the land.
- Crystal Caverns access points are not publicly known. Discovering one is a significant event, not a routine travel option.
- The Shadowed Hollows has no clean boundary. Entering it is gradual — not a threshold crossing.
- Valley weather reflects the emotional state of resident dragons. Narrative tool, not a mechanical system.
- The Feywood is constrained by the crater's magical field. It cannot deposit travelers outside the valley walls regardless of how deep inside the glade they travel.
