## The Dragon Guard

*Drakenvale's primary military and external defense force.*

**Commander:** [[Zarkeros the Inferno]]

**Composition:** Dragonborn warriors, kobold engineers, mortal mages, dragons of various alignments.

**Divisions:**

- **Aerial Division** — Dragons, wyvern riders, enchanted constructs. Reconnaissance and aerial combat.
- **Reserve Forces** — Volunteers from mortal and kobold populations. Logistical and combat support during crises.

**Responsibilities:** Border patrol and perimeter defense; repelling external threats (rogue dragons, treasure hunters, hostile factions); enforcing the Ptarian Code when disputes escalate to violence; coordination with the Sapphire Sentinels and Wardens during multi-front threats; ceremonial roles at the Festival of Wings and diplomatic events.

**Equipment:** Radiant Weapons (divine-energy blades and spears), Runic Armor (enchanted against physical and magical attacks), Construct Guardians (golem patrols), magical ward arrays along Drakenvale's borders.

**Symbol:** Ptarian Crest — a dragon's claw holding a radiant flame. Platinum-trimmed armor and banners.

**GM Notes:** The Dragon Guard and the Wardens have overlapping jurisdiction during crises. Zarkeros commands both in military situations, which creates tension with Eryndor over priority of sacred site protection vs. frontline response.

## The Wardens

*Elite internal security force and guardians of Drakenvale's sacred sites.*

**Commander (Peacetime):** [[Eryndor the Radiant]]
**Commander (Military/Crisis):** [[Zarkeros the Inferno]] (temporary)

**Composition:** Dragonborn paladins and clerics, kobold ward specialists, mortal investigators and mages, construct guardians.

**Responsibilities:**

- Guard the Platinum Heart, Amethyst Vault, and Sealed Temple to Tiamat
- Investigate magical anomalies and internal disturbances
- Neutralize internal threats — rogue dragons, corrupted individuals, alignment-driven conflicts
- First responders to ward breaches at sacred sites
- When mobilized by Zarkeros: precision strike force leveraging deep knowledge of Drakenvale's geography and magical infrastructure

**Training:** Defensive and close-quarters combat; non-lethal takedowns for internal disputes; ward maintenance and counterspelling; conflict resolution and investigation.

**Symbol:** Platinum-edged insignia shaped like an open flame. Armor inscribed with runes of protection and unity.

**GM Notes:** The Wardens are distinct from the Dragon Guard — they are not a division of it. Their dual-reporting structure is a latent source of institutional tension. A player who earns Warden trust gains access to information about internal threats that the Dragon Guard doesn't have visibility into.

## The Platinum Acolytes

*Religious order dedicated to Bahamut's ideals. Spiritual backbone of Drakenvale.*

**Leader:** High Acolyte — a wise dragonborn of deep spiritual insight (unnamed; assignable). Overseen broadly by [[Eryndor the Radiant]].

**Ranks:** Acolyte → Cleric/Paladin → Lorekeeper → Council Advisor

**Divisions:**

- **Clerics** — Rituals, spiritual counsel, healing and blessings
- **Paladins** — Sacred warriors; protect holy sites; enforce justice
- **Lorekeepers** — Custodians of religious and historical texts

**Responsibilities:**

- Daily prayers, sermons, personal counsel
- Guardianship of the Platinum Heart; Vigilance Rituals at the Sealed Temple to Tiamat
- Ceremonies of Atonement and Rites of Redemption for offenders
- Teaching the Ptarian Code to all residents regardless of species
- Vetting of external trade with the Silver Wing Envoys for ethical alignment

**Key Rituals:**

| Ritual | Occasion | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Morning Devotions | Daily | Invoke Bahamut's guidance |
| Rite of Balance | Equinoxes | Celebrate unity among alignments |
| Rite of Redemption | As needed | Atonement for offenders |
| Festival of Justice | Annual | Honor Bahamut's teachings; public trials and blessings |
| Vigilance Rituals | Regular | Reinforce wards on the Sealed Temple |

**Sacred Relics:** The Platinum Chalice (reveals truth during trials), the Radiant Shield (High Acolyte's ceremonial shield), the Atonement Blade (used in redemption rites).

**GM Notes:** Platinum Acolytes are the most accessible "neutral" faction for players. They will assist with healing, information about Drakenvale's history, and introductions to the Council — but they expect alignment with the Code in return. The Vigilance Rituals at the Temple are a standing hook: if a ritual is disrupted or fails, corruption can begin to seep out.

## The Infernal Forge Guild

*Master craftspeople and enchanters. Sustains Drakenvale's military, daily life, and external trade goods.*

**Leader:** Forge Warden — a senior dragonborn of exceptional craft and diplomatic skill (unnamed; assignable). Operates under Zarkeros's resource oversight.

**Divisions:**

- **Artisan Assembly** — Practical tools, ceremonial items, everyday enchanted goods
- **Armory Division** — Weapons, armor, and battlefield artifacts for the Dragon Guard and Wardens
- **Experimental Wing** — Prototype items, new enchanting techniques
- **Alchemical Forge** — Potions, salves, alchemical infusions
- **Resource Coordination Unit** — Mining and procurement of platinum, gemstones, and rare magical components

**Signature Creations:**

| Item | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Radiant Blades | Divine-energy swords/axes; effective vs. undead and chaotic creatures |
| Runic Shields | Deflect magic and physical attacks |
| Glyph-Inscribed Armor | Activates in combat; grants situational resistances |
| Radiant Crystal Amulets | Absorb a portion of incoming damage |
| Infernal Golems | Dragon-shaped constructs; channel elemental breath attacks |
| Platinum-Weave Fabric | Lightweight, durable; ceremonial robes and light armor |

**Challenges:** Mithral and adamantine scarcity; balancing experimental innovation against traditional methods; ensuring exported goods don't compromise Drakenvale's secrecy.

**GM Notes:** The Forge Guild is the source of most enchanted equipment players might encounter or acquire in Drakenvale. It also supplies the Dragon Guard and Wardens. A disruption to the Guild's supply chain (resource shortage, sabotage, internal conflict) has immediate downstream effects on Drakenvale's defense capacity.

## The Arcane Conservatory

*Hub of magical education, research, and knowledge preservation.*

**Oversight:** [[Varethyn of the Amethyst Gaze]] (guardian of arcane knowledge).

**Facilities:**

- **The Amethyst Vault** — Meditation, arcane study, conflict resolution; enchanted mirrors and still pools; hosts seminars on magical ethics and philosophy
- **The Library of Eternity** — Vast archive of scrolls, tomes, enchanted memory crystals; magically expanding shelves; sections restricted for dangerous or advanced lore

**Responsibilities:** Train residents in responsible magic use; advance magical theory; preserve draconic and sanctuary history; curate the Lore Vault; collaborate with the Infernal Forge Guild on applied magical techniques.

**Access:** Open to all residents. Restricted sections require Council approval. Mortals and kobolds have equal access to standard materials; advanced arcane training is merit-based.

**GM Notes:** The Conservatory is the knowledge hub. Players researching Drakenvale's history, the Temple to Tiamat, the Rift of Discord, or arcane threats will be directed here. Varethyn's oversight means information obtained here may also reach the Amethyst Veil.

## The Vigil

*The surviving operational remnant of the Platinum Accord, based at the Platinum Oath Monastery.*

**Leader:** Serevane (tiefling monk; long-serving Accord survivor)

**Base:** Platinum Oath Monastery (Rift perimeter)

**Public posture:** Independent monastery order cooperating with the Wardens on Rift monitoring.

**Internal reality:** The Vigil preserves Accord doctrine, watches Rift stability, protects the Platinum Warden's tomb complex, and trains select Warden candidates for high-risk perimeter operations.

**Responsibilities:**

- Continuous Rift-condition monitoring and anomaly escalation
- Support and hardening for Temple/Tiamat-adjacent vigilance operations
- Restricted tomb access governance and continuity rites tied to the Platinum Warden's legacy
- Advanced perimeter training for vetted Warden candidates

**Access policy:** Monastery grounds are controlled access. The tomb interior is restricted to Vigil members and specifically authorized Warden candidates.

**GM Notes:** Treat the Vigil as distinct from modern Wardens and Platinum Acolytes: cooperative but not subordinate in day-to-day doctrine. They are a living remnant of the Accord, not a museum of it.

## The Silver Wing Envoys

*Manages external relations and diplomacy while maintaining Drakenvale's secrecy.*

**Known function:** Dragonborn emissaries who represent Drakenvale to external allies. Operate under strict secrecy protocols — neutral meeting grounds, no disclosure of location. Manage alliance vetting and trade agreements alongside the SSTC.

**GM Notes:** Stub — detailed structure not yet authored. The Envoys are the public face of Drakenvale's diplomacy. The Sapphire Sentinels are their shadow counterpart.

## The Silver Scale Trading Company

*Drakenvale's primary external trade partner. Independent guild with deep Council ties.*

**Home base:** Drakenvale, with chapters across the Sword Coast and neighboring regions.

### Leadership: The Scaled Council**

| Member | Role | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Kaerys Emberclaw** | CEO | Silver dragonborn; sharp negotiator; ethical trade advocate |
| **Tazrik Flameweaver** | Logistics Head | Oversees transport routes and security |
| [[Eryndor the Radiant]] | Draconic Advisor | Diplomatic guidance; Ptarian Code alignment |
| [[Zarkeros the Inferno]] | Draconic Advisor | Resource coordination; logistical force |
| [[Varethyn of the Amethyst Gaze]] | Draconic Advisor | Code alignment; *covertly uses SSTC reach for The Amethyst Veil* |

**Operations:**

| Activity | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Enchanted goods trade | Artifacts, potions, components — many sourced from the Infernal Forge |
| Cultural exchange | Art, music, literature from Drakenvale; ideological soft power |
| Secure transport | Draconic-guarded caravans and airship routes |
| Rare resource sourcing | Platinum, gemstones, magical flora |
| Philanthropic projects | Schools, hospitals, temples in operating regions |
| Economic arbitration | Barter internally; adapts to currency externally |

**The Amethyst Veil:** Varethyn operates a covert intelligence network leveraging SSTC trade routes. Not publicly known. Discovery creates leverage — and significant personal risk.

**Reputation:**

- Adventurers — trusted supplier of enchanted goods and reliable transport
- Nobility — luxury goods, cultural patronage, prestige
- Rival merchants — feared; dominant in magical goods markets
- Critics — viewed as exclusionary; strict draconic standards can alienate non-dragonborn traders

**Conflict Hooks:** Rival guild sabotage (Zhentarim interference); internal ethical disputes over profitable-but-Code-violating opportunities; caravan attacks on high-value loads; political scrutiny from Waterdeep and Baldur's Gate.

**Route reference:** See `prompts/sstc_operations.md` for current connected nodes, lane defaults, and supply-chain assumptions.

## The Order of the Platinum Flame

*Bahamut's knightly order. Religious institution and external military force combined.*

**Mission:** Spread Bahamut's teachings; actively combat tyranny, corruption, and Tiamat's cults; protect sacred sites across Faerûn; embody the Ptarian Code beyond Drakenvale's borders.

**Structure:**

| Role | Description |
| --- | --- |
| High Seat of Justice | Leader; chosen through divine ritual invoking Bahamut |
| Council of Scales | Governing body; paladins, clerics, dragonborn elders |
| Initiate | New recruits; training phase |
| Platinum Knight | Full member; proven dedication |
| Flamebearer | Veteran; leads missions, mentors recruits |
| Dragonsworn | Elite; may carry divine blessings or sacred relics |

**Key Figures:**

| NPC | Role | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **High Warden Sereth Dawnscale** | High Seat of Justice | Gold dragonborn paladin; justice and mercy in balance |
| **Brother Altharion** | Trainer | Human cleric; wisdom and faith; trains initiates |
| **Lady Thariel Sunspear** | Platinum Knight | Half-elf; led campaigns against Tiamat's cults |

**Key Locations:**

| Location | Description |
| --- | --- |
| The Platinum Citadel | Mountain fortress; mithral and silver walls; primary HQ |
| Sanctuary of the Flame | Floating temple; accessible only to the faithful |
| Shrines across Faerûn | Regional outposts on the Sword Coast and beyond |

**Conflict Hooks:** Constant clashes with Tiamat's cult; mercy vs. justice dilemmas; internal ideological rifts over interpretation of the Code.
