# Drakenvale — Geography

Version 1.0 — April 2026  
Status: Canonical. This is the source of truth for the physical structure of Drakenvale and the Hollow Crown.

---

## Formation

The Hollow Crown is a hidden basin formed when a world-killing meteor struck a mountain of near-mythic, Everest-scale magnitude and shattered its summit. The impact hollowed the mountain’s interior, blasted ruin across the upper heights, and left behind a vast enclosed basin roughly 350 kilometers across. The surviving mountain mass forms the crater rim — the Alpine Peaks — a broken crown of jagged ridges, glacial walls, cloud-wreathed summits, and unstable stone shelves that, from the outside, reads only as an impossible mountain. There is no obvious pass, no visible river outlet, and no sign that a habitable realm lies within.

At the center of the basin, the meteor lodged rather than vanishing entirely. Over ages, its celestial matter fused with the platinum-veined heartrock of the mountain, forming a colossal pale mass of white stone streaked with silver-metallic veins. This fused impact mass is known as the **Heartmass**. From the Heartmass the Stronghold of Drakenvale was carved.

Within the Stronghold, in the Draconic Hall, rests the **Heartstone** — a radiant crystal uncovered during the earliest excavations of the Heartmass. In the sanctuary’s founding age, the dragons gathered their magic into it, binding power, memory, and purpose into a single living artifact. The Heartstone is the spiritual and political center of the realm.

The Stronghold rises above the city of Drakenvale, which spread across the lower terraces and surrounding lakefront at its base. From the upper sanctums and sacred channels of the Stronghold, waters descend in terraces, falls, and reflecting basins into the Sacred Pools and then into the great southern lake below. Around this center, civilization radiates outward into grassland, forest, wetland, caverns, highlands, and scarred dark regions.

Magical wards woven into the mountain and basin during the sanctuary’s founding reinforce the natural concealment of the Hollow Crown. The realm is not invisible; it is simply difficult to approach, difficult to understand, and nearly impossible to enter without knowledge of the safe routes through stone, wardline, and weather.

## Orientation

The Hollow Crown is organized around four interacting spatial logics.

**Central civic axis:**  
At the center of the basin stands the Heartmass, the fused celestial-platinum impact core. Carved from it is the Stronghold of Drakenvale. Around the Stronghold spreads the city of Drakenvale, then the surrounding Draconic Grasslands, then the wider basin regions.

**Theological axis (high center to deep south):**  
The Platinum Heart crowns the Stronghold’s central spire, elevated above the city and all other civic spaces. The Temple to Tiamat lies underground beneath the Shadowed Hollows in the southern dark quadrant. These are the realm’s highest sacred point and deepest forbidden point, set in deliberate opposition.

**Hydrological axis (center to southwest):**  
Sacred and civic waters descend from the Stronghold’s southern face into the great southern lake. From there, streams, distributaries, marsh channels, and subsurface drainage move toward the Mystic Wetlands in the southwest, the basin’s primary low catchment. Hidden underground outflow carries water through the mountain below the basin floor.

**Geological axis (northeast to southwest):**  
The Volcanic Highlands occupy the northeastern interior of the basin where impact fracture and deep geothermal rupture left the land permanently active. Their heat and mineral wealth contrast with the wet low basin of the southwest.

Because these axes do not fully align, no single sector contains all political power, all sacred authority, all industry, or all danger. This distribution is one of the reasons Drakenvale functions as a living realm rather than a fortress under constant collapse.

## Central Structure

### Heartmass — Basin Center

The Heartmass is the colossal fused central mass of celestial meteor-stone and platinum-veined mountain heartrock created by the impact. It is the basin’s dominant geological feature and the foundation of the Stronghold. Pale, immense, and visibly unlike the surrounding stone, it rises from the center of the Hollow Crown as both ruin and revelation.

### Stronghold of Drakenvale — Carved from the Heartmass

The Stronghold stands at the center of the Hollow Crown, carved directly from the Heartmass. It is the realm’s fortress, civic core, sacred axis, and symbolic center.

Contained within the Stronghold are:
- the **Draconic Hall**, seat of the Council and chamber of the Heartstone
- the **Platinum Heart**, the high sanctum of Bahamut and the Platinum Flame
- the **Administrative Quarter**, steward halls, civic records, and petition spaces
- the **Arcane Conservatory**, center of magical study and preservation
- the **Amethyst Vault**, at ground level in garden grounds beside the central spire
- the **Aeries**, open-air dragon perches and elevated private spaces

The city of Drakenvale spreads outward from the lower terraces of the Stronghold.

### Heartstone — Within the Draconic Hall

The Heartstone is the radiant crystal core housed in the Draconic Hall. It is not the whole central landform, but the awakened magical artifact discovered within it. It serves as a focus of governance, memory, and draconic unity.

### Drakenvale — City Around the Stronghold

Drakenvale is the central city built around and below the Stronghold. It occupies the lower stone terraces, plazas, roads, bridges, and lakefront approaches surrounding the fortress. The city is concentrated most heavily on the southern, lake-facing side of the Stronghold, with lighter spread around its other accessible edges.

### Southern Lake

A great lake lies south of the Stronghold at the base of the city. Sacred waters descending from the Stronghold’s upper levels and lower channels eventually feed this lake. It is the central freshwater anchor of the city’s southern face and the visual counterweight to the towering fortress above.

## Major Regions

### Alpine Peaks — The Crater Rim

The Alpine Peaks form the full outer ring of the Hollow Crown. They are the broken, glacial, near-impassable remains of the mountain’s surviving crown. Sheer cliff systems, icebound ridges, cloud belts, high passes, and glacial crystals define the terrain. Entry points exist, but they are hidden, unmarked, and only usable with local knowledge or guidance.

### Inner Ramparts — Broken Inner Escarpments

Between the Alpine Peaks and the lower basin regions lies a belt of broken inner escarpments, cliff shelves, stone ledges, hanging valleys, old impact fractures, and difficult descending routes. This region is known collectively as the **Inner Ramparts**. It is not one continuous wall, but a transitional highland zone running around much of the basin interior.

The Inner Ramparts contain:
- hidden watchpoints
- cliff roads
- old stone cuts
- aeries and dragon ledges
- cave mouths and access points into deeper systems
- waterfalls and basin-feeding streams
- scattered settlements in more stable sections

### Draconic Grasslands — Central Ring Around Drakenvale

The Draconic Grasslands form the open central meadows surrounding the city of Drakenvale and the Stronghold. This is the ceremonial and social heartland of the basin: highly visible, minimally forested, and charged with the latent magic of centuries of draconic presence. The grasslands extend outward from the city in all directions, but are most expansive to the south between the city, the southern lake, and the outer settled agricultural lands.

### Temperate Forest — Outer Basin Woodland

The Temperate Forest is the largest natural region by surface area within the basin. It surrounds much of the central grassland ring and spreads across the western, northwestern, and southwestern sectors of the Hollow Crown. Ancient silverwood groves, layered undergrowth, enchanted flora, hidden roads, and quiet settlements define it.

### Feywood Glade — Fixed Pocket Within the Temperate Forest

The Feywood Glade occupies a fixed physical location within the Temperate Forest, but its interior does not obey ordinary spatial logic. It is larger within than without, connected to the Feywild, and constrained by the Hollow Crown’s surrounding magical field. It may redirect travelers to different points within the valley, but it cannot deposit anyone beyond the mountain walls.

### Volcanic Highlands — Northeast

The Volcanic Highlands occupy the northeastern interior of the basin, where the impact fractured deep stone and left one region permanently geothermally active. Basalt formations, steam vents, lava channels, and fire-infused mineral strata dominate the terrain. Zarkeros’s domain, Zarkharath, Cinderpit, and the Infernal Forge all belong to this region.

This is not a ring around the basin. It is a single major highland sector.

### Mystic Wetlands — Southwest Low Basin

The Mystic Wetlands occupy the southwestern low basin where water gathers, slows, spreads, and disappears into subterranean outflow channels. Fog, marsh, floating vegetation, liminal pools, and disorienting magical phenomena define the region. It is the basin’s primary drainage region beyond the southern lake.

### Crystal Caverns — Underground Beneath Basin and Mountain

The Crystal Caverns run beneath the entire Hollow Crown, under the Stronghold, under the forests, under the mountains, and beneath the dark quadrant. They are a basin-wide subterranean system of mineral resonance, psionic amplification, luminescent pools, and dangerous beauty. Deephollow and the deeper cavern networks belong to this layer.

### Shadowed Hollows — South, Dark Quadrant

The Shadowed Hollows occupy the southern dark quadrant of the basin above the buried Temple to Tiamat. This is not a natural region but a corrupted one: necrotic seepage from the sealed temple has warped the forest, dimmed the air, blackened patches of soil, and made the region increasingly hostile to ordinary life.

### Rift of Discord — South, Adjacent to the Shadowed Hollows

The Rift of Discord is a separate wound near the Shadowed Hollows, created during the Discordant War rather than by the meteor impact. It is a chaotic magical scar of fractured ground, floating rock, unstable rifts in the air, and unpredictable magical weather.

### Temple to Tiamat — Underground, Beneath the Shadowed Hollows

The Temple to Tiamat lies beneath the Shadowed Hollows in the deep southern subsurface of the Hollow Crown. The corruption of the Hollows above originates here. The Temple is sealed, heavily warded, and treated as one of the most dangerous points in the realm.

## Environmental Character

### Weather

The weather of the Hollow Crown is partially shaped by enclosure and partially by draconic magic. Morning mists, trapped cloud layers, dramatic storms, and calm luminous nights are all common. In times of political or emotional unrest among the dragons, storms may intensify, winds may rise, and unusual weather patterns may spread across the basin.

### Water

Hydrology is central to the basin’s structure.

- Sacred waters descend from the Stronghold into the southern lake
- Streams and rivers branch outward into forest and lowland channels
- Water gathers in the Mystic Wetlands and disappears into hidden subterranean drainage
- No obvious surface river exits the Hollow Crown
- The basin does not flood because deep outflow carries water through the mountain below the basin floor

### Ecology

The Hollow Crown is not a flat bowl but a layered living world. Open central meadows, managed agricultural edges, old forest, wet lowlands, geothermal fracture zones, cave networks, sacred terraces, and corrupted southern ground all coexist within the same enclosed system. Its diversity is a product of catastrophic origin, long isolation, magical saturation, and deliberate draconic stewardship.

## Settlement Placement Summary

The Hollow Crown contains several major settlement clusters and support nodes:

- **Drakenvale / Stronghold** — central civic and sacred hub
- **Ashfield and southern agricultural lands** — southern grassland and field belt
- **Dracélune** — western basin threshold settlement at the edge of the Feywood
- **Zarkharath / Cinderpit** — northeastern volcanic-industrial zone
- **Mirefall / Brackenmoor** — southwestern low basin and wetland edge
- **Deephollow** — major subterranean settlement in the Crystal Caverns
- **Lastmark / Stonemark** — highland and approach settlements in more stable rampart sectors
- **Greymantle / Platinum Oath corridor** — edge-settlement and controlled approach near the southern dark quadrant

These nodes should be treated as the basin’s practical anchors for trade, travel, and narrative movement.

### Regional Read

- **North and northeast** are harsher, steeper, and more geothermally active.
- **West** is forest-heavy, liminal, and trade-facing through Dracélune.
- **Center** is dominated by the Heartmass, Stronghold, city, and Draconic Grasslands.
- **South** is shaped by the lake, agricultural belt, and wider basin descent.
- **Southwest** holds the wetlands and major drainage system.
- **Southeast / south-central east** holds the dark quadrant: the Hollows, the Temple below, and the Rift beside them.
- **Below all regions** run the Crystal Caverns, with Deephollow occupying one of the most important subterranean nodes.

### Route Logic

Major travel flows follow a few repeatable patterns:

- **Central routes** radiate from Drakenvale city and the Stronghold into the Grasslands, then outward into forest, highland, and southern roads.
- **Western routes** move through the Temperate Forest toward Dracélune, Feywood-adjacent traffic, and the wetland transition.
- **Northeastern routes** climb toward Zarkharath, Cinderpit, and the Infernal Forge by hardened volcanic approaches.
- **Southern routes** pass through agricultural lands around Ashfield and along the lake margins before splitting toward the wetlands or toward the dark quadrant.
- **Dark quadrant routes** are controlled, limited, or dangerous by default; Greymantle and the Platinum Oath corridor form the most stable edge-access pattern.
- **High approach routes** into the Hollow Crown pass through stable segments of the Inner Ramparts, especially near Lastmark and Stonemark.

## Geographic Summary

The Hollow Crown is a hidden interior world organized in rings, sectors, and layered depths. At its center stands the Heartmass, the fused celestial-platinum impact core from which the Stronghold of Drakenvale was carved. Within the Stronghold lies the Heartstone, the realm’s living crystal center. Around the Stronghold spreads the city of Drakenvale, then the Draconic Grasslands, then the larger natural and hazardous regions of forest, wetland, caverns, volcanic highland, corrupted southern quadrant, and enclosing alpine walls. Between the outer crown and lower basin runs the broken escarpment zone of the Inner Ramparts. The Temple to Tiamat remains beneath the Shadowed Hollows. The Rift of Discord remains nearby but separate. The wetlands drain the basin through hidden underground channels. The Feywood Glade is fixed in place but internally lawless. The Crystal Caverns run beneath all of it.

This is the canonical geographic structure of Drakenvale.
