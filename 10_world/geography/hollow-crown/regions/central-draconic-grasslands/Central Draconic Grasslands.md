# Central Draconic Grasslands

The open agricultural and patrol-traveled southern basin corridor of the [[Hollow Crown]] — the broad central zone running from the foot of the [[Stronghold of Drakenvale]] outward, linking the sanctuary's stronghold-facing settlements, highland approaches, and grassland routes. The region holds the realm's civic heart: the [[Stronghold of Drakenvale]] at its center, [[Drakenvale City]] curving along the northern shore of the [[Southern Lake]] at the Stronghold's southern face, and the wider grassland network reaching toward the agricultural settlements, the highland edge, and the eastern routes out toward the volcanic quadrant.

## Geographic Role

The Central Draconic Grasslands are the realm's basin floor — a long shallow corridor of fertile terrain bounded on the north by the rising approaches to the [[Alpine Peaks]], on the south by the [[Southern Lake]] and the agricultural belt, on the east by the [[Draconic Grasslands Edge]] and the routes leading to the [[Northeastern Volcanic Highlands]], and on the west by the western forests and the [[Feywood]] threshold. The basin's character is open, walkable, and well-patrolled. The [[Dragon Guard]] flies the corridor in routine rotation; [[Wardens]] handle the internal civic patrols.

This is the most travel-dense part of the sanctuary. Caravans, pilgrims, civic couriers, and the daily movement of Drakenvale City's working population all cross this region. The road network is well-maintained, the agricultural settlements are stable and prosperous, and the sight lines from the Stronghold's heights cover almost the entire corridor. Of all the Hollow Crown's terrain, this is the part the realm is most thoroughly aware of at any given moment.
