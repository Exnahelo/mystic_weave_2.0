# Drakenvale City

The realm's principal metropolitan center, built onto the southern face of the [[Stronghold of Drakenvale]] and curving along the north shore of the [[Southern Lake]]. The city rises from the lakefront in tiered stone districts, its upper terraces blending into the Stronghold's lower approaches and its lower districts spreading along the water in market streets, plazas, workshops, and residential quarters. It is species-mixed by default, civically ordered under the [[Codex of Remembrance]], and carries the daily texture of an active high-functioning capital — guilds working, markets trading, [[Wardens]] patrolling, [[Dragon Guard]] overhead, and the [[Sacred Pools]] cascading down the Stronghold's face as the city's defining visual landmark.

## Scene Texture

Drakenvale City occupies the shoreline crescent where the Stronghold meets the [[Southern Lake]]. The city is built *into* the landscape rather than on top of it — streets follow the natural contours of the basin's southern slope, buildings rise from the same pale [[Heartmass]] stone as the fortress above, and the lakefront curves in a long gentle arc punctuated by piers, promenades, and the broad stone plaza where the ceremonial path to the [[Sacred Pools]] begins its ascent.

A visitor arriving for the first time takes in the scale before the detail. The Stronghold rises behind and above the city in a single overwhelming presence, its carved towers and ramparts dominating the sky. The Sacred Pools cascade down its southern face in a continuous shimmer of water catching light on platinum-veined stone. The city spreads along the lake in concentric tiers, each tier set slightly higher than the one seaward of it, stone roads and worked staircases connecting them. Drakenvale does not sprawl. It rises.

The streets are busy but not chaotic. The Dragon Guard maintains patrols on the major thoroughfares and the lakefront; Wardens handle internal security and anomaly response in the less-visible parts of civic life. Crime exists, but open lawlessness is unusual — the city is too well-monitored and too civically invested for it to flourish publicly. What persists does so quietly, behind trade fronts and in the lower residential warrens near the lake's western edge.

The population is mixed by default. Drakari, humans, elves, kobolds, vulkars, dwarves, halflings, and halfbreeds of various combinations work alongside each other without the enclave patterns common to less integrated settlements. Species-specific districts do not exist in formal terms, though certain trades cluster along ethnic and cultural lines the way trades do everywhere. Kobolds tend to work the [[Infernal Forge]] supply chains and underground logistics. Vulkars disproportionately fill certain scholarly and hospitality roles. Elves are rare but present, typically long-residing. The mix is stable and has been for generations.

Markets occupy the middle tiers, centered on three great plazas: the Lakefront Market for fish, grain, and imported goods; the Guild Market for craft and service; and the Upper Market for finer goods, arcane supplies, and items commissioned through the [[Circle of Artisans]]. Each plaza has its own character. Lakefront is loud, crowded, and fast. Guild is rhythmic and professional, with journeymen moving between workshop and stall. Upper Market is quieter, more deliberate, frequented by the well-off and those with specific commissions.

The city carries music almost continuously. The [[Sapphire Choir]] maintains performance spaces throughout — formal halls, open-air stages in the plazas, small intimate venues in the residential districts. Street performers work the market crowds under Choir-sanctioned licenses. Festivals punctuate the calendar, and residents plan their years around them.

## Districts

Drakenvale City is not a single undifferentiated urban mass. It has internal structure:

- **The Stronghold Terraces** — the uppermost districts, where the city meets the fortress. Administrative and civic-adjacent residences, upscale guildhalls, and the formal ascending path to the [[Administrative Quarter]]. Higher rent, higher status, direct access to the Stronghold's services.
- **The Middle Tiers** — the city's primary market and workshop districts. Most residents live and work in this zone. The three great market plazas sit here. The Circle of Artisans' main civic hall is here. Guild activity is densest.
- **The Lakefront** — the shoreline districts, from the ceremonial plaza at the base of the Sacred Pools around to the western curve. Piers, fish markets, boat workshops, dockside inns, and the [[Silver Scale Trading Company]]'s main warehousing. The liveliest and most trade-focused part of the city.
- **The Lower Warrens** — the older, denser residential quarter along the western lakefront. Working residents, long-established families, and the city's more marginal populations. The warrens are not a slum — the Codex's civic discipline prevents that — but they are the part of the city where underworld activity persists most visibly. Wardens patrol here with patience rather than aggression.
- **The Promenade** — the ceremonial lakefront walk extending from the base of the Sacred Pools around the city's full arc. Public gardens, formal monuments, scheduled Sapphire Choir performance spaces, and the city's quiet pride. Residents walk here on festival evenings and rest days.

## Institutional Presence

Drakenvale City hosts the physical presence of multiple realm-wide institutions:

- **[[Silver Scale Trading Company]]** — the realm's principal external trade body, with lakefront warehousing and administrative offices. [[Kaerys Emberclaw]] is the senior authority here; [[Tazrik Flameweaver]] manages operational logistics.
- **[[Circle of Artisans]]** — the civic-cultural craft body responsible for infrastructure and commissioned work. Their main hall is in the middle tiers.
- **[[Sapphire Choir]]** — the cultural institution maintaining oral tradition, music, and ceremonial performance. Multiple performance spaces across the city; administrative headquarters in the upper tiers.
- **[[Dragon Guard]]** — visible patrol presence throughout. Local barracks in the Middle Tiers and Lakefront districts.
- **[[Wardens]]** — internal security presence, less visible but continuous. Warden stations exist in each major district.
- **[[Platinum Acolytes]]** — the Acolytes' civic presence operates from a small temple in the upper tiers, coordinating with the senior Acolytes at the Sacred Pools and [[Platinum Heart]].
- **Infernal Forge supply chains** — [[Zarkharath]]'s metalwork output moves through city warehouses before distribution to guilds and the Dragon Guard.

Two institutions are *not* in Drakenvale City: the [[Hall of Scales]] (its own settlement, overseen by [[Ardrynn the Measured]]) and the [[Arcane Conservatory]] (inside the Stronghold proper). Scholarly and arcane work happen elsewhere; the city is civic, commercial, and cultural.

## Anchor NPCs

- **[[Kaerys Emberclaw]]** — senior Silver Scale Trading Company leader. Operates from the Lakefront. Principal interface for trade, commerce, and external economic matters.
- **[[Lethira Vale]]** — Silver Wing–linked administrative and transport coordinator. Quieter operator, effective at routes, permissions, and threshold politics.
- **[[Lead Artisan of the Circle]]** — senior civic craft authority, Tier-3 generative role.
- **[[Director of the Sapphire Choir]]** — senior cultural authority, Tier-3 generative role.

[[Tazrik Flameweaver]], Kaerys's operational deputy, is present in the city but is more often on the road with caravans.

## Access

Drakenvale City is fully open. Residents and registered visitors move freely. External visitors arriving via trade caravan or escorted travel are processed at the Lakefront or the [[Administrative Quarter]] depending on their business. Unauthorized entry to the realm is theoretically impossible due to the ward-controlled access at [[Lastmark]]; once a visitor is in the realm, Drakenvale City is where they most likely end up first.

The city connects to the Stronghold (ascending to the Administrative Quarter via ceremonial approach), to the [[Sacred Pools]] (via the base plaza), to the [[Southern Lake]] (along the entire shoreline), to [[Ashfield]] (via the southeastern agricultural road), and to the [[Draconic Grasslands Edge]] (via the eastern civic road leading outward from the city).

## Authoring Notes

Drakenvale City is authored as a single parent settlement node. Its internal districts (Stronghold Terraces, Middle Tiers, Lakefront, Lower Warrens, Promenade) are described in scene texture but are NOT authored as independent sub-nodes. If a future storyline requires a specific district as its own navigable location (a specific warren, a specific market, a specific neighborhood), it can be authored at that time.

[[Kaerys Emberclaw]] and [[Lethira Vale]] are existing canonical NPCs from `npcs.md`. Their primary scene anchor is Drakenvale City. [[Tazrik Flameweaver]] is also city-anchored but mobile.

The Lead Artisan of the Circle and the Director of the Sapphire Choir are Tier-3 generative roles with the city as their base of operations.

The city's connection to [[Ashfield]] is the southeastern agricultural road. The connection to [[Draconic Grasslands Edge]] is the eastern civic road out of the city. The connection to [[Southern Lake]] is continuous along the northern lakefront. All in-map connections are two-way. The existing topology baseline has some of these as one-way directional hub/spoke links (e.g., Ashfield → Stronghold of Drakenvale); with the Drakenvale City node now authored as the civic destination, the preferred topology is two-way between Drakenvale City and each of these adjacent nodes. Prior one-way links that routed directly to the Stronghold may be revisited in a topology-pass at the end of the location authoring work.

The [[Hall of Scales]] is explicitly NOT in Drakenvale City. It is a separate settlement under [[Ardrynn the Measured]].

Underworld activity exists but is not openly depicted. If a specific criminal organization or underground storyline requires staging, it should be authored as a narrative layer within the Lower Warrens or the Lakefront's back-alley economy, not as a distinct location node.
