# Sacred Pools

A descending terrace of sacred waters cascading down the southern face of the [[Stronghold of Drakenvale]], beginning as springs that well up through the platinum-veined [[Heartmass]] at the [[Platinum Heart]] far above and ending in the [[Southern Lake]] at the city's edge. The pool complex is a series of shallow terraces, channels, and reflecting basins cut into the fortress's outer stone, arranged so the water moves continuously but quietly. The Pools are used for ritual reflection, healing rites, and the long sacred practice of the [[Platinum Acolytes]]; they are also, simply, one of the most beautiful places in the realm. Residents come here to think, to grieve, to forgive, and to be still.

## Scene Texture

The Pools are best approached from the city. A broad ceremonial path rises from the [[Drakenvale City]] waterfront along the Stronghold's southern face, climbing beside the falling water through a series of open terraces. Each terrace holds a shallow basin — some large enough for a dozen pilgrims to sit around the rim, others small and private, tucked into alcoves cut from the pale stone. The water flows from the terrace above into the one below in thin, controlled cascades, managed by channels that have been shaped and reshaped by the [[Platinum Acolytes|Acolytes]] over centuries.

The water is always cold. Always clear. Always moving. It originates at the [[Platinum Heart]], wells up through the [[Heartmass]], and emerges at the summit before descending through the fortress — some of it routed through interior channels (the reflecting pool in the [[Amethyst Vault]]'s gardens, the cooling trough in the [[Draconic Forge]]), the greater portion cascading openly down the Stronghold's southern face. Its character does not change between source and terminus. Water drawn from the lowest public basin is indistinguishable from water drawn at the [[Platinum Heart]] itself.

The visual effect is striking. The pale [[Heartmass]] stone is streaked darker where centuries of water flow have polished it. The platinum veins in the stone catch the light of the water from below, and the water reflects the platinum back. On clear days, the whole descending complex shimmers. At dusk, the pools take on a deep amber tone from the setting sun. At night, they are lit by the soft platinum glow that seems to rise from the stone itself — the same ambient light that marks any space in direct contact with the [[Heartmass]].

Pilgrims and residents come here in silence, mostly. The Acolytes do not mandate silence, but the place produces it. Conversation tends toward the quiet and the deliberate. Visitors sit on the stone rims of the basins, or walk slowly along the connecting paths, or kneel at the lowest public terrace where the water meets the city. Some bring offerings — small tokens, written prayers, inscribed stones — and place them at the dedicated offering niches built into the terrace walls. The Acolytes clear the niches daily without reading what is left.

## Functions

- **Ritual reflection.** The Pools are the primary public site for the realm's reflective religious practice. Pilgrims come for guidance, for grief, for atonement, for gratitude. The water is understood to carry a trace of the [[Platinum Flame]]'s sanctification, and visitors who sit by it long enough often report the same quality of clarity that the [[Amethyst Vault]] produces by a different method.
- **Healing rites.** The [[Platinum Acolytes]] conduct formal healing rites at the Pools. Physical healing — the water has genuine restorative properties for minor wounds and fevers, verified by centuries of practice — and spiritual healing for conditions the body cannot name. The rites are open to any resident of the sanctuary without charge. Foreign visitors require Acolyte authorization.
- **Formal observances.** Certain seasonal ceremonies and rites of passage are held at the Pools. Acolyte investitures happen at the lowest terrace. Oaths that do not require the [[Platinum Flame]] itself — minor covenants, guild dedications, formal apologies — are often sworn at one of the mid-level terraces.
- **Pilgrimage endpoint.** The Pools function as a pilgrimage destination. Residents from distant settlements travel here on foot for personal reasons. The journey itself is part of the practice. Pilgrims arriving from the outlying regions typically stay a day or two, performing private rites, before returning home.
- **Continuity with the Platinum Heart.** The Pools are the physical and ritual link between the high sanctum and the civic center. A person who cannot or will not climb to the [[Platinum Heart]] can commune with the same sacred water at the Pools. The Acolytes regard this as a deliberate design of the sanctuary — sacred contact made continuously available, not gated by altitude.

## The Acolytes at the Pools

The [[Platinum Acolytes]] maintain a continuous presence at the Pools. At any hour, at least one [[Attending Acolyte of the Pools|Attending Acolyte]] is present at the lowest public terrace, available for guidance, minor rites, or simple companionship for those who need it. Senior Acolytes conduct formal healing rites and investitures on scheduled days. The Attending Acolyte role is Tier-3 generative — specific names can be instantiated in play.

The Acolytes' work here is, by their own description, mostly listening. They do not preach. They do not interpret. They attend. The Acolytes who serve the Pools tend to be chosen for temperament rather than rank: patient, unhurried, willing to sit with grief or silence for long periods without needing to resolve it. The position is considered honorable and is rarely refused.

## Water Properties

The water's effects are subtle but documented:

- **Physical.** Minor wounds heal noticeably faster when bathed in Pool water. Fevers break. Fatigue eases. Chronic conditions do not resolve, but acute discomfort reliably abates. The water is not a substitute for medical treatment, but it is a genuine adjunct.
- **Reflective.** Visitors who spend extended time by the Pools often arrive at clarity on questions they did not come with an answer for. This is not divination — the water does not show the future — but many who come confused leave decided.
- **Sanctifying.** Objects immersed in the water take on a mild sacred resonance that lasts for days afterward. This is why the [[Draconic Forge]]'s cooling trough uses Pool water: the sacred resonance is absorbed into objects being quenched, contributing to the ritual character of the work.
- **Memorial.** The water "remembers" — the Acolytes' phrase — in ways that are not fully understood. Pilgrims who return after many years sometimes report the water's clarity responding to their presence with a recognition that the Acolytes do not attempt to explain.

## Access

The Pools are open to residents of the sanctuary at any hour. The ceremonial path from [[Drakenvale City]] is unguarded but monitored by the Acolytes. Foreign visitors are welcomed to the lowest terrace but require Acolyte authorization to ascend beyond it. The upper terraces near the [[Platinum Heart]] are reserved for senior Acolytes and those engaged in formal rites.

The connection upward to the [[Platinum Heart]] is continuous — a character may follow the water from the Pools up through the fortress to its source. This route is not forbidden, but it is culturally reserved for those with reason to climb. Most who make the ascent are Acolytes in training, pilgrims on formal practice, or residents whose need is great.

## Authoring Notes

The [[Attending Acolyte of the Pools]] is a Tier-3 generative role.

The Sacred Pools are a vertically distributed system. For navigation purposes, this node represents the publicly-accessible terraced complex on the Stronghold's southern face, visible from [[Drakenvale City]]. A character "at the Sacred Pools" is understood to be at this public pool complex. The upper source at the [[Platinum Heart]] and the interior channels ([[Amethyst Vault]] gardens, [[Draconic Forge]] cooling trough) are acknowledged as continuous but are not independently navigable — they are represented through their own nodes ([[Platinum Heart]], [[Amethyst Vault]]) or as narrative detail within other nodes (the Forge's trough).

The connection to [[Southern Lake]] is two-way and will reciprocate when the Southern Lake node is authored in a future batch.

The connection to [[Drakenvale City]] is two-way. A resident of the city ascending the ceremonial path reaches the Pools; a pilgrim descending reaches the city.

The water's documented effects are deliberately modest in mechanical terms. Healing is adjunct, not curative; reflection is clarifying, not prophetic; sanctification is mild, not powerful. This matches the Drakenvale design principle that sacred effects should feel earned and bounded rather than reliably exploitable.
