# Amethyst Vault

A ground-level sanctum set within the [[Stronghold of Drakenvale]]'s inner gardens, its pale stone walls veined with natural amethyst that catches light from the reflective pools and enchanted mirrors within. The Vault serves as [[Varethyn of the Amethyst Gaze|Varethyn]]'s public seat — a space for mediation, reflection, and the contemplative arbitration that the [[Draconic Hall]]'s weightier proceedings cannot accommodate. Disputes that would fracture in open council often resolve here, in silence, under the amethyst light. The surrounding gardens are deliberately ordinary — trees, benches, a long reflecting channel — and serve as a decompression zone between the Vault and the fortress proper.

## Scene Texture

The approach to the Vault passes through the Stronghold's inner gardens, one of the few areas in the fortress that genuinely feels like a softer place. The gardens are not ornate. They are considered — planted for stillness rather than display. Silver-barked trees line gravel paths. A long reflecting channel, fed from the [[Sacred Pools]] higher in the fortress, runs the length of the garden and ends at the Vault's outer steps. Benches stand at irregular intervals, positioned by sight-lines rather than by pattern. Most visitors pause here before entering. That is the garden's purpose.

The Vault itself is a low, broad structure of the same pale [[Heartmass]] stone as the rest of the Stronghold, but here the stone shows its amethyst veining openly. Long wandering lines of violet-purple crystal run through the walls, thicker than elsewhere, catching and refracting whatever light is present. Inside, the walls are polished; the amethyst lines appear to glow softly from within. The effect is not theatrical. It is patient.

The interior is a single circular chamber with a domed ceiling. Still pools occupy the four cardinal points of the floor — shallow, perfectly level, each filled with water from a different source the Vault's attendants keep quiet about. Between them stand enchanted mirrors in dark wooden frames, each one oriented so that its reflection catches one of the pools obliquely. The Vault is not a hall of mirrors. It is a room that asks you to look at things from several angles.

[[Varethyn of the Amethyst Gaze|Varethyn]], when present, occupies the center. Her scales are the same amethyst as the walls, and in the Vault's light she can be difficult to locate at a glance — the room itself seems to extend her. When she speaks, the acoustics are softer than in the [[Draconic Hall]]. The Vault carries voice without amplifying it.

## Functions

- **Mediation.** Disputes that require resolution without formal adjudication are handled here. Parties come expecting to be heard, to hear each other, and to reach an agreement neither party fully authored. Varethyn's presence is the mechanism.
- **Reflective consultation.** Council members, senior [[Wardens]], scholars, and ranking petitioners occasionally come to the Vault to think something through with Varethyn's guidance. These sessions are private. Records are not kept.
- **Truth-work.** The enchanted mirrors and pools are used for a specific class of divinatory reading — not future-sight, not clairvoyance, but clarification. A person who has been lying to themselves about a decision often leaves the Vault having stopped.
- **Private arcane instruction.** Varethyn teaches a small number of advanced students here, typically students whose work overlaps with moral or metaphysical questions that the [[Arcane Conservatory]] treats as outside scope. Acceptance is rare. Refusal is common.
- **Personal retreat.** The Vault is Varethyn's working home within the Stronghold. She is present more often than visitors realize; she prefers to be left to her thought unless approached with reason.

## The Mirrors and Pools

The four pools and the mirrors between them are the Vault's working instruments. They are not toys and they are not decorative. A visitor who attempts to use them without [[Varethyn of the Amethyst Gaze|Varethyn]]'s guidance will see nothing unusual — ordinary reflections, ordinary water. Under Varethyn's focus, the surfaces respond. What they show varies with what the viewer is prepared to see. The canon record of the Vault's workings is thin by design; the [[Platinum Acolytes|Acolytes]] who staff the outer gardens have learned not to ask for reports.

## Access

The gardens are public. Residents may walk the paths, rest on the benches, and sit beside the reflecting channel at any hour. The Vault interior is not public. Entry is by Varethyn's invitation or by scheduled mediation session arranged through the [[Administrative Quarter]]. The threshold is not guarded; it is respected. Those who attempt to enter uninvited usually find themselves suddenly reluctant to do so, an effect the Acolytes have not officially catalogued and Varethyn has not officially explained.

## Authoring Notes

The Amethyst Vault has a hidden second access point: a secret tunnel beneath the Vault connects to [[Varethyn's Lair]], which in turn opens into the [[Crystal Caverns]]. **This connection is NOT listed in the Vault's Connected Nodes.** It is discovery-gated and known to [[Varethyn of the Amethyst Gaze|Varethyn]] alone (and a small number of long-trusted figures). Narrative access to Varethyn's Lair from the Vault is possible only with Varethyn's consent or explicit discovery action.

If a player is granted access to the tunnel, the GPT should treat the tunnel as a one-time reveal moment and log Varethyn's Lair as discovered for that character's session state only. The canonical world state for Varethyn's Lair remains hidden.

[[Varethyn of the Amethyst Gaze|Varethyn]]'s presence is default-ON for this node. She is the anchor NPC. When narrating a Vault scene, she should be assumed present unless the scene specifically establishes otherwise.
