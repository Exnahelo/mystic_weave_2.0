# The Aeries

Open-air platforms enchanted with protective wards, cut into the [[Stronghold of Drakenvale]]'s upper reaches as landing and resting space for the realm's dragons. Resident Council dragons hold private alcoves here; visiting dragons alight on the broader public platforms. The views are unmatched — the entire [[Hollow Crown]] visible in every direction, from the [[Alpine Peaks]] to the [[Southern Lake]]. For a mortal visitor, the Aeries are humbling; for a dragon, they are the closest the Stronghold comes to feeling like home.

## Scene Texture

The Aeries occupy the uppermost reaches of the Stronghold, below only the [[Platinum Heart]] itself. Three broad platforms of pale [[Heartmass]] stone extend outward from the fortress like the open palms of the mountain, each sized to accommodate a landing ancient dragon with room to settle comfortably. Smaller alcoves line the inward walls — private spaces for resident dragons, each carved to suit its occupant and layered with personal touches that have accumulated over decades or centuries.

The wind is constant at this height. On clear days it is dry and thin; in storms it cuts hard enough to force mortal visitors to grip the stone. The wards keep the worst of the weather contained — rain and snow dissolve a few paces above the platform surface, and lightning refuses to strike here — but the wind itself is allowed to pass through. Dragons prefer it that way. A sheltered aerie is a cage, not a rest.

The views are the Aeries' defining feature. From the outer edge of the main platform, a visitor can see the whole of the [[Hollow Crown]] spread out below — [[Drakenvale City]] curving along the [[Southern Lake]]'s north shore, the [[Draconic Grasslands Edge|Draconic Grasslands]] rolling eastward toward the volcanic quadrant, the western forests fading into the [[Feywood]] threshold, the dark stain of the [[Shadowed Hollows]] to the south. On exceptional days, the [[Alpine Peaks]] appear to form a single unbroken wall of ice and stone around the entire realm. On most days, the clouds are closer than the ground.

The stone underfoot shows the long wear of dragons. Smooth channels mark the paths the resident Council members have walked countless times between their alcoves and the platform edges. The stone does not scar easily, but the weight of ancient dragons walking the same lines for a hundred years leaves its mark.

## Functions

- **Council landing.** [[Eryndor the Radiant|Eryndor]], [[Zarkeros the Inferno|Zarkeros]], and [[Varethyn of the Amethyst Gaze|Varethyn]] arrive and depart the Stronghold through the Aeries when flying rather than walking the interior corridors. Each has a designated alcove.
- **Visiting dragon reception.** Dragons traveling to Drakenvale — resident dragons from outlying regions, visitors from external draconic networks, dragons summoned for [[Conclave]] — land on the main platforms. A dragon's arrival at the Aeries is formally recorded by the [[Administrative Quarter]].
- **Resident dragon quarters.** A small number of resident Council-adjacent and institutional dragons keep private alcoves here. The arrangement is stable and unchanging; alcoves are assigned, not claimed.
- **Ceremonial gatherings.** Major [[Conclave]] events, Council-witnessed oaths involving dragons, and formal arrival processions for sanctioned guests occur on the main platforms.
- **Observation.** The views are practical as well as ceremonial. A Council dragon standing at the edge of the Aeries can read weather patterns across the basin, track unusual activity in any direction, and survey the realm as a single coherent whole.

## Access

The Aeries are accessible to dragons by flight. For mortal visitors, the climb is through the Stronghold's interior stair system — a long ascent comparable to the [[Platinum Heart]]'s approach, though less ceremonially ritualized. [[Dragon Guard]] presence is constant at the stair's upper entry. Mortal access requires cause: a messenger bearing word for a resident dragon, a petitioner granted Council audience at the Aeries rather than the [[Draconic Hall|Hall]], a scholar or [[Platinum Acolytes|Acolyte]] on specific errand.

Unauthorized mortal presence in the Aeries is not typically a violent matter. It is, however, exceedingly awkward. Visitors who wander up by mistake are escorted down with polite firmness, and the fact of their visit is noted. The alcoves of resident dragons are not entered by mortals at all, except by explicit invitation of the occupant.

## The Wards

The protective wards are old. They date to the Stronghold's founding and have been maintained continuously by the [[Platinum Acolytes]] and the [[Arcane Conservatory|Conservatory]] working together. The wards filter weather, deflect lightning, and provide a subtle aerial stability that helps very large dragons land on platforms that would otherwise require more runway than the Stronghold's geometry allows. The ward system is not obvious to observation. It reveals itself only in edge cases — the moment a heavy rain reaches the invisible boundary, the moment a lightning bolt curves away before it strikes.

The wards do not extend outward to cover open flight around the Stronghold. They are platform-specific. A dragon in the air above [[Drakenvale City]] is on her own.

## Authoring Notes

There is no default anchor NPC for this node — the Council dragons' primary narrative anchor points are elsewhere: the [[Draconic Hall]] for governance, the [[Amethyst Vault]] for [[Varethyn of the Amethyst Gaze|Varethyn]]'s mediation work, and their respective domains ([[Platinum Heart]] for [[Eryndor the Radiant|Eryndor]]'s faith life, the Stronghold more broadly for [[Zarkeros the Inferno|Zarkeros]]). A dragon encountered *at* the Aeries is a scene-framing decision rather than a persistent NPC presence.

Resident non-Council dragons (scholars, guardians, long-term visitors) may be introduced as Tier-3 generative roles during play. See `npcs.md` for the Resident Dragon Scholar and Resident Dragon Guardian roles.

The broader concept of scattered lairs for dragons in the [[Alpine Peaks]] — personal territories for recluse dragons, griffin nests, and similar — is intentionally NOT folded into this node. Those belong to future Alpine wilderness authoring if and when that region expands.
