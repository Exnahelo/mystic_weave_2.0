# Draconic Hall

The great vaulted council chamber at the ceremonial core of the [[Stronghold of Drakenvale]], sized for three ancient dragons to convene without crowding. The floor bears a luminous mosaic depicting the balance of [[Solveris]] and [[Mordrax]]; at the chamber's center stands the [[Heartstone]], the platinum-veined crystal artifact that serves as a fourth and silent voice on the Council. Here the Draconic Council deliberates, formal petitions are heard, treaties are ratified, and the realm's gravest legal matters are judged. Entry is controlled. What is decided here binds the sanctuary.

## Scene Texture

The scale is the first thing that strikes a visitor. The Hall was not built for people. Its proportions are draconic — the coffered ceiling rises to a height that makes human speech sound thin unless deliberately projected, and the pale stone walls recede into shadow beyond what lamplight can reach. A mortal petitioner stands at the mosaic's edge; the Council stands upon it.

The mosaic itself dominates the floor: a circular composition thirty paces across, [[Solveris]] in platinum and white on one side, [[Mordrax]] in obsidian and black on the other, the two figures mirrored rather than opposed. Enchanted inlay holds a soft internal light; the mosaic glows faintly at all hours without any visible source. Walking across it, visitors often find themselves slowing without meaning to.

The [[Heartstone]] rises from a low pedestal at the exact center of the mosaic. It is not large — no taller than a grown drakari — but it is impossible to ignore. Its platinum veins pulse in slow, irregular rhythms that track the emotional weight of whatever is being discussed in the Hall. When the Council agrees, the rhythm steadies. When the Council is in conflict, the crystal's light thickens and slows. Those attuned to it can sometimes hear, at the edge of perception, a sound that is not quite language and not quite music.

Sound in the Hall behaves strangely. Ordinary conversation carries clearly to every corner; raised voices do not. The room refuses to amplify anger. Veteran petitioners have learned to keep their tone level; those who come here shouting find themselves inaudible within a few words and have to start over.

## Functions

- **Council deliberation.** The three Council dragons — [[Eryndor the Radiant|Eryndor]], [[Zarkeros the Inferno|Zarkeros]], and [[Varethyn of the Amethyst Gaze|Varethyn]] — convene in the Hall for major governance matters. Minor decisions are handled through administrative channels; the Hall is reserved for the consequential.
- **Formal petitions.** Residents, institutions, and foreign emissaries may petition the Council here. Petitions are scheduled, vetted by the [[Administrative Quarter]], and heard in order. The Hall is not an open audience chamber.
- **Legal proceedings.** Cases requiring Council judgment are heard here. Testimony, evidence, and argument are received on the mosaic floor, with the Council seated above on wing-shaped ledges cut from the chamber's walls.
- **Treaty and covenant ratification.** External agreements are ratified in the Hall, with the [[Heartstone]] as witness. The realm's most binding oaths — formal alliances, accession to the [[Codex of Remembrance]], succession of Council authority — are sworn here.
- **Heartstone consultation.** Rarely, in moments of true deadlock, the Council consults the Heartstone directly. What exactly this exchange entails is not publicly known. The result is respected without question.

## The Heartstone

The [[Heartstone]] was discovered during the carving of the Stronghold itself — a single luminous crystal formation at the core of the platinum-veined [[Heartmass]]. The three founding dragons imbued it with their magic, establishing it as a focal artifact of draconic unity. It has served as a silent fourth member of the Council ever since. It does not speak in any ordinary sense. It *weighs in* — altering the emotional texture of the chamber, steadying or unsettling the deliberating dragons, and occasionally producing what the old records describe as "a clarity no one in the room brought with them."

The Heartstone is not removable. Attempts to move it, even ceremonially, have never been made. The Council has never seen reason to.

## Access

The Hall is reached from the Stronghold's central keep. Entry is controlled by the [[Dragon Guard]] at the outer doors and by the [[Administrative Quarter]] for scheduled petitioners. Unannounced entry is permitted to Council dragons, to senior [[Wardens]] in cases of security emergency, and to nobody else.

## Authoring Notes

The [[Heartstone]] is an object within the Draconic Hall, not a separate location. Any narrative requiring Heartstone interaction resolves within this node.

The [[Trial of Wings]] is ceremonially adjudicated from the Hall but held elsewhere (typically on a designated ceremonial ground within the Stronghold or adjacent to it) because the Hall itself is not sized for dragon combat, however ritualized. If a Trial-of-Wings location is needed for play, it should be authored as a separate node.
