# Draconic Forge

A small sacred forge beneath the [[Stronghold of Drakenvale]], fueled by the controlled breath of willing dragons rather than ordinary fire or volcanic heat. The Draconic Forge exists for ceremonial and symbolic work — [[Accord]] medallions, [[Wardens|Warden]] investiture tokens, [[Trial of Wings]] ceremonial blades, sanctified sacred instruments. It is not a production forge and was never meant to be. Its heat rises through the fortress above, contributing to the Stronghold's warmth during the colder seasons, and its presence beneath the keep is itself part of the realm's quiet ritual geography.

## Scene Texture

The Forge sits well below the main fortress levels, reached by a long descent of worn stone stairs from the Stronghold's service corridors. The passage is narrow by Stronghold standards — built to mortal proportion, not draconic — and the walls warm noticeably as one descends. By the final turn, the air is heavy, dry, and carries the clean metallic scent of heated stone. There is no smoke. The breath-fire that fuels the Forge burns cleaner than any wood or coal.

The Forge chamber itself is circular, domed, and smaller than a visitor expects. The floor is polished volcanic basalt — a gift from [[Zarkharath]], laid by the first forge-smiths. The walls are layered stone lined with dark ceramic, able to absorb and redistribute the intense heat that dragon breath produces. At the center stands the hearth: a broad circular pit of reinforced stone with a raised rim, open at the top, around which the workbenches and anvils are arrayed. The hearth is not lit continuously. It is lit only when a sanctioned dragon has been invited to breathe into it, and the resulting coals burn for hours or days afterward depending on the working.

Tools are few and beautiful. A half-dozen anvils in varying sizes. A working bench with a row of hammers in descending weight. A water trough fed from the [[Sacred Pools]] far above — cold spring water cooling a forge warmed by [[Solveris]]-aligned dragon breath, a small continuous paradox the smiths do not discuss. A low altar against one wall, where a sanctifying oil is kept and where each working begins and ends with a brief rite. There is no clutter. The Forge is spare in the way a temple is spare.

Heat rises through the stone above. On cold winter days in the Stronghold, the middle levels of the fortress feel faintly warmer than the physics of the walls should allow. That warmth originates here.

## Functions

- **Ceremonial item crafting.** The Forge produces the formal objects of sacred civic life — [[Accord]] medallions, [[Wardens|Warden]] platinum-scale tokens, sanctified sacred-rite instruments, ceremonial blades for the [[Trial of Wings]], and the like. Production is slow, deliberate, and always tied to a specific occasion or investiture.
- **Sanctification of existing works.** Objects crafted elsewhere (the [[Infernal Forge]] at [[Zarkharath]], master smiths in [[Drakenvale City|the city]]) are sometimes brought here for a final sanctifying working — a layer of draconic-breath-sealed enchantment that marks the object as sanctuary-consecrated rather than merely well-made.
- **Warmth infrastructure.** The heat rising from the Forge contributes to the Stronghold's thermal management during cold seasons. This was not the original purpose. It has become part of the Stronghold's design regardless.
- **Sacred craft instruction.** A small number of apprentices train here under the [[Master Smith of the Draconic Forge|Master Smith]]. Admission requires both demonstrated crafting skill and the Council's recognition of moral fitness. The Forge does not produce ordinary smiths. It produces sacred smiths, of whom the realm needs perhaps one or two new practitioners a generation.

## The Breath-Fire Arrangement

Dragons do not live in the Forge. The Forge does not have a resident flame. When a working is commissioned — by the Council, by the [[Platinum Acolytes|Acolytes]], by the Vigil at [[Platinum Oath Monastery]], or by any of the senior institutions entitled to request one — a dragon is asked to attend. The dragon descends to the Forge, breathes a controlled flame into the central hearth, and departs. The breath-fire remains as embers and residual heat for the duration of the working, often days. When a working requires fresh flame, another dragon is invited.

The Council dragons participate when the work is significant — [[Eryndor the Radiant|Eryndor]] most frequently, [[Zarkeros the Inferno|Zarkeros]] for work with martial resonance, [[Varethyn of the Amethyst Gaze|Varethyn]] rarely but not never. Resident non-Council dragons contribute for lesser workings. Visiting dragons attending [[Conclave]] sometimes request the honor of providing breath-fire for a specific piece, and the request is typically granted if the working is suitable.

No dragon's breath is considered interchangeable. The character of the fire shapes the object. A token forged in Eryndor's breath carries different qualities than one forged in Zarkeros's, and the [[Master Smith of the Draconic Forge|Master Smith]] chooses the dragon to match the work. This is not spoken of as magic, although it plainly is.

## The Master Smith

The [[Master Smith of the Draconic Forge]] oversees all working, trains apprentices, and serves as the realm's authority on sacred-craft matters. The role is Tier-3 generative. Current incumbents are typically drakari of advanced age and unusual moral discipline, and the role has been held continuously since the sanctuary's founding. Succession is deliberate; the Master Smith names a primary apprentice well before retirement, and the transfer is witnessed by at least one Council dragon.

## Access

The Forge is not open to the public. Access is by commissioned business (a sanctioned working in progress), apprentice standing, or specific invitation. The stair down from the service corridors is not guarded — the filter is cultural, not martial. Residents know better than to wander down. Unauthorized visitors would not be harmed, but they would be politely and firmly escorted back up the stair, and the Master Smith would hear of it.

## Authoring Notes

The [[Master Smith of the Draconic Forge]] is a Tier-3 generative role. Specific named incumbents can be introduced during play.

The Draconic Forge is explicitly distinct from the [[Infernal Forge]] at [[Zarkharath]]. The Infernal Forge is the realm's industrial production center — enchanted weapons, tools, armor, constructs for the [[Dragon Guard]], forge output supporting the whole economy. The Draconic Forge is a sacred annex, producing a handful of items per year, each tied to a specific ceremonial purpose.

The "different dragons produce different qualities" mechanic is deliberately soft-authored. It can be drawn on narratively for investiture scenes, artifact storylines, or questions about a specific historical item's provenance, without requiring hard mechanical rules. If a future arc needs concrete mechanical effects from dragon-breath provenance (a [[Zarkeros the Inferno|Zarkeros]]-forged blade carries a fire resonance, an [[Eryndor the Radiant|Eryndor]]-forged medallion carries divine warding, etc.), the framework is in place.

The water trough fed from the [[Sacred Pools]] creates a quiet continuity between the [[Platinum Heart]] (source) and the Draconic Forge (subterranean endpoint of one water channel). It is not a major narrative hook, but it reinforces that the Sacred Pools genuinely flow throughout the Stronghold.

Replacement note: the Draconic Forge replaces the prior `infernal-forge` reference that appeared in the Stronghold's Key Locations table in legacy world prose. The canon fix moves the sole [[Infernal Forge]] canon to [[Zarkharath]].
