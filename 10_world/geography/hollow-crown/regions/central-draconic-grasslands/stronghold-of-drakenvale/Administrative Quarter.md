# Administrative Quarter

The civic and bureaucratic core of the [[Stronghold of Drakenvale]], occupying the middle levels of the keep and handling the realm's steady flow of records, petitions, licenses, and sanctioned correspondence. Here stewards process the paperwork that keeps Drakenvale running — trade filings, guild registries, [[Wardens|Warden]] duty rotations, petition scheduling for Council audience, and the long civic memory of the sanctuary. It is the least dramatic part of the fortress and the most continuously occupied. Most residents who ever enter the Stronghold enter it here.

## Scene Texture

The Quarter is a sprawl of halls, offices, and archive chambers occupying the Stronghold's middle levels, reached by the main civic approach from [[Drakenvale City]]. Unlike the ceremonial spaces above, the Quarter feels lived-in. The stone is still pale [[Heartmass]], still veined with platinum, but it is scuffed in the high-traffic corridors, worn smooth at the thresholds, and warmed by centuries of lamp smoke and parchment dust. The acoustics are ordinary. People speak at ordinary volume. Footsteps echo like footsteps.

The main petition hall is the Quarter's public face — a broad, high-ceilinged chamber lined with scheduling desks along both long walls. Residents come here to file petitions, apply for licenses, register births and deaths, record trade agreements, submit written testimony, and queue for audiences with officials. The wait can be long. The stewards are efficient but the volume is steady and the procedures are exact. Benches line the central aisle for those waiting their turn.

Beyond the petition hall, the Quarter branches into specialized divisions:

- **The Records Halls** — deep archive chambers holding civic, legal, and economic documentation going back to the sanctuary's founding. Older materials are housed in reinforced inner vaults; working records are kept accessible to stewards. Access is stratified by clearance.
- **The Petition Vetting Offices** — where formal petitions destined for the [[Draconic Hall]] are reviewed, cleaned up, scheduled, and either advanced to the Council or redirected. Few petitions reach the Hall without passing through here first.
- **The Stewardship Offices** — daily operational management for the Stronghold itself and for civic coordination with the realm's settlements. Much of the work here is logistics: which caravans need escorts, which roads need repair, which Warden patrols are rotating.
- **The Treasury and Ledger Rooms** — economic records, tax and trade accounting, and the realm's internal barter-ledger system. [[Silver Scale Trading Company]] activity is cross-referenced here. Guild registrations are filed here.
- **The Correspondence Wing** — inbound and outbound sanctioned external correspondence, coordinating with the [[Silver Wing Envoys]] for diplomatic matters and with the [[Sapphire Sentinels]] for sensitive or covert material.

The Quarter's work is unending. Stewards arrive before dawn and leave after dusk. Lamps in the petition hall burn from first bell to last. The archivists in the Records Halls keep their own rhythms, sometimes disappearing into the deep chambers for days at a stretch. The place has the texture of a living institution — not exciting, but trusted, and ever-present.

## Functions

- **Petition intake and vetting.** Residents and institutions seeking Council audience file here. Petitions are reviewed, documented, categorized, and scheduled. The majority are resolved at the Quarter level — a steward's ruling, a cross-reference against existing precedent, a redirect to the appropriate guild or institution. Only matters requiring Council attention proceed upward.
- **Civic records.** Births, deaths, marriages, property transfers, trade registrations, guild memberships, and other formal civic documentation are maintained here. The records are complete going back to the early [[Age of Harmony]]; earlier fragments survive in the deep archives.
- **Stewardship coordination.** The Quarter coordinates Stronghold operations (maintenance, provisioning, staff scheduling) and civic coordination with the realm's settlements (courier routing, road network status, regional administrative liaison).
- **Legal documentation.** Contracts, oaths, formal agreements, and other binding instruments are registered here. Treaties ratified in the [[Draconic Hall]] are filed here afterward.
- **External correspondence.** Sanctioned contact with the outside world flows through the Correspondence Wing, which coordinates with the [[Silver Wing Envoys]] on diplomatic matters and handles routine cross-border communication ([[Silver Scale Trading Company]] contracts, [[Lastmark]] military reports, etc.).
- **Historical research.** The Records Halls are available to scholars with appropriate clearance. [[Hall of Scales]] faculty, [[Arcane Conservatory|Conservatory]] researchers, and [[Accord]]-adjacent investigators can access materials appropriate to their work.

## Records Access

The Records Halls are stratified:

- **Open records** — births, deaths, property, trade registrations, guild rolls, published proclamations. Available to any resident on request, with identification.
- **Restricted records** — ongoing investigations, sealed legal matters, certain trade documents, [[Wardens|Warden]] duty assignments, internal security documentation. Access by role or cleared request only.
- **Council-sealed records** — matters the Council has specifically sealed. Access by Council authorization only. A handful of documents in this tier date to the [[Discordant War]] and have never been reopened.

A visitor seeking information in the Records Halls typically works through a Records Steward, who retrieves materials on request rather than allowing open stack access. The stewards know the holdings with unreasonable precision. Speaking poorly to them is a mistake that has consequences well beyond the immediate visit.

## The Chief Steward

The [[Chief Steward of the Quarter]] oversees daily operations, mediates inter-division disputes, and serves as the Quarter's formal interface with the Council. The role is generative — specific incumbents may be named during play. The Chief Steward is typically a drakari or long-serving mortal, invariably politically careful, and one of the more quietly influential figures in the sanctuary. Petition scheduling alone gives the role real weight.

## Access

The Quarter is public. The main civic approach from [[Drakenvale City]] leads directly to the petition hall. [[Dragon Guard]] presence at the approach is routine but not restrictive — the Quarter is meant to be accessible. Internal division access varies by function: the petition hall and open records are walk-in, the stewardship and treasury offices require scheduled appointments, and the deeper Records Halls require clearance-appropriate steward escort.

Unauthorized entry to sealed records is not trivially possible — the deep archives are physically locked, warded, and staffed continuously. Attempts are rare and generally unsuccessful. They are also, uniformly, career-ending.

## Authoring Notes

The [[Chief Steward of the Quarter]] is a Tier-3 generative role. Specific named stewards may be introduced during play as needed; the role itself persists.

The Records Halls are a deliberate narrative hook for investigation and research arcs. A character trying to trace a family lineage, a disputed trade claim, a sealed legal matter, or the civic memory of an event (the [[Discordant War]], the founding, specific Council rulings) works through this node. Deep-archive access is a natural gate mechanism — requiring [[Ardrynn the Measured]]'s recommendation ([[Hall of Scales]]), an [[Platinum Acolytes|Acolyte]] sponsor, a [[Wardens|Warden]] justification, or Council authorization.

The Correspondence Wing's coordination with the [[Sapphire Sentinels]] is referenced but not elaborated. Sapphire Sentinel work is sensitive by nature; if a Sentinel-adjacent arc develops, the specific mechanism of coordination can be authored at that time.

The petition-scheduling function creates natural friction for players attempting to reach the Council. A player wanting Council audience works through this node first; the petition-vetting stewards decide whether the matter reaches the [[Draconic Hall|Hall]], and on what timeline. This is intentional gating — it keeps the Council from being casually accessible.
