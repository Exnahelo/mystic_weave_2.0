# Stronghold of Drakenvale

The central fortress of the [[Hollow Crown]], carved directly from the pale platinum-veined [[Heartmass]] at the basin's center. Towers, terraces, and wing-shaped buttresses rise from the fused meteor-and-mountain stone in a scale that only ancient dragons and centuries of sacred labor could have produced. Its lower terraces blend into the stone plazas and civic roads of [[Drakenvale City]] to the south; its upper reaches end in the open [[Aeries]] and the high sanctum of the [[Platinum Heart]]. Within its walls lie the [[Draconic Hall]], the [[Arcane Conservatory]], the [[Administrative Quarter]], and the [[Amethyst Vault]], with the [[Draconic Forge]] beneath and the [[Sacred Pools]] beginning in its heights. It is fortress, cathedral, seat of government, and symbol of the sanctuary's continuity all at once.

## Scene Texture

The Stronghold does not feel built. It feels *uncovered* — as though the [[Heartmass]] always contained this shape, and the founders merely removed what the stone was not. The walls show the subtle grain of meteoric fusion: pale stone threaded with veins of platinum that catch torchlight in long unbroken lines. Visitors often comment on the way sound behaves here. The great halls are quiet in a way larger spaces usually are not; the stone seems to hold sound rather than echo it.

The main public approach is from [[Drakenvale City]], up a broad ceremonial avenue of pale-cut stone that ascends the southern face in a series of switchback terraces. Civic life thickens and then thins as one climbs. Markets and residences give way to steward offices, then to guild halls, then to the formal gates of the fortress proper. The [[Dragon Guard]] is visible at every gate. The guard is not ceremonial — it is disciplined, attentive, and entirely comfortable with the fact that the place it defends has never been taken.

The Stronghold is too large to traverse quickly. The [[Draconic Hall]] alone is sized for three ancient dragons to convene in council without crowding; the scale propagates outward from there. Even the administrative corridors feel deliberate, high-ceilinged, and quietly formal. Most visitors see only the outer halls. Only those with standing, reason, or invitation proceed deeper.

## Internal Layout

The fortress operates on a rough vertical stratification, though the internal geography is not a simple stack:

- **Lower terraces and outer gates** — civic entry, visitor screening, outer guild functions, the public face of the [[Administrative Quarter]].
- **Central keep** — the [[Draconic Hall]] at its ceremonial core, with the ground-level gardens and the [[Amethyst Vault]] on the eastern side.
- **Middle reaches** — the [[Arcane Conservatory]]'s towers, the interior libraries, the private council chambers, and the deeper [[Administrative Quarter]].
- **Upper reaches** — the [[Aeries]], the high observation platforms, and the approach to the [[Platinum Heart]] at the summit.
- **Beneath** — the [[Draconic Forge]] and the sub-keep service levels; infrastructure for the whole fortress lives here, including the headwaters of the [[Sacred Pools]].

## Current Functions

- **Seat of Council.** The [[Draconic Hall]] is where [[Eryndor the Radiant|Eryndor]], [[Zarkeros the Inferno|Zarkeros]], and [[Varethyn of the Amethyst Gaze|Varethyn]] convene on matters that affect the whole sanctuary. The [[Heartstone]] mediates.
- **Sacred axis.** The [[Platinum Heart]], the [[Amethyst Vault]], the [[Sacred Pools]], and the [[Draconic Forge]] together form the ritual and spiritual core of the realm.
- **Administrative nerve.** The [[Administrative Quarter]] handles civic records, stewardship, petitions, and the formal interface between the Council and the realm's institutions.
- **Advanced learning.** The [[Arcane Conservatory]] operates from the middle reaches; the [[Hall of Scales]] is a separate institution in its own settlement elsewhere.
- **Defense and residence.** The [[Dragon Guard]] is headquartered here. Resident Council dragons keep private quarters within the upper reaches.
