# Platinum Heart

The high sanctum of [[Solveris]] at the summit of the [[Stronghold of Drakenvale]], an open circular platform of pale platinum-veined stone rising into the sky above the entire [[Hollow Crown]]. At its center burns the [[Platinum Flame]], an eternal silver-white light that inspires righteousness and marks the sacred axis of the realm. The [[Sacred Pools]] begin here as clear water welling up from the stone itself before descending through the fortress. From the Heart's edge, the whole valley is visible — city, grasslands, lake, distant forests, and the ring of [[Alpine Peaks]] beyond. It is where the [[Oath of the Fallen]] was first sworn and where the [[Vindrael|Platinum Warden]] led the [[Accord]] in its founding oath.

## Scene Texture

The approach is a long ascending spiral — a broad stone stair cut into the Stronghold's highest tiers, open to the air on one side and to the inner keep on the other. Pilgrims make the climb on foot; dragons land directly, alighting on the outer ring of the platform. The air thins as one climbs. Sound from the city below fades. By the final turn of the stair, the only things audible are wind, the climber's own breath, and — growing — the low continuous tone of the Flame.

The platform itself is unroofed. The sky above is the ceiling. The circle is ringed with twelve standing stones, each carved with a different facet of the [[Codex of Remembrance]]; between them the view opens in every direction. The [[Alpine Peaks]] form a jagged horizon on all sides, and the whole basin of the [[Hollow Crown]] lies visible below — the city, the lake, the grasslands, the forests in the west, the dark quadrant in the south. On clear days, one can see the far walls of the realm itself.

At the center, the [[Platinum Flame]] burns without fuel. It rises from a low circular basin of polished white stone, silver-white at its base, pale gold at its tip, casting light without heat. It does not flicker in the wind. Its color shifts subtly with the moral weight of those present — clearer in the presence of the honest, slightly muted in the presence of concealment or bad faith. Long-serving Acolytes have learned to read it. Most visitors do not realize it is reading them back.

The [[Sacred Pools]] begin immediately around the Flame's basin. Water rises up through the stone itself — no pump, no visible source — and fills a ring of shallow pools that overflow in controlled channels down the Stronghold's outer walls. The water is always cold, always clear, and always moving. Pilgrims who touch it have reported dreams for weeks afterward. The Acolytes do not forbid the practice; they simply remind visitors that the water remembers.

## Functions

- **High devotional site.** The Platinum Heart is the sanctuary's primary [[Solveris]]-aligned worship site. Daily rites, seasonal ceremonies, and major sacred observances are conducted here.
- **Oathgiving.** Formal oaths of consequence — [[Accord]] initiation, [[Wardens|Warden]] investiture, binding covenants before the Council — are sworn at the Flame. An oath sworn at the Platinum Heart is considered inviolable by civic and sacred law alike.
- **Pilgrimage.** The Heart is open to any resident of the sanctuary who wishes to ascend, though the climb itself acts as an informal filter. Foreign visitors require permission and escort.
- **Source of the Sacred Pools.** The water that flows through the fortress and descends into the [[Southern Lake]] originates here. The Pools are not ornamental; they are continuous from this source.
- **Historical anchor.** This is where the founding [[Oath of the Fallen]] was sworn, and where [[Vindrael]] — the Platinum Warden — led the first [[Accord]] in the original [[Oath of Scales]]. The platform carries that history in the same way a battlefield carries the memory of what happened on it.

## The Platinum Flame

The [[Platinum Flame]] is Drakenvale's defining sacred object. It has burned continuously since the founding. Its light is said to be [[Solveris]]'s own regard, held in the realm as long as the realm remains worthy of it. It does not consume fuel, cannot be extinguished by ordinary means, and has never — in recorded history — been reduced to embers. The [[Platinum Acolytes]] tend it, though "tending" is largely ceremonial; the Flame does not require care. What the Acolytes actually do is witness it.

The Flame responded visibly only twice in recorded memory: once at the moment of [[Vindrael]]'s sacrifice, when it briefly burned deep gold, and once during the signing of the amended [[Codex of Remembrance]] after the [[Discordant War]], when it dimmed for a full day. Both events are recorded and neither is formally interpreted. The Acolytes prefer the weight of the observation over the thinness of a conclusion.

## Access and Protocol

The Heart is not locked. No gate or guard bars the stair, beyond the ordinary [[Dragon Guard]] presence at the Stronghold's main approaches. The filter is cultural. Residents climb with respect and depart with composure. The [[High Acolyte of the Platinum Flame]] is present during ceremonial hours and greets or redirects visitors as appropriate. Disrespect is rare; those who attempt it tend to find the experience more uncomfortable than the social consequences explain.

Dragons visiting the Heart land on the outer ring. Drakari, kobolds, and humanoid residents ascend by stair. There is no status hierarchy in the approach — a farmer's climb and a Council dragon's arrival carry equal weight in the sanctum's sight.

## Authoring Notes

The connection to [[Sacred Pools]] is intentional and two-way. The Pools begin at the Platinum Heart and descend through the fortress; a character following the water from either end can move between these nodes directly.

[[Vindrael]] (the Platinum Warden) is referenced here by name because the Heart is the primary site of his founding oath. His tomb is at [[Platinum Oath Monastery]], not here. The Heart carries his ritual legacy; the Monastery carries his body.

The [[High Acolyte of the Platinum Flame]] is a Tier-3 generative role under the [[Platinum Acolytes]] order (see `groups.md`). Any specific named Acolyte introduced in play should be instantiated against this role, not replace it.
