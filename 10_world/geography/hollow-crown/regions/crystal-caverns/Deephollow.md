# Deephollow

The only true underground city in the realm, carved into a vast crystal-rich cavern deep beneath the [[Hollow Crown]]'s surface. Deephollow began as a mining camp when prospectors discovered a chamber veined with resonance crystals, deep metals, and subterranean magical materials; over centuries it grew into a stable underground settlement of thousands, organized around ongoing extraction and the specialized trade that moves its goods to the surface markets. The crystals catch and scatter the city's torchlight in slow shifting patterns across the walls and ceiling of the chamber, giving Deephollow a constant low luminescence that surface visitors find beautiful and unsettling in roughly equal measure.

## Scene Texture

The approach to Deephollow is a descent. The primary known route runs from a staging depot in the [[Central Draconic Grasslands|central grasslands]] down through a reinforced shaft network — widened and stabilized over centuries of use, with switchback lift platforms, ladder galleries, and long ramped tunnels that carry cart traffic. A traveler descending for the first time feels the temperature drop, feels the air grow heavier and moister, and sees the light change character as the cavern's ambient crystal-glow begins to compete with the torches and lanterns of the descent route. By the time the main cavern opens before them, they are a thousand feet below the surface, and the city is laid out across the cavern floor.

The cavern itself is enormous. Its ceiling rises hundreds of feet overhead in an irregular vault; its floor stretches outward in a roughly oval basin perhaps half a mile across at the longest measurement. Stalactites and crystal formations hang from the ceiling like a slow-motion storm, catching and refracting the city's lights. The walls are veined with crystals of varying color — amethyst-violets, smoky quartz, the pale rose of resonance-grade stones, and the deep blue of the rarer types — giving the whole chamber a mottled luminous character that varies with the time of day the city's lighting happens to be tuned to.

The city is built across the cavern floor in terraces. Lower levels closer to the primary mine entrances; upper levels rising toward the ceiling vault on stone platforms, bridges, and the carved faces of the cavern's own walls. Residences cluster in the upper terraces where the ambient temperature is more stable. Commercial and industrial work happens on the lower terraces. The cavern's acoustics carry sound differently than a surface city — voices echo across the open vault at unexpected distances, and the continuous rhythmic sound of distant mining work (chiseling, cart wheels on stone tracks, the low resonance-hum of crystal cutting) forms the city's constant background.

The population is heavily mixed but leans toward species with natural affinities for underground life. Dwarves form the largest demographic — Deephollow has grown into the realm's dwarvish cultural center, with several generations of families whose ancestors came for the mining and stayed for everything else. Kobolds are well-represented, particularly in mine crews and craft workshops. Gnomes appear in scholarly and technical roles. Humans, drakari, and other surface-dominant species are present but fewer, and they tend to be specialists, traders, administrators, or long-term residents who have adapted to the underground lifestyle.

Deephollow feels, to a first-time surface visitor, both familiar and alien. Familiar because it is a city — markets, workshops, taverns, residential streets, civic halls, the texture of daily life. Alien because the sky is stone, because the light is never truly bright, because directions here are measured in levels and wedges rather than cardinal directions, and because everything about the place is slower, heavier, and more deliberate than surface life.

## Districts and Layout

Deephollow is organized by vertical level and radial wedge:

- **The Floor** (lowest tier) — primary mining staging, ore carts, smelting and initial processing, the heavy industrial work. Loud, hot by underground standards, and constantly busy. The [[Deep Marshal]]'s operational offices sit at the Floor's civic center.
- **The Mid-Terraces** — commercial and craft districts. Markets, workshops, specialized craft guilds (lapidaries, gem cutters, resonance-crystal specialists, metalworkers working the local deep metals), the main taverns, traveler inns, and the Assay Office. This is where most daytime life happens.
- **The Upper Terraces** — residential quarters, civic halls, schools, temples ([[Solveris]]-aligned observance adapted for underground life), and the homes of the wealthier merchants and Guild officials.
- **The Vault** — the highest accessible parts of the cavern, used for the city's administrative structures, the [[Deep Marshal]]'s formal chambers, the [[Guild of Underdelvers]]' headquarters, and the few private residences that command views across the entire city.
- **The Outflows** — the working tunnels and mine adits branching outward from the main cavern. These extend for miles in multiple directions, varying from well-worked and safe to partially-abandoned and hazardous. Active mining happens here; the Outflows are not residential.

A separate deeper tier, the **Lower Tunnels**, extends below the city's main levels into largely unmapped territory. The Lower Tunnels are not part of Deephollow proper — they are the city's interface with the dangerous depths of the [[Crystal Caverns]] network. (See [[Deephollow Lower Tunnels]].)

## Functions

- **Mining and extraction.** Deephollow's primary economic activity. Resonance crystals (valued for spell amplification and arcane component work), deep metals (rare alloys and specialty ores not found in surface deposits), specialty minerals, and subterranean organic materials (cave-grown fungi, blindfish oils, rare phosphorescent algae) all move through the city's economy. Output is substantial and supplies institutions across the realm.
- **Specialized craft.** The city's lapidaries, gem cutters, and resonance-crystal specialists are the realm's best at their disciplines. Commissions from the [[Arcane Conservatory]], the [[Infernal Forge]] guild, the [[Wardens]], and the broader commercial economy all flow here. Some of the realm's most valuable craft work is done in Deephollow workshops.
- **Assay and certification.** The Assay Office is the realm-recognized authority on underground material authentication — identifying ore grades, validating resonance crystal quality tiers, certifying deep-metal alloy compositions. No Guild-recognized commission on underground materials is considered complete without Assay Office certification.
- **Subterranean logistics.** Deephollow serves as the staging hub for any movement through the [[Crystal Caverns]] network. Guides, route-knowledge, emergency rescue crews, and the infrastructure for supported exploration all originate here.
- **Underground cultural anchor.** Deephollow is the realm's primary center of dwarvish culture, kobold urban settlement, and the broader underground-adapted population. Festivals, traditions, and cultural institutions distinct from surface life are maintained here.

## Access and the Cavern Network

The primary descent route — the reinforced shaft network from the [[Central Draconic Grasslands|central grasslands]] staging depot — is the standard access path for most travelers. Other known access points exist throughout the basin: minor shafts from settlements adjacent to cavern outcrops, emergency routes, and the unmarked access tunnels used by the [[Crystal Caverns]]' Outflows that reach into surface locations at scattered points.

The broader [[Crystal Caverns]] network extends outward from Deephollow in all directions, mostly unmapped and increasingly dangerous as distance from the city increases. Deephollow's authorities maintain mapped zones in a roughly one-mile radius of the city where patrols and established routes keep conditions stable. Beyond that radius, exploration is at the explorer's own risk. The deep unmapped zones harbor hazards ranging from structural instability and toxic gas pockets to subterranean predators and the occasional genuinely strange phenomenon that no one has had the opportunity to fully investigate.

The Lower Tunnels — a particular descent path below the city's main levels — are their own threshold into even deeper and less-understood territory. They are a distinct location, not a district of Deephollow. (See [[Deephollow Lower Tunnels]].)

## The Deep Marshal

The [[Deep Marshal]] is Deephollow's senior civic authority — an elected position filled from the city's Guild leadership, serving fixed terms, combining the functions of mayor, senior administrator, and chief of the underground constabulary. The Deep Marshal handles:

- Civic governance and dispute resolution
- Mine operation licensing and coordination
- Emergency response for cavern incidents (collapses, flooding, lost-explorer rescues)
- The interface between Deephollow and the surface realm's institutions

The role is Tier-3 generative. Typically the Deep Marshal is a dwarvish senior guild figure, elected by confirmation of the major guilds, the residents' councils, and formal recognition from the [[Draconic Council]] on the surface. Tenure runs five years, renewable once.

The current Deep Marshal is not named here. Specific incumbents can be introduced during play.

## The Assay Master

The [[Assay Master]] oversees the Assay Office and is the realm-recognized authority on underground material authentication. Tier-3 generative role. Typically a senior gnome or dwarf specialist with decades of work in material identification. The Assay Master's certification is accepted across the realm's institutional buyers without question.
