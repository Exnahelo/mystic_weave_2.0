# Beneath the Southern Dark Quadrant

The subterranean region beneath the [[Shadowed Hollows]] in the southern dark quadrant of the [[Hollow Crown]] — the deep stone country directly below the realm's most corrupted surface zone, holding within its bedrock the [[Temple of Mordrax]] and the descent paths that lead down to its threshold. The region is not an inhabited zone. No settlements lie within it. Its singular significance is the Temple it contains, and the seal that has held there since the realm's founding.

## Geographic Role

This region is the underworld layer directly below the southern dark quadrant — the surface region containing the [[Shadowed Hollows]] and the historical site of the [[Dark Hold]]. The descent from the Hollows' deeper interior leads down through rock of increasing density and decreasing warmth, opening eventually onto the forechamber of the [[Temple of Mordrax]]. No other navigable destination exists within the region's authored canon.

The region is bounded above by the corrupted surface of the [[Shadowed Hollows]] and laterally by the natural rock barriers that have, since before the realm's founding, kept the Temple's siting effectively isolated from the rest of the underworld. The [[Crystal Caverns]] network extends widely beneath the rest of the [[Hollow Crown]] basin, but no canonical passage connects the Caverns to this region. The Temple's underworld is its own pocket.

## Constituent Locations

Authored:

- [[Temple of Mordrax]] — the sealed temple beneath the [[Shadowed Hollows]], inherited by Drakenvale's founders as a fact of the land they had chosen to settle, sealed as the [[Oath of the Fallen]]'s final working, and maintained continuously by the [[Vigil]] since the [[Discordant War]].

The region contains no other authored navigable locations. The descent path from the [[Shadowed Hollows]] through to the Temple's forechamber is implicit in the Temple node's Access section.

## Authoring Notes

This region root was synthesized for the user vault. The source vault does not contain a region-zone root file at this level — the `beneath_southern_dark_quadrant/` folder in source contains only `temple_of_mordrax.md`. Synthesis draws on the Temple of Mordrax's own scene texture and historical content, on `world.md` and `history.md` references to the southern dark quadrant and the [[Shadowed Hollows]], and on the structural symmetry with [[Crystal Caverns]] (the other authored underworld region under the [[Hollow Crown]]).

The region's singular significance is the [[Temple of Mordrax]]. This is intentional. There are no settlements, no other navigable locations, no ordinary travel routes through this underworld pocket. Its narrative weight is concentrated entirely on the Temple and its seal.

The connection to [[Shadowed Hollows]] is the canonical surface-to-underworld descent path. The reciprocal edge will be established when the surface dark-quadrant zone is authored as its own region; until then, this connection is a forward reference.

This region does NOT connect to [[Crystal Caverns]]. The two underworld regions are canonically distinct, separated by natural rock barriers that have isolated the Temple's siting since before the realm's founding. Any future storyline involving subterranean travel between the regions should treat that travel as a non-trivial undertaking requiring specific narrative justification.
